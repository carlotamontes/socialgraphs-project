Peter F. Bellinger (1924–2017) was an American entomology professor who specialized in Collembola.

 Career 
Bellinger was a forward observer in World War II with the United States Army, where he ranked corporal. After the war he went to Harvard University. He then transferred to Harvard, eventually obtaining a Harvard PHD in Collembology.

 Literature 
Bellinger described many species of springtails alongside University of California professor Peter F. Bellinger. This eventually accumulated to their final publication of C&B in 1998. He was also noted for helping review Frans Janssen's website Collembolla.org.

 Species described 
Some species described by Bellinger are listed below:

Ptenothrix delongi (Christiansen & Bellinger, 1981) 
Ptenothrix californica (Christiansen & Bellinger, 1981) 
Ptenothrix beta (Christiansen & Bellinger, 1981)
