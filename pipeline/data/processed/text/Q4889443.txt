Benjamin Michael Zuckerman (born August 16, 1943) is an American astrophysicist and environmentalist, and an emeritus professor in the Department of Physics & Astronomy at UCLA. His recent work focusses primarily on the formation and evolution of planetary systems around various types of stars. Zuckerman was a member of the board of directors of the Sierra Club from 2002 to 2005 and the Sea Shepherd Conservation Society from 2002 to 2013. He was an advocate for U.S. population stabilization, demonstrated by his co-founding of the internal Sierra Club group Sierrans for U.S. Population Stabilization and his Presidency of the non-profit organization Californians for Population Stabilization (CAPS) until its merger with NumbersUSA in 2025. Both groups argued for immigration and fertility levels that would enable the U.S. population to stabilize, thereby helping to protect the U.S. environment. 

 Education 
Zuckerman completed two degrees in 1963, one in Physics and one in Aeronautic & Astronautics at Massachusetts Institute of Technology. He finished his PhD thesis in Astronomy in 1968 at Harvard University.
