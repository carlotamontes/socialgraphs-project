Florian Święs (; born 24 September 1939) is a Polish biologist. He is a full professor of Biology and Earth Sciences at the Maria Curie-Skłodowska University. In 1992 he became the Head of the Geobiology Division at the Institute of Biology.

He received his MA from UMCS in 1962, his Ph.D. in 1968, and became a professor in 1992.
