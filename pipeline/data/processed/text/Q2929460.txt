Denis Henriquez (Oranjestad, October 10, 1945) is an Aruban writer.

He studied physics in Delft and worked as a teacher  at the Erasmiaans Gymnasium from Rotterdam.

Prizes
Prijsvraag van het Antilliaans Verhaal, 1990
