Vadim Veniaminovich Brazhkin (born October 24, 1961, in Zlatoust) is a Russian physicist, Academician of the Russian Academy of Sciences (since 2016), and Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences. He is Director of the Institute of High Pressure Physics of the Russian Academy of Sciences. He specializes in the physics of phase transitions and disordered media, high-pressure physics, and condensed matter physics. He has authored approximately 500 scientific publications and has been cited over 10,000 times. His h-index is 43 (WOS), 53 (Google Scholar), and 44 (Scopus).

 Early life and education 
Vadim Veniaminovich Brazhkin was born on October 24, 1961 in Zlatoust. In 1978, he entered the Faculty of Physics and Energy Problems at Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT). After graduating from the faculty, he entered graduate school at MIPT. In 1987, he defended his PhD dissertation.

Career
He began working at the Institute for High Pressure Physics, Russian Academy of Sciences (HPPI RAS), located in Troitsk, Moscow. In 1996 he defended his doctoral dissertation. Since 1993, he has held the position of Deputy Director of the Institute of High Pressure Physics of the Russian Academy of Sciences. In 2016, he became HPPI RAS's Director.
In 2011, he was elected as a Corresponding Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences.
In 2016, he was elected as an academician of the Russian Academy of Sciences.

Brazhkin discovered and studied a new phenomenon: phase transformations in melts of elementary substances and simple compounds under pressure. He established that the transformations are accompanied by sharp changes in the structure and properties of the liquid; in a number of substances, dielectric metal transitions are realized. Under the leadership of Brazhkin, a series of fundamental works on the study of sharp and blurred structural transformations in glasses and amorphous solids was also carried out. He discovered the first logarithmic kinetics of such transformations for a number of non-glass-forming molecular compounds () by quenching the melt at high pressures.

While studying the phenomenon of solid-phase amorphization of crystals under pressure, Brazhkin was the first to obtain bulk phases of amorphous tetrahedral semiconductors. He experimentally established that solid-phase amorphization is caused by a decrease in the shear moduli of the corresponding crystal lattices.

Brazhkin has completed a large series of studies on the effect of high pressure on the viscosity of liquids. It has been established that the viscosity of many liquid oxides and chalcogenides undergoing phase transformations drops sharply under compression by several orders of magnitude.
	Brazhkin showed that most molecular compounds based on light elements are thermodynamically metastable phases. At high pressures, these substances undergo irreversible transformations into polymerized states.
	Research into transformations in liquids and glasses, conducted by Brazhkin since 1986, has effectively led to the creation of a new direction in condensed matter physics. Brazhkin was co-chairman of the first major international meeting (2001) devoted to phase transformations in disordered media.

	In recent years, Brazhkin's research has shifted toward studying the structure and dynamics of supercritical fluids. Brazhkin and his colleagues have obtained significant results in theoretical and experimental studies of fluids. They have established that an analog of the liquid-gas line, in terms of the excitation spectrum in fluids, exists at any pressure. These results demonstrate the need for a significant revision of the "classical" view of the nature of the liquid state.

He is a member of the July 1st Club.

Awards and honours
 On February 5, 2024, he was awarded the Medal of the Order "For Merit to the Fatherland", 2nd class, for his great contribution to the development of Russian science, many years of fruitful work, and in connection with the 300th anniversary of the founding of the Russian Academy of Sciences.
