Larissa V. Bravina is a Russian computational particle physicist who works in Norway as a professor of physics at the University of Oslo. Her research applies computational methods to the study of the collisions of heavy ions at relativistic speeds.

Education and career
Bravina received a master's degree in physics in 1982 from Moscow State University, and continued there for a 1990 Ph.D.

After working as a staff scientist in the Institute for Nuclear Physics of Moscow State University beginning in 1982, she first came to Norway in 1992 as a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Bergen. In the late 1990s she left Moscow again for two visiting positions in Germany, as a Humboldt Fellow at Goethe University Frankfurt and then at the University of Tübingen. She moved to the University of Oslo in 2003.

Recognition
Bravina was elected to Academia Europaea in 2014.
