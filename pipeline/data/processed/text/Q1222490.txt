Dieter Meschede (born 17 April 1954) is a German physicist and lightweight rower.

Meschede was born in 1954 in Lathen in the Emsland district.

Meschede won a gold medal at the 1976 World Rowing Championships in Villach with the lightweight men's eight.

Meschede studied physics and in 1989, he was the inaugural recipient of the , awarded to young promising scientists. In 2007, he was awarded the . He teaches quantum technology at the University of Bonn. He was the president of the Deutsche Physikalische Gesellschaft and held this position from 2018 for a two-year period until 2020.
