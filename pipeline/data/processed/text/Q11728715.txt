João Manuel Peixoto Ferreira (; born 20 November 1978) is a Portuguese biologist and politician, serving as Member of the European Parliament for the Portuguese Communist Party (PCP); part of the European United Left–Nordic Green Left, where he was a vice-chair from 7 February 2012 to 30 June 2014, since 2009.

João Ferreira is a member of the Central Committee of the Portuguese Communist Party, and a member of the Lisbon Municipal Council.
