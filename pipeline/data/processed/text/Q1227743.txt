Dirk Ahrens (born 1971) is a German biologist and taxonomist at the Museum Koenig (Zoological Research Museum Alexander Koenig) in Bonn, Germany. He specializes in scarab beetles (Scarabaeidae) and became curator of the museum's Coleoptera section on 1 May 2010.

 Biography 

Ahrens studied biology at the University of Greifswald, specializing in zoology and ecology. He graduated with a diploma in 1997 and subsequently worked as a research associate at the State Museum of Zoology in Dresden.

From 2001 to 2004, he pursued doctoral research at the German Entomological Institute in Eberswalde, receiving his doctoral degree from the Free University of Berlin. In 2005, he worked for Helvetas, a Swiss development cooperation organization. From 2006 to 2008, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Natural History Museum in Munich. In 2010, he joined the Zoological Research Museum Alexander Koenig in Bonn.

His research interests include the molecular systematics of beetles, evolutionary biology, and integrative taxonomy. According to the museum's 2010 annual report, his scientific publications had described nearly 500 new species.
