Fabio Garbari (born 1937) is an Italian botanist who has a degree in Biological Science from the University of Pisa awarded in 1958. From 1963 to 2007 he was a faculty member in the Department of Botanical Science () of the University of Pisa. In 2019 he was awarded the Veitch Memorial Medal by the Royal Horticultural Society. The International Plant Names Index lists 37 taxa named by Garbari.
