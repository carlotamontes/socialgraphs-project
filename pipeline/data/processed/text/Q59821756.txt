Nathalie Brigitte Anne Picqué (born December 2, 1973) is a French physicist. Picqué is the Director of the Precision Physics Division at the Max Born Institute for Nonlinear Optics and Short Pulse Spectroscopy. She also holds a joint appointment in experimental physics Professor at HU Berlin.

She is widely recognized for her pioneering work in developing broadband spectroscopy techniques using optical frequency combs. These techniques including dual-comb spectroscopy and Fourier-transform spectroscopy, have significantly pushed the limits of precision spectroscopy.

 Education and Career 
Nathalie Picqué received a master's degree in laser physics from Paris-Sorbonne University and Ecole Polytechnique, in Paris, France. She went on to conduct her doctoral research at the Paris-Saclay University in Orsay, France in Fourier-transform spectroscopy, graduating in 1998. This was followed by a postdoctoral position at the European Laboratory for Non-Linear Spectroscopy in Florence, Italy, where she expanded on her doctoral work in frequency metrology and precision measurement with lasers. In 2001, she accepted a CNRS position and joined the  Laboratoire de Photophysique Moléculaire in Orsay (now ISMO).

In 2008, she moved to the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics (MPQ) on a sabbatical, to work with the then-recent Nobel Laureate Theodor W. Hänsch, on dual-combs spectroscopy. Eventually, she continued as a group leader at MPQ in Garching before moving to her current position at the Max Born Institute for Non Linear Optics and Short Pulse Spectroscopy.
