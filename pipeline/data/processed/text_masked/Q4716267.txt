NAME's work with Thibault Damour of reducing the two-body problem in general relativity to an effective one-body formalism,  and PRON research at the intersection of analytical-relativity modeling and numerical relativity simulations were employed to observe gravitational waves from merging binary black holes for the first time, and infer their astrophysical and cosmological properties.
