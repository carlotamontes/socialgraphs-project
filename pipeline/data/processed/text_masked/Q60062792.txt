Quantum kinetic theory NAME co‑developed the real‑time NAME–Baym approach for correlated quantum systems out of equilibrium, including the electron gas, electron-hole plasmas, and dense quantum plasmas, a method that laid the groundwork for modern simulations of plasma oscillations in correlated systems and ultrafast dynamics following laser excitation or potential quenches.
