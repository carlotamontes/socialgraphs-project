Research De Wijn describes PRON research area as "applied nonequilibrium statistical mechanics".
It has included studies in the tribology of nanoscale surfaces and in the viscosity of complex fluids and gases, including the behavior of quick clay, phase transitions caused by too-careful driving in traffic jams, and nanoscale models of the material behavior that causes friction.
