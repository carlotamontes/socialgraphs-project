NAME (born 27 September 1956) is a French chemist.
An international specialist in bioinorganic chemistry, PRON currently teaches at the Collège de France in Paris, where PRON heads the Laboratory of Chemistry of Biological Processes.
Biography  NAME is a graduate of the École normale supérieure de l'enseignement technique (which became the École normale supérieure Paris-Saclay in 2016), and holds a doctorate in science.
In 2005, PRON was elected a member of the French Academy of Sciences and in 2019 foreign member of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences.
Since 2008–2009, PRON holds the Chair of Chemistry of Biological Processes at the Collège de France.
PRON chairs the Fondation du Collège de France and is a member of the EDF scientific council.
Research studies  NAME has deepened PRON understanding of the structure and reactivity of the metal centres present in metalloproteins.
Its research can have applications in the fields of chemistry (selective catalysts), health (anti-cancer, antioxidants), environment (bioremediation, green chemistry) and energy (hydrogen production and carbon dioxide transformation).

Position statements  PRON intervened in the public debate to encourage public reappropriation of major scientific research and simplification of the French research system; according to PRON, the evaluation of researchers a posteriori is the only method for taking the necessary risks in research.
During the reflection on the energy transition, with PRON colleagues from the French Academy of Sciences, PRON encouraged the use of nuclear and shale gas.
As the problem of storing and restoring intermittent renewable energies has not been solved, PRON criticizes the forced march towards energy transition.
PRON argues that the intensification of nuclear use is a necessity to reduce CO2 emissions.
On 29 December 2018, PRON strongly criticised L'Affaire du siècle, a successful petition in a World Forum calling for the French State to be condemned for its failure to respect climate commitments, as "unfair, stupid, and ineffective".
