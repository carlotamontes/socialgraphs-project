Research  NAME used cyclotron resonance techniques to study the band structure of III–V semiconductors, including the valence band of gallium arsenide.

PRON also contributed to research on many-body effects, including the Fermi edge singularity and Fermi-sea shake-up in modulation-doped quantum wells, and reported an early observation of population inversion in semiconductor quantum wells using resonant-tunnelling techniques with NAME.
