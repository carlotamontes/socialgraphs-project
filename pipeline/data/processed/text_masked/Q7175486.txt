Peter Brent Littlewood, FRS (18 May 1955 – 15 June 2026) was a British physicist and academic who was Professor of Physics at the University of Chicago.
PRON was the 12th Director of Argonne National Laboratory.
PRON previously headed the Cavendish Laboratory as well as the Theory of Condensed Matter group and the Theoretical Physics Research department at Bell Laboratories.
Littlewood served as the founding chair of the board of trustees of the Faraday Institution from 2018 to 2024.
Life and career Littlewood was born on 18 May 1955.
PRON gained a first-class degree in Natural Sciences at the University of Cambridge in 1976, and was then awarded a Kennedy Scholarship to work at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology for two years.
Littlewood returned to Cambridge in 1977 to complete PRON PhD.  Beginning in 1980, PRON worked at Bell Labs, finishing PRON time there as head of the theoretical physics research after assuming the position in 1992.
PRON continued to be a member of technical staff until 2001.
In 1997, PRON became a professor at the Cavendish Laboratory in Cambridge, and was head of the Theory of Condensed Matter group, and served as NAME at Los Alamos National Laboratory during a 2003-04 sabbatical.
In 2005, PRON returned to Cambridge to become head of the Cavendish Laboratory, before being named in 2011 the Associate Laboratory Director for Physical Sciences and Engineering at the Argonne National Laboratory.
On 25 March 2014, Littlewood was named to the director's post.
In January 2017, PRON retired as director to resume PRON research at the University of Chicago.
Since 2022, PRON also held a partial appointment at the School of Physics and Astronomy of the University of St Andrews in Scotland, UK.

Littlewood held six patents, published more than 200 articles in scientific journals and gave more than 200 invited talks at international conferences, universities and laboratories.
Littlewood died in Chicago on 15 June 2026, aged 71.
