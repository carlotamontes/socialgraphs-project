NAME (born 12 December 1948) is a Distinguished Professor of Physics at Lehman College and a member of the doctoral faculty at the Graduate Center of the City University of New York.
NAME is a Fellow of the American Physical Society (APS), elected 1993 for "seminal contributions to random ferromagnetism, macroscopic quantum tunneling, and hexatic order in high Tc materials".
PRON is mostly known for PRON work on quantum tunneling of magnetization.
NAME explained magnetic avalanches experimentally observed in molecular magnets as deflagration.
NAME received PRON undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral education at Kharkiv University in Ukraine, in the school of theoretical physics founded by NAME and PRON students.
The denial by the USSR of an exit visa to Chudnovsky in 1979 led to PRON unemployment for eight years, during which PRON continued independent research in theoretical physics and participated in the unofficial NAME in Moscow.
PRON was frequently harassed and interrogated by the KGB.
In 1987 NAME was allowed to emigrate and joined the faculty of the physics department of Tufts University in Boston.
The following year PRON moved to the City University of New York (CUNY).
PRON has held visiting positions at research centers in the US, Asia, and Europe—most notably at the University of Barcelona - Spain, where PRON co-organizes Annual International Workshop on Magnetism and Superconductivity.
NAME has been active in the field of human rights.
PRON has served as chair of the APS Committee on International Freedom of Scientists, Member-at-Large  of the Forum on International Physics, Chair of the Committee on Human Rights of Scientists of the New York Academy of Sciences, and co-chair of the Committee of Concerned Scientists.
In 1990s PRON directed Program for Refugee Scientists that resettled over one hundred refugee scientists in the United States.
