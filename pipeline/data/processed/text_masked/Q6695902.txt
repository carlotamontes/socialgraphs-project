NAME (born 16 June 1961 in Perugia) is a scientist in the area of noise and nonlinear dynamics.
PRON is currently the Director of the NAME in Physical System Laboratory (NiPS Lab) at the Physics Department of the Università di Perugia, in Italy.
Education and career  PRON graduated in physics at the University of Perugia and obtained PRON Ph.D. in physics from the University of Pisa in 1991 (S. Santucci advisor) with a thesis entitled "Stochastic Resonance".
PRON is currently Professor of Physics at the Faculty of Science of the University of Perugia in Italy and the Director of The Noise in Physical Systems (NiPS) Laboratory.
NiPS, a research facility within the Physics Department of the University of Perugia, has a long-standing tradition in studying physical systems in the presence of noise.
In 2004	PRON was awarded the First prize for innovation ideas, a Spin-off competition, of the University of Perugia.
Eventually, this led to the establishment of NAME srl.
An innovative company devoted to the development of advanced technology in the field of self-powered wireless devices.
In 2016 PRON received the Special Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics for the observation of gravitational waves, opening new horizons in astronomy and physics, as a member of the LIGO-Virgo collaboration.
In 2016-2019 PRON served as President of the Fondazione POST (Museo della NAME).
During the Covid-19 pandemic, together with NAME, proposed a stochastic model for dealing with fluctuations in epidemic diffusion.
PRON scientific papers received more than 100.000 citations.
