PRON research involves the study of the interstellar medium, especially interstellar dust, photodissociation regions, and interstellar shock waves.
PRON is one of the authors (together with NAME) of the public domain DDSCAT code based on the discrete dipole approximation which has application to light scattering by non-spherical particles and nanophotonics.
