NAME (born 10 May 1975) is a professor in computational space physics at the University of Helsinki; PRON particular area of interest is magnetospheric physics and solar wind - magnetosphere interactions.
Life  Palmroth is from Sahalahti, a small village in the former municipality by the same name near the city of Tampere, Finland.
Palmroth graduated high school from Kangasala High School and completed PRON master's degree in physics from the University of Helsinki in 1999.
PRON received PRON doctorate in philosophy majoring in physics in 2003.
Palmroth completed PRON doctoral thesis in English about: Solar wind: magnetosphere interaction as determined by observations and a global MHD Simulation dealt with the interaction of the solar wind (the jet of particles originating from the sun) with the Earth with the magnetosphere based on observational data and a global magnetohydrodynamic simulation.
At the Finnish Meteorological Institute from 2011 to 2016 PRON led the Earth observation research team, and from 2013 to 2016, PRON was a space researcher there.
Since the beginning of 2017, PRON has been a professor of space physics at the University of Helsinki.
During the years 2018–2025, PRON will direct the Finnish Center of Excellence for Sustainable Space Science and Technology.
PRON has studied the need to address the tons of "space junk" that currently orbits the Earth.

Palmroth is also a member of the Academy of Technical Sciences.
