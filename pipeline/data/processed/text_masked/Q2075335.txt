NAME (born 21 January 1962 in Pforzheim, Germany) is a German physicist and materials scientist.
PRON is the director of the Fraunhofer-Institut für Werkstoffmechanik IWM, (Fraunhofer Institute for Mechanics of Materials IWM) in Freiburg, Germany and professor for mechanics of materials at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT).
NAME was elected a member of the National Academy of Engineering in 2016 for multi-scaled modelling techniques that improve fracture and deformation behavior of structural materials.
Biography NAME received PRON degree in physics (1988) and PRON doctoral degree (1991) from the University of Stuttgart.
After extended visits at the Sandia National Laboratories in Livermore, California, postdoctoral work at the Imperial College, London and the University of Oxford, PRON returned to the Max-Planck-Institute in Stuttgart as a group leader and established the group “Modeling and Simulation of Thin Film Phenomena”.
In 2001, PRON took over the professorship for Materials Mechanics at the University of Karlsruhe, now the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT).
There, PRON heads departments at the Institute for Applied Materials IAM-ZM and the Institute for Nanotechnology INT.
PRON also heads the Fraunhofer Institute for Mechanics of Materials IWM .
In 2015, NAME was appointed to the German Science Council and was GENDERWORD of its Scientific Commission from 2017 to 2021.
From 2016 to 2020, PRON was an elected member of the Fraunhofer Senate and, from 2019 to 2025, a member of the Presidium of the Fraunhofer Society as a member of the Executive Board of the Fraunhofer Group MATERIALS.
Research The German materials scientist NAME conducts research in the field mechanics of materials, focusing on questions relating to the behavior of materials under load.
PRON work centers on understanding materials, components, and systems at the limits of their load-bearing capacity.
PRON goal is to make materials and components safer, more reliable, more durable, and to improve material and energy efficiency of technical systems.
NAME investigates the structure and properties of materials from their atomic structure to their macroscopic behavior.
PRON research is aimed at understanding the behavior of materials, capturing it digitally and modeling it mathematically.
In addition to deformation, fatigue, and fracture processes, PRON is particularly interested in friction and wear.
PRON concepts of multiscale material modeling combine nano- and microstructural processes inside the material with its macroscopic behavior.
NAME and PRON team are doing pioneering work in linking material data, material modeling, and product development.
