NAME (born 1961 in Konya, Turkey) is a Turkish-Austrian physicist.
PRON is a professor of physical chemistry at the Johannes Kepler University (JKU) Linz.
There, PRON leads the Institut for Physical Chemistry as well as the Institut for Organic Solar Cells (LIOS).
Life   Niyazi Serdar Sarıçiftçi graduated from the Austrian St. George's College in Istanbul.
PRON also studied classical piano at the Music Conservatory in Istanbul (1970–1980).
Then PRON began studying physics at the University of Vienna (1980–1989).
After obtaining the doctorate (1989), PRON conducted research on the 2nd Physical Institute of the University of Stuttgart, Germany (1989–1992).
In 1992 PRON received the academic teaching license (venia docendi) by the Central Interuniversitary Commission (YÖK) in Ankara, Turkey.
PRON then went to the Institute for Polymers & Organic Solids at the University of California, Santa Barbara, United States, where PRON worked between 1992 and 1996 and, together with NAME (Nobel Prize in Chemistry, 2000) discovered and investigated the polymeric organic solar cells.
In April 1996, PRON accepted the appointment as Chair of Physical Chemistry at the Johannes Kepler University Linz.
Since 1996 PRON gives lectures as a full professor at the JKU and is the head of the Institute for Physical Chemistry.
In 2000 PRON was appointed founding director of the Linz Institute for Organic Solar Cells (LIOS) at JKU.
Between 2003 and 2009 PRON was elected to the City Council of the City of Linz (SPÖ Group).
Furthermore, Sarıçiftçi is a founding member of the Linz Circle.
PRON is also a member of various associations and societies: Fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry (FRSC), American Chemical Society (ACS), Materials Research Society (MRS), Austrian Physical Society (ÖPG), Austrian Chemical Society (GÖCH) and Fellow of SPIE.
2014, PRON was elected a corresponding member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (AAS).
Work and research priorities  Sarıçiftçi specializes in the field of organic semiconductors and their applications.
In particular, PRON has worked in the field of organic solar cells.
Chemical energy storage of solar energy by means of CO2 recycling.
