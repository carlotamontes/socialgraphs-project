Rafal NAME (born 3 August 1969) is a British experimental physicist.
PRON is currently co-director of the Ernst Ruska-Centre for Microscopy and Spectroscopy with Electrons (ER-C) in Forschungszentrum Jülich and professor of experimental physics in RWTH Aachen University.
Education NAME was educated at the University of Cambridge where PRON was awarded a PhD in 1994 for research into semiconductors.
Research PRON research involves the development of quantitative techniques in electron microscopy and has recently focused on the use of off-axis electron holography to study magnetic and electrostatic fields in nanoscale materials, thin films and devices.
