Giulia Zanderighi is an Italian-born theoretical physicist born in 1974 in Milan, Italy.
PRON is the first GENDERWORD director at the Max Planck Institute for Physics.
Education  Giulia Zanderighi received PRON undergraduate degree from the University of Milan in 1998 and PRON PhD in physics from the University of Pavia in 2001.
Career  Zanderighi held postdoctoral positions at Durham University at the Institute for Particle Physics Phenomenology in Durham (UK) from 2001 to 2003, NAME in Batavia (USA) from 2003 to 2005, and became a fellow in the theoretical department at CERN from 2005 to 2007.
In 2007, PRON became an assistant professor at the University of Oxford, and in 2014 PRON became a Professor of Physics there  and Tutorial Fellow at Wadham College in 2007.
In 2014 PRON took a leave from this position, holding in a five-year staff position at CERN.
On January 1, 2019, PRON was appointed director at the Max Planck Institute for Physics.
PRON leads the department of novel computational techniques in particle phenomenology and is the first GENDERWORD director at the institute in its more than 100-year history.
PRON is an internationally recognized expert in collider phenomenology.
