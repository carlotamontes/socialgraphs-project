NAME (born 16 September 1969) is a Norwegian chemist.
PRON is a professor of chemistry at the University of Tromsø.
PRON is author or coauthor of more than 150 scientific articles and director of the Centre for Theoretical and Computational Chemistry in Tromsø.
In 2008, PRON was the recipient of the Dirac medal from the World Association of Theoretical and Computational Chemists.
PRON is one of the main contributors to the NAME program package.
In 2012 PRON was elected fellow of the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters.
Since April 2010, NAME is president of the Norwegian Chemical Society.
In February 2013, NAME was elected Prorektor (= Vicepresident) for research of the University of Tromsø.
PRON will take up this function on August 1, 2013.
