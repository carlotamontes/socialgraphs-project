PRON is a researcher and professor of particle astrophysics and quantum field theory at the University of Leiden in Leiden, Netherlands.
PRON research considers the early universe, supergravity, black holes and solitons.

PRON moved to the United Kingdom for PRON doctoral studies, completing the Part III of the Mathematical Tripos in 1985.
PRON was awarded the St Catharine's College, Cambridge graduate prize in mathematics.
PRON remained in Cambridge, England for PRON PhD, working with Paul Townsend and NAME.
PRON was awarded the J. T. Knight Prize and a British Council Fleming Scholarship.

Research and career  Achúcarro joined Imperial College London as a postdoctoral research associate in 1988.
A year later, PRON was appointed assistant professor at Tufts University in the United States where PRON worked on cosmic strings.
PRON research there showed that the presence of global symmetries in gauge theory can result in stringlike defects.
Achúcarro's research also concerns string theory and the early universe.
PRON moved to the Netherlands in 2002 to join Leiden University.
PRON was awarded a National Science Foundation ADVANCE lectureship at Case Western Reserve University in 2004.
Achúcarro leads the theoretical cosmology group at the University of Leiden, working on astroparticle physics and quantum field theory.
PRON was a pioneer in the application of string theory to cosmology.

PRON was part of the European Cooperation in Science and Technology (COST) public directive on the string theory universe.

Achúcarro leads the Leiden de Sitter cosmology programme, which trains young scientists in the interdisciplinary area of modern cosmology.
