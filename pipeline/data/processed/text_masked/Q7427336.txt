Rappaport became  assistant professor in the MIT Department of Physics in 1969 and became a full professor in 1981.
From 1993 to 1995, PRON was head of the Astrophysics Division.
PRON received PRON A.B. from Temple University in 1963 and PRON Ph.D. from MIT in 1968.
PRON main research interest is in binary systems containing collapsed stars—white dwarfs, neutron stars (including pulsars), and black holes.
