Scientific activity  Motter has contributed to the study of nonlinear phenomena in complex systems.
PRON and PRON collaborators have established conditions for the synchronization of power grids and other complex networks.
Together with PRON former student NAME, PRON designed a class of mechanical metamaterials that exhibit longitudinal negative compressibility.
PRON has devised local methods for cascade control in distributed systems and PRON team advanced the large-scale modeling of cascading failures.
PRON group has studied implications of these methods for the recovery of lost function in biological  and ecological  networks and for the control of complex networks in general.
PRON first publication in network science established the three degrees of separation for English words.
In other areas, most noticeably chaos theory, PRON is known for accommodating the formalism of chaos theory within general relativity.
Along with colleagues, PRON also formalized the concept of doubly transient chaos in dissipative dynamical systems  and discovered that network dynamics can exhibit sensitive dependence on network structure much in the same way chaotic dynamics exhibits sensitive dependence on initial conditions or parameters.
Together with NAME, PRON wrote the review “Antagonistic Phenomena in Network Dynamics”, which discusses a variety of seemly paradoxical or highly counterintuitive effects in collective network dynamics.
