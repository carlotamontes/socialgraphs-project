Bérengère Dubrulle (born 1965) is a French astrophysicist whose research involves the study of turbulence and vortices in fluid dynamics and magnetohydrodynamics, and their application in modeling planet formation and climate change.
PRON is a director of research for the French National Centre for Scientific Research, and the director of the École de physique des Houches.
Education and career NAME was born in 1965 in Dieppe, on the north coast of France.
PRON was a student at the École normale supérieure de jeunes filles from 1985 to 1989, earning a master's degree in quantum mechanics in 1987 through Pierre and Marie Curie University and defending a Ph.D. in astrophysics in 1990 through NAME under the direction of .
PRON completed a habilitation in physics in 1996, through Paris Diderot University.
Meanwhile, PRON became a scientist (chargée de recherche) in the French National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS) in 1991, was promoted to director of research in 2000, and was named as director of research, exceptional class, in 2021.
PRON is affiliated with the NAME laboratory (Systèmes Physiques Hors-équilibres hYdrodynamiques éNergie et compleXité, out-of-equilibrium Systems and Physics - HYdrodynamics - eNergy and compleXity) at CEA Paris-Saclay.
Since 2020, PRON has also been director of the École de physique des Houches.
