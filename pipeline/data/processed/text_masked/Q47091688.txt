Between 1973 and 1998 PRON worked at Philips Research Laboratories.
PRON was professor of optics at Delft University of Technology between 1998 and 2008.

PRON studied physics at Delft University of Technology.

PRON PhD thesis was on holography using spatially incoherent light.
In 1973 NAME returned to the Netherlands to work at Philips Research Laboratories.

In 1988 Braat became a part-time professor of geometrical optics at Delft University of Technology.
In 1998 PRON became a full professor of optics.
During this time PRON performed research on extreme ultraviolet lithography, optical aperture synthesis for astronomy, high density optical recording, NAME theory and Terahertz imaging.
In 1991 NAME was one of the founders of the European Optical Society.
From 2004 to 2006 PRON served as its President.
Braat retired as professor in 2008.
In 1994 NAME won the Edward Longstreth Medal for Computer and Cognitive Science of the Franklin Institute for "contributions to optical data recording and design of aspherical objective lenses for read-out systems of optical storage".
Braat was elected a member of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences in 2001.
PRON became a Fellow of the European Optical Society in 2012.
In 2019 PRON received the Holst Memorial Lecture Award from Eindhoven University of Technology.
