NAME went on to work with McEwen and NAME on a 1990 study that examined the brain using Golgi's method, a technique first described by Camillo Golgi in 1873.
The study showed that estradiol increased the number and density of excitatory synapses of CA1 pyramidal cells in the rat hippocampus, as well as the density of dendritic spines.
