HSQC  In 1980 NAME and NAME introduced the heteronuclear single quantum coherence (HSQC) experiment.
Their paper, "Natural abundance nitrogen-15 NMR by enhanced heteronuclear spectroscopy", described a two-dimensional Fourier-transform method using polarization transfer to enhance the observation of nitrogen-15 nuclei at natural abundance.
HSQC subsequently became a widely used experiment in solution NMR, including the study of organic molecules and proteins.
The experiment correlates the chemical shifts of protons with those of heteronuclei such as carbon-13 or nitrogen-15 and became a basis for numerous later heteronuclear NMR experiments.
