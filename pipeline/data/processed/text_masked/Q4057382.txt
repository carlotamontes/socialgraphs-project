NAME (; ; born 2 December 1938) is an Armenian Ukrainian mathematician and theoretical physicist, professor and head of the Department of Theoretical Physics at  Odessa University.
PRON is known for PRON contributions to operator theory and functional analysis.
Biography Adamyan was born in Odessa on 2 December 1938.
In 1956–1961 Adamyan studied at the Odessa I.I.Mechnikov State University.
PRON graduated with a degree in theoretical physics, supervised by NAME.
In 1961–1964 PRON was a PhD student in the Odessa Construction Institute, under the supervision of NAME.
From 1964 to 1966 PRON worked as a senior research fellow at the Odessa Lomonosov Technological Institute.
On 20 June 1966, Adamyan defended PRON PhD thesis in functional analysis and the theory of functions specialty at the Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics, Yerevan University.
PRON taught at the Department of Mathematics at the Odessa Construction Institute for one year after PRON thesis defense.
Afterwards, PRON headed a laboratory at the Institute of Physics of Odessa State University until 1975.
On 17 October 1974 NAME defended PRON doctoral thesis in functional analysis and theory of functions at the Academy of Sciences of the Institute of Mathematics in Kiev.
After defending PRON doctoral thesis, PRON became an associate professor at Odessa State University, and then, in 1977, full professor.
In 1978, PRON became the head of the Department of Theoretical Physics.
Within the department, PRON stimulated the development of new areas, such as plasma physics, solid state physics and physics of nanostructures (see nanotechnology).
Research Adamyan's principal fields of research are functional analysis and operator theory.
In addition, since 1966 PRON has been working on different problems of theoretical physics, such as plasma theory and the theory of nanostructures.
Throughout PRON long scientific activity Professor NAME worked in universities in Australia, New Zealand, Germany and Spain and received several honorary degrees and awards.
In 1994–1995 PRON was appointed Soros Professor.
NAME is a member of American Mathematical Society (AMS), the Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik (Society of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics) and the Ukrainian Physical Society.
