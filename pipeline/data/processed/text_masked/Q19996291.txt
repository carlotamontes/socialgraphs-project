Education and career  NAME was born in Lausanne and studied physics at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, where PRON received PRON diploma in 1984 and PhD in 1987 under the supervision of NAME.
PRON received habilitation from the University of Lyon in 1991.
Research  PRON research activities are related with applications of ultrafast spectroscopy for biological, medical, and environmental research.
PRON is working with NAME on laser beams to control the weather.
The technique is similar to cloud seeding, and could potentially influence the triggering and guiding of lightning.
In 2018, PRON is one of the two winners of the ZEISS Research Award for PRON research on high intensity lasers and their applications to atmospheric sciences.
Since 2017 PRON group is taking part into the Consortium of the European project Laser Lighting Rod.
In 2023 the LLR consortium demonstrated the first guiding of natural lightning over more than 50 m using laser filamentation.
