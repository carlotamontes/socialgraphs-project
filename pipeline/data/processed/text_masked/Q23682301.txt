NAME (born 1948) is a Portuguese scientist specialized in protein crystallography.
PRON was a full professor at the Institute of Chemical and Biological Technology (ITQB) of the NOVA University Lisbon and former president of the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT), the main funding institution for science in Portugal.
Early life and training NAME Fonseca NAME was born on 31 July 1948 in Vila Nova de Famalicão in the north of Portugal.
After graduating in chemical engineering at the University of Porto in 1971, PRON studied chemical crystallography at Imperial College London, obtaining a doctorate in 1978.
Academic career The year after obtaining PRON doctorate, NAME took up a position as assistant professor at the Instituto Superior Técnico of the University of Lisbon, a position PRON held until 1998.
There PRON created and directed a research group on crystallography of organic, inorganic and organometallic molecules.
In 1989, PRON participated in the foundation of the Institute of Chemical and Biological Technology (ITQB), based in Oeiras, as part of the NOVA University, where PRON became a full professor in 1998.
In 1996 PRON was appointed as ITQB's deputy director, a position PRON held until 2005.
Between 2007 and 2013, PRON was vice-chancellor of the NOVA University.
In PRON career PRON has supervised 30 postgraduate students.
NAME's research work has focused on bioinorganic chemistry and the study of proteins using the X-ray crystallography technique.
PRON is the coordinator of activities in this area at ITQB, where PRON also directs the structural genomics laboratory.
PRON has studied molecules of interest to medicine, namely proteins related to the immune system, and differentiated various structures of metalloproteins and metalloenzymes.
Much of PRON research work requires the use of synchrotron radiation to study the structure of proteins.
In order to facilitate access for Portuguese scientists, PRON coordinated Portugal's accession process to the European Synchrotron Radiation Facility (ESRF) in Grenoble and represented Portugal on the Board of the ESRF from 1998 to 2002.
PRON was a member of the ESRF Science Advisory Committee between 2012 and 2015.
Since 2012 PRON has been a member of the Proposal Review Committee of the Swiss Light Source Synchrotron and a member of several other similar advisory boards, including the European Research Council and the Academy of Finland.
PRON was vice-president of the Portuguese Biochemical Society from 1998 to 2004.
PRON edited the Journal of Biological Inorganic Chemistry between 2001 and 2015.
In April 2015 NAME was appointed President of the Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia (Foundation for Science and Technology - FCT), a public entity under the supervision of the Ministry of Education and Science, that decides on research funding.
A political appointment, PRON left the position in January 2016.
PRON had also been an advisor to the board of directors of FCT under the previous presidency of NAME.
When PRON resigned in 2015, NAME was named president.
Awards and honours Awards and honours received by NAME include: NAME received the 2004 EuroBIC Medal, awarded by the European Congress on Bioorganic Chemistry in recognition of PRON contributions to this scientific area.
In 2004, PRON received an award for the best article on biophysics published in 2003, from the Sociedade Portuguesa de Biofísica.
Both PRON home municipality, NAME, and the municipality where  PRON works, Oeiras, headquarters of the ITQB, have awarded PRON a Municipal Medal of Honour.
In 2008 PRON received the Câmara Pestana Prize, jointly awarded by the Instituto Bacteriológico de Câmara Pestana and NAME of Portugal for research work on RNA published in the journal, Nature, in 2006.
NAME was one of twenty Portuguese GENDERWORD scientists photographed by NAME for an exhibition on GENDERWORD in Science, which took place in March 2015 in Lisbon.
