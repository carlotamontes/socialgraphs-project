Marta Sales-Pardo (born 1976) is a Spanish statistical physicist, complex systems scientist, and network scientist.
PRON is an associate professor at Rovira i Virgili University, in the Department of Chemical Engineering.
PRON is notable for PRON contributions to complex networks, where PRON has studied both social and biochemical systems, especially the contribution of each node or individual to the overall network.
PRON research has earned several mentions in the Fulbright Program and the ICREA Program, among others.
Education and career   Sales-Pardo earned a Bachelors of Science in physics from the University of Barcelona in 1998.
PRON then earned a PhD in physics from the University of Barcelona in 2002, advised by Felix Ritort.
In 2003, PRON became a post-doctoral fellow at the Chemical and Biological Engineering Department at Northwestern University.
PRON obtained the Fulbright scholarship in 2004 for two years, and in 2008 became a research assistant professor at Northwestern University.
Since 2010, PRON has been an associate professor at Rovira i Virgili University.
Research  Sales-Pardo is most well known for PRON studies on complex networks, including a paper studying the statistical significance of modularity in randomly-generated complex networks.
PRON most cited works discuss how to mine data from large complex networks accurately, successfully capturing the properties of the mined distribution.
