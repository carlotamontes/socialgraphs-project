NAME (born 21 March 1977) is a German high jumper of Ghanaian descent.
At the 1999 World Championships PRON reached the final, but failed to clear the opening height there.
In June 2000 in Rehlingen PRON achieved a career best jump of 1.94 metres.
Two months later PRON finished eighth at the 2000 Olympics, equalling PRON personal best result.
PRON became German champion in 2000 and won PRON only national indoor medal in the same year, a silver medal.
PRON represented the clubs TSV Bayer 04 Leverkusen and OSC Berlin.
In June 2001, again in Rehlingen, PRON tested positive for the anabolic steroid oxandrolone.
PRON received a suspension from the sport which lasted between July 2001 and July 2003.
