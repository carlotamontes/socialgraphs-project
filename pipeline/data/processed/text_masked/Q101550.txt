NAME (born 27 December 1971 in Sobernheim, now Bad Sobernheim, Germany) is a German physicist and director at the Max Planck Institute for Nuclear Physics in Heidelberg, Germany.
Life and scientific work Blaum studied physics at the Johannes Gutenberg-University Mainz, Germany.
After PRON physics diploma in 1997 and several research visits at the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, Richland, Washington, US, PRON graduated in 2000 as Doctor rerum naturalium in physics.
From 2000 to 2002 PRON was postdoctoral research associate of GSI Darmstadt (working group of NAME) and until 2004 Research Associate (CERN Fellow) at the European Organization for Nuclear Research CERN, Geneva, Switzerland and Project Leader for "Mass spectrometry of exotic nuclides with ISOLTRAP" at ISOLDE.
In October 2004 NAME became Project Leader of the "Helmholtz Research Group for Young Investigators" on "Experiments with Stored and Cooled Ions" at the Johannes Gutenberg-University Mainz.
In 2006 PRON habilitated in experimental physics at the University NAME about High precision mass spectrometry with Penning traps and storage rings.
From 2004 to 2008 Blaum taught at the Johannes Gutenberg-University NAME.
PRON achievement was honored with the Teaching Award of Rheinland-Pfalz 2006.
In October 2007 PRON was appointed as director and scientific member at the Max-Planck-Institute for Nuclear Physics in Heidelberg, Germany.
Since April 2008 PRON is honorary professor (W3) at the Ruprecht-Karls-University Heidelberg, Germany.
In November 2008 PRON was elected as a Fellow of the American Physical Society (APS).
After PRON first term (2012–2014), PRON was again vice GENDERWORD of the advisory committee "Hadrons and Nuclei" of the Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) from 2018 to March 2020.
During the membership period 2012–2019 NAME was a member of the DFG (German Research Foundation) Review Board 308 "Optics, Quantum Optics and Physics of Atoms, NAME and NAME".
From June 2016 to December 2019 PRON was deputy GENDERWORD of the FAIR/GSI Joint Scientific Council, GENDERWORD of the Scientific Committee of GSI and member of the supervisory board of GSI.
From July 2020 to June 2023, NAME was vice president of the Max Planck Society and responsible for the Institutes of the Chemistry, Physics and Technology Section.
Main research areas PRON scientific work focuses on precision experiments with stored and cooled ions as well as the investigation of fundamental processes of molecular ions.
Another main research field is the development of novel storage, cooling and detection techniques for future experiments.
