NAME (born October 29, 1942, in Terracina) is an Italian theoretical physicist who works in the field of elementary particle physics, quantum field theory and string theory.
Life Di Vecchia graduated from the University of Rome with NAME in 1966.
As a post-doctoral researcher, PRON worked at the Nuclear Research Center in Frascati (where a permanent position was offered to PRON) and spent two years at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and CERN.
In 1974 PRON became Assistant Professor at the NORDITA in Copenhagen.
In 1978 PRON came back for a year at CERN.
In 1979 PRON became a professor at the Free University of Berlin and from 1980 to 1986 PRON taught at the Bergische Universität Wuppertal.
Since 1986 PRON has been a professor at NORDITA.
Since NORDITA moved to Stockholm PRON spent half of the time there and half of the time at the Niels Bohr Institute in Copenhagen.
PRON actively participates to the tea time breaks at the Niels Bohr Institute, organized everyday by NAME at 15.30.
Work In the 1970s Di Vecchia was one of the pioneers of string theory.
Among other things, PRON formulated with NAME and others the locally supersymmetric Lagrangian for fermionic strings (i.e. those with fermionic excitations, half-integer spin).
Previously, the Nambu-Goto action had been known for the bosonic string and different groups tried to construct a fermionic action.
In 1972, along with NAME and NAME, PRON introduced the "DDF states" scheme (also known as the "DDF construction"), which is named by the initials of the three scientists.
Along with NAME and NAME PRON formulated string theory as a two-dimensional analogue of the general theory of relativity.
Afterwards, PRON turned PRON investigations to instantons in quantum field theory and other.
PRON returned to string theory in 1981 with the publication of the works by NAME who used the effect of NAME and colleagues for the quantization of strings, then known as the NAME action.
In the 2000s PRON focused on the expansion of the AdS/CFT correspondence to low supersymmetric and non-conformal gauge theories, the construction of four-dimensional effective Lagrangians for lower energies from the compactification of magnetised D-brane models, and the high-energy scattering of closed strings in the framework of the theory of D-branes.
Since 1994, PRON has been organizing Scandinavian conferences for string theory at NORDITA.
In 2003 PRON became a member of the Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters.
