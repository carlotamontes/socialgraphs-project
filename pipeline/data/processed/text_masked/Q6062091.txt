NAME (Greek: Ιωάννης Λυριντζής; born 2 November 1953) is a professor of physics in archaeology (archaeological science) whose field of specialization is the application of natural sciences to archaeology and cultural heritage.
PRON studied physics at the University of Patras and continued at the University of Edinburgh, where PRON obtained PRON Ph.D. in 1980.
PRON has undertaken postgraduate work at the University of Oxford, NAME, the University of Edinburgh, and the Academy of Athens.
Academic career  Liritzis is a professor of Archaeometry and natural sciences at Henan University in Kaifeng, China and was professor in archaeometry and natural sciences at the University of the Aegean.
PRON directed and founded the Laboratory of Archaeometry in 1999 and the Laboratory of Environmental Archaeology.
PRON was also director and initiator of the Masters course Applied Archaeological Sciences.
PRON served as head of the Department of Mediterranean Studies at the University of the Aegean, a member of the University Senate, and a member of the Executive Committee of the National Recognition of Foreign Academic Diplomas.
PRON worked at the Greek Ministry of Culture (1984–1989) and the Academy of Athens (1989–1999).
PRON has made contributions to several interdisciplinary research fields (geophysics, astronomy, planetology, paleomagnetism, paleoclimatology) and PRON work has been published in scientific journals such as Nature.
Liritzis is a member of the Academia Europaea, vice-president of the European Academy of Sciences and Arts, and a member of the World Academy of Art and Science.
Work Liritzis is known for developing two dating methods.
In 1994, PRON introduced surface luminescence dating, which extended the principles of optical dating and thermoluminescence dating to carved rock surfaces (granite, basalt and sandstone) from ancient monuments and artifacts.
In 2002, PRON introduced a new approach to obsidian hydration dating based on the surface saturation layer and the SIMS profile of hydrogen (SIMS-SS method).
Both methods have since been refined and extended by other scientists.
PRON has also made contributions in archaeoastronomy and in geophysics, including work on seismicity statistics and archaeomagnetism.
PRON initiated the Delphi4Delphi International Project.
NAME was elected as a membre correspondent NAME, Arts et Belles-Lettres de Dijon and a member of the European Academy of Sciences and Arts.
PRON was awarded the Prize of the Academy of Athens for PRON book Archaeometry: Dating Methods in Archaeology (1986) and the Costa Navarino International Archeometry Award (2010) issued by the University of the Peloponesse.
PRON held visiting positions at Henan University (China), University of California San Diego, and Edinburgh University.
PRON is the principal investigator who initiated and coordinates the Aegean University archaeological excavation project at Delphi and NAME, a Late Mycenaean site near Delphi, Greece.
The archaeology and history of Egypt was introduced into Greek university curricula in the Department of Mediterranean Studies of the University of the Aegean in 1999.
Liritzis established the chair and facilitated the appointment of the first lecturer in Egyptology in 2003.
Protocols of collaboration were established between Egyptian universities and the University of the Aegean.
The Heritage journal published an honorary volume upon PRON retirement, focusing on PRON contributions and collaboration with Egyptian researchers on the study of cultural heritage in Egypt.
