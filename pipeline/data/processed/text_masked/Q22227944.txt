PRON was an adjunct professor of chemical engineering at King Abdulaziz University in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia (2011-2022).

In 2024, PRON won the Top-Talent Award of Zhejiang Province, China, to set up a center for click chemistry in materials science.
PRON serves/served on the editorial advisory boards of Langmuir, Advanced Materials Interfaces and Applied Surface Science and was a senior editor of Langmuir from 2016 to 2020.

PRON is also the founder (2011) of a spin-off company, Surfix.
