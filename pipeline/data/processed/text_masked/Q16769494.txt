Hideo Aoki is a Japanese theoretical physicist.
PRON is an emeritus professor at the University of Tokyo, Japan, and works in the field of condensed-matter physics and cold-atom physics.
Biography Hideo Aoki studied theoretical condensed-matter physics, and received PRON PhD in physics from the University of Tokyo in 1978, where PRON doctoral research was on the quantum NAME effect.
PRON was a visiting scholar at the Cavendish Laboratory, University of Cambridge, UK in 1980-1982.
PRON then built up PRON group in 1986 at the department of physics, University of Tokyo in theoretical condensed-matter physics, as a full professor from 1998.
In 2016 PRON retired from the University of Tokyo and became an emeritus professor, and subsequently took up a research position at National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), Tsukuba, Japan until 2022.
In 2017 PRON was a guest professor at ETH Zurich, Switzerland, and also a guest scientist at the Max-Planck-Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter, Hamburg in Germany in 2018.
Research The theoretical works of NAME and PRON group are focussed on many-body and topological effects in electron systems, namely, superconductivity, magnetism, and topological systems (including graphene).
For these system PRON explored materials designed for correlated electron systems, and in particular non-equilibrium phenomena in recent years.
PRON has authored or co-authored over 300 articles ranging across condensed-matter physics.
