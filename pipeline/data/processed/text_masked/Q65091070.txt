NAME (born 1969) is a quantum physicist and professor in Department of Physics at the University of Otago, NAME, New Zealand.
PRON was the inaugural director of the Dodd-Walls Centre for Photonic and Quantum Technologies, a New Zealand government-funded national Centre of Research Excellence.
PRON served as director from the centre's inception in January 2015 through to the end of March 2023.
Hutchinson's research interests are in the areas of quantum biology, NAME condensates, and the underlying mathematics of quantum physics.
Biography  Born in England in 1969, Hutchinson completed a BSc at the University of Exeter, UK, and received a PhD degree there in 1994 in the area of theoretical physics.
Whilst at Exeter PRON was awarded half colours in 1992 for "excellence in student sport".
PRON also holds a PGDip (Arts) in philosophy from the University of Otago.
PRON moved to New Zealand in 2000, and became a full citizen in February 2015.
Hutchinson is currently chair of the board at Otago Museum, and was a departmental science advisor for New Zealand's Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment from 2021 - 2024.
Academic career  Hutchinson held postdoctoral research fellowships at Dublin City University, Ireland, and then at Queen's University at Kingston, Ontario.
PRON was lecturer in physics at Somerville College, University of Oxford in the UK from 1997 to 2000.
After visiting the University of Otago, NAME, New Zealand during a Royal Society of London Study Visit in 1998, PRON moved to Dunedin in 2000 to take up a lectureship position at the university.
PRON was promoted to senior lecturer in 2003, then associate professor, and in February 2015 became a full professor.
In 2004 PRON was the first recipient of Otago University's Rowheath Trust Award and NAME, for outstanding research performance by an early-career researcher.
PRON attended the annual World Economic Forum "Meeting of New Champions" in Tianjin (2008) and Dalian (2009), selected as "one of the world's 60 outstanding young scientists"; the attendees went on to form the Global Young Academy in Berlin in 2010, of which Hutchinson is an alumnus.
Hutchinson was a member of the Jack Dodd Centre for Quantum Technology at Otago's Department of Physics, and served as the director from 2010 to 2015, except for a period in 2012 when PRON was a visiting professor at the Centre for Quantum Technologies at the National University of Singapore.
In 2018 NAME was visiting fellow at Merton College at the University of Oxford, an opportunity given to just a handful of educators around the world per year.
On 1 January 2015 PRON became director of the newly founded Dodd-Walls Centre for Photonic and Quantum Technologies, a Centre of Research Excellence (CoRE).
Hosted by the University of Otago, the NAME-Walls centre is composed of approximately 200 researchers and students from six New Zealand universities: Otago University, the University of Auckland, Victoria University of Wellington, AUT, Massey University, and the University of Canterbury.

Research  Hutchinson is a theoretical physicist, specialising in quantum systems and ultra-cold atomic gases.
Specifically, PRON examines the effects of disorder in Bose–Einstein condensates (systems first created in 1995, and at University of Otago since 1998, which form at close to absolute zero).
PRON has also worked on understanding energy transport on the FMO complex found in deep-sea bacteria which rely on infrared photons, and its possible implications for producing more efficient solar photovoltaic panels.
