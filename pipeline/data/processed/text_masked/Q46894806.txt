PRON is a professor at the Graz University of Technology and heads the Institute of Chemistry and Technology of Materials (ICTM).
Career   studied chemistry at the Leibniz University Hannover where PRON also earned PRON doctorate.
PRON habilitation focused on ultraslow ion transport in solids.
Since 2011, PRON has been a professor of solid-state chemistry of modern energy storage systems at the Graz University of Technology.
From 2012 to 2019, PRON also led the CD-Lab "Lithium Batteries: Aging Effects, Technology and New Materials".
In 2016, PRON became the head of the Institute of Chemistry and Technology of Materials (ICTM) at TU Graz.
Research  's research focuses on ionic diffusion in crystalline and amorphous solids.
PRON group uses techniques such as temperature-dependent nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) relaxation measurements and broadband conductivity spectroscopy to study ionic transport processes.
PRON is the author or co-author of over 200 scientific publications.
