NAME (born 1956) is The Tamaki Endowed Chair of Atmospheric Sciences of the University of Washington and a fellow at the American Geophysical Union.
PRON research interests include understanding how interactions between the ocean, land, atmosphere, and sea ice lead to climatic variability at timescales that vary from seasonal to decadal timescales, as well as the paleoclimate.
PRON is also interested in how climate variability (including El Nino) affects food production.
PRON received PRON PhD in 1988 at the University of Washington Department of Atmospheric Sciences.
PRON has published over 100 papers in peer-reviewed journals in atmospheric sciences and oceanography.
PRON also helps organize an annual set of climate dynamics courses.
NAME won the Carl-Gustaf Rossby Research Medal from the American Meteorological Society in 2021, for PRON "contributions to understanding climate variability for phenomena ranging from the El Niño/Southern Oscillation and the Pacific Decadal Oscillation to paleoclimate."
