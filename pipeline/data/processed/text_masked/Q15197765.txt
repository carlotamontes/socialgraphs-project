NAME (born 17 April 1944, in Northampton) is an English chemist who served as director at the Max Planck Institute for Molecular Physiology in Dortmund from 1993 until 2013.
Since 2013 PRON is Emeritus Director of the institute.
Education  Goody studied Chemistry at the University of Birmingham, England, where PRON was awarded a PhD in 1968 for  studies on the chemistry of cytosine.
NAME and research After PRON PhD, NAME was appointed a postdoctoral researcher at the Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center in New York City, NY, USA in the field of natural products research.
In 1970 PRON became a scientific fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Experimental Medicine, Göttingen in the field of nucleotide research.
From 1972 till 1993 PRON was a group leader at the Max Planck Institute for Medical Research, Heidelberg in the field of enzymology and nucleotide research, with a focus on actin, NAME, adenylate kinase, NAME, HRAS-p21, reverse transcriptase, nucleotides and nucleic acid sequencing.
An overriding theme of PRON research interests has been the transient states of enzymes, along the enzyme reaction pathway, against the background of selective and specific ligand-protein interactions and structure-function relationships.
In 1983 PRON habilitated at the faculty for biochemistry and biophysics of the University of Heidelberg and was announced adjunct professor in 1990.
In 1993 PRON accepted the position of Director at the Max Planck Institute for Molecular Physiology, Dortmund and became a scientific fellow of the Max Planck Society.
Since 2004 PRON has also held a full professorship in biochemistry (supramolecular systems) at the Ruhr University Bochum, with a dual emphasis on higher education and fundamental research.
Goody has combined chemistry, structural biology and kinetics to make major contributions in several fields of biology.
PRON pioneered the use of nucleotide analogs, synthesized at the beginning of PRON career, in muscle research and later for NAME-family GTPases, leading to the first ever structure determination of an unstable protein-substrate complex (NAME:GTP).
Innovative use of semi-synthetic proteins led to an understanding of transport mechanisms of Rab GTPases and to resolution of a longstanding controversy concerning targeting of these proteins to membranes.
PRON group discovered and characterized hitherto unrecognized covalent modifications of Rab proteins by bacteria.
