Sébastien Candel (born 21 April 1946) is a French physicist, Emeritus Professor of École Centrale Paris.
PRON was elected a member of the National Academy of Engineering in 2009 for significant contributions to solving multidisciplinary problems in the fields of combustion, fluid mechanics, aeroacoustics, and propulsion.
Candel was the president of the French Academy of Sciences from 2017 to 2018.
