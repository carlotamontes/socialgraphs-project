In 2017 PRON was also appointed as Robinson Professor of Theoretical Astrophysics at Caltech.
Teukolsky is one of the pioneers of numerical relativity: the subject that deals with equations involving general relativity using supercomputers.
PRON is a coauthor of the Numerical Recipes series of books on scientific computing.
Today PRON research group works on numerical relativity calculations to predict signals from the LIGO and LISA experiments.
