NAME (born September 27, 1958) is a Norwegian professor of physics and was rector (2019–2023) at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in Trondheim.
PRON was prorector of education at NTNU from August 1, 2017, to August 20, 2019.
PRON was appointed acting rector of NTNU on August 21, 2019, later being officially employed as rector on December 13 the same year.
PRON areas of research are surface and materials physics, scanning probe microscopy, photoelectron spectroscopy and density functional theory.
Education and professional career  NAME was born in Drammen.
PRON has an NAME (sivilingeniør) in applied physics from the Norwegian Institute of Technology (NTH), Trondheim, (1982), and in 1987 PRON received PRON PhD in physics at NTNU, with the thesis Infrared spectroscopy for studies of molecular vibrations at surfaces : construction and testing of a wavelength modulation infrared spectrometer.
After having finished PRON PhD, PRON worked as a post doctor at Stanford University, Stanford Synchrotron Radiation Laboratory in 1988–1989, and held various research positions at NTH, NTNU and SINTEF.
In 1998 PRON was appointed professor at the Department of Physics, NTNU.
PRON has been a strong voice in Norway for the development of synchrotron light research, especially towards, and in cooperation with, the MAX-lab in Lund.
PRON was also a member of the NAME-lab board between 2006 and 2010, and of the MAX IV Laboratory Program Advisory Committee (PAC in the period 2011–2014 PRON is currently (2018) chairing the Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC) at MAX IV Laboratory.
