PRON was previously pro-vice-chancellor for research and Hooke Professor of Experimental Physics at the University of Oxford,  and a professorial fellow at St Hugh's College, Oxford.
PRON is also director of the NQIT (Networked Quantum Information Technologies) hub within the UK National Quantum Technology Programme, which is led by the University of Oxford.
PRON is also a Fellow of the Institute of Physics, the American Physical Society and the Optical Society of America.
PRON will return to Oxford from October 2025 as Director of the Oxford Quantum Institute.
NAME was educated at Imperial College London, and The Institute of Optics, University of Rochester.
