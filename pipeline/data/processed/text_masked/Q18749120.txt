PRON received PRON B.A. from Harvard University in 1967 and was awarded PRON Ph.D. at Stanford in 1972 under the mentorship of NAME.
After a postdoctoral appointment with NAME, PRON was appointed assistant professor at Princeton University.
PRON moved to Colorado State University in 1979, and again to Columbia University in 1997, where PRON remains Professor of Chemistry.
From 1992-2003 PRON was an associate editor of Journal of the American Chemical Society  PRON laboratory demonstrated the possibility of dinuclear reductive elimination from transition metal alkyls and hydrides by comparing the mechanisms of reductive elimination from (H)2Os(CO)4, (H)(CH3)Os(CO)4, and (CH3)2Os(CO)4.
PRON group later reported some of the first detailed pKa measurements of metal hydrides and demonstrated that the rates of protonation at transition metals can be quite slow.
PRON group has also reported on the use of metal-hydride bonds as radical initiators of cyclization reactions.
