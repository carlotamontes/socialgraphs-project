Pascale Françoise Xavier Cossart (; née Gheerbrant; born 21 March 1948) is a French bacteriologist who is affiliated with the Pasteur Institute of Paris.

Biography  Cossart was born in the north of France in 1948.
PRON grew up and attended school in Arras.
Cossart earned a B.S. and M.S. from Lille University in 1968.
PRON then earned an M.S. in chemistry from Georgetown University in 1971, and PRON Ph.D. in biochemistry at the Pasteur Institute and the University of Paris in 1977 (University Paris Diderot).
PRON completed PRON postdoctoral fellowship at the Pasteur Institute.
PRON is currently a Professor and Head of the Unité des Interactions Bactéries Cellules at the Pasteur Institute.
