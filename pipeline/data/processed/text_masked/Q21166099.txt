Sydney Leach FRS (11 April 1924 – 24 December 2019) was a British scientist based in France whose research areas included the fields of spectroscopy, photophysics and photochemistry.
PRON work studying molecular ions led to new areas of interest, including planetary and atmospheric science, as well as the newly developing field of astrobiology.
PRON scientific career was based in Paris, including PRON founding Directorship at Laboratoire de Photophysique Moléculaire Orsay, and PRON later move to L'Observatoire de Meudon where PRON was based for the remainder of PRON career.
Leach was elected Fellow of the Royal Society in 1993.
