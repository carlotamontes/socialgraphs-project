Ana Cecilia Noguez Garrido  (born July 17, 1966) is a Mexican physicist, professor, and science communicator; PRON is a researcher and was the first GENDERWORD director of the Institute of Physics of the National Autonomous University of Mexico from 2019 to 2023.
Cecilia Noguez specializes in the interaction of light with matter on a nanometric scale.
In 2016, PRON was awarded with the National Prize for Arts and Sciences.
Education and early career In May 1990, Ana Cecilia Noguez obtained PRON bachelor's degree in physics at the Faculty of Sciences of the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM).
Since then, PRON has been working with the optical properties of mediums composed of nanoparticles.
In 1993 and 1995, PRON obtained a master's degree and a doctorate in physics, respectively, also at UNAM.
As part of PRON PhD, Noguez spent a research stay for over one year at the University of Rome II.
From July 1995 to 1996, PRON had a postdoctoral residency at Ohio University's Department of Physics and Astronomy.
During PRON PhD, PRON also studied the optical response of semiconductor crystal surfaces.
Career In 1996, Cecilia Noguez joined the Institute of Physics of UNAM, where PRON continues to work today.
PRON was designated as director of the Institute of Physics for the period 2019–2023, becoming the first GENDERWORD to hold the position.
Along with PRON research work, Cecilia Noguez teaches in both undergraduate and graduate programs at UNAM.
PRON is a member of the Sistema Nacional de Investigadores (since 1994), the Mexican Academy of Sciences, and the .
Noguez has conducted theoretical and computational research to study the interaction between light and matter at the nanometer scale.
Particularly, PRON has made contributions in order to understand how the geometry, size, composition, and the medium surrounding a nanoparticle influence its optical properties.
PRON work is mainly concentrated in areas such as plasmonics and surface physics.
PRON studies have contributed to the development of plasmonic nanostructures and two-dimensional films for the control of their optical activity.
Noguez's scientific contributions to the study of optical properties and surface plasmons of metallic nanoparticles had been recognized in different occasion.
For example, PRON work titled “Surface Plasmons on Metal Nanoparticles:  The Influence of Shape and Physical Environment”, published on the Journal of Physical Chemistry C, was recognized as the Mexican paper in physics written by one author with more cites.
Cecilia Noguez has dedicated part of PRON trajectory to promote nanoscience in Mexico; PRON was part of the organization of the first network of nanoscience at UNAM (REGINA).
Besides, PRON contributed to establish the Nanoscience Division of the Physical Mexican Society and the National Network of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology of CONACYT.
PRON has also participated in activities aimed to promote science among general public.
In 2018, PRON participated as a mentor for the initiative "Niñas STEM, pueden" (NAME, They Can).
