Research Early in PRON career, Strogatz worked on a variety of problems in mathematical biology, including the geometry of supercoiled DNA, the topology of three-dimensional chemical waves, and the collective behavior of biological oscillators, such as swarms of synchronously flashing fireflies.

PRON more recent work examines complex systems and their consequences in everyday life, such as the role of crowd synchronization in the wobbling of London's Millennium Bridge on its opening day, and the dynamics of structural balance in social systems.
Perhaps PRON best-known research contribution is PRON 1998 Nature paper with NAME, entitled "Collective dynamics of small-world networks".
This paper is widely regarded as a foundational contribution to the interdisciplinary field of complex networks, whose applications range from graph theory and statistical physics to sociology, business, epidemiology, and neuroscience.
