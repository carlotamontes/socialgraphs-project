NAME (born 17 January 1955) is a French experimental physicist whose research concerns fluid mechanics, suspensions of particles in liquids, and particle-laden flows.
PRON is a director of research for the French National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS), affiliated with the  Laboratoire Matière et Systèmes Complexes at the University of Paris.
Currently, NAME serves as the editor of the Journal of Fluid Mechanics Rapid edited by Cambridge University Press.

PRON earned a doctorate of the third cycle in 1981 at Paris-Sud University under the supervision of , and a state doctorate in 1986 at the University of Provence under the supervision of NAME.
PRON joined CNRS in 1982, became a director of research in 1996, and in 2015 was promoted to exceptional-class director of research.
PRON was deputy director of the Institut Universitaire des Systèmes Thermiques Industriels (IUSTI) at Aix-Marseille University from 2008 to 2011, and since 2012 has been rector of the International Centre for Mechanical Sciences (CISM), a non-profit scientific organization based in Udine, Italy.
Book NAME is the coauthor with NAME of the book A Physical Introduction to Suspension Dynamics (Cambridge University Press, 2012).
Recognition NAME is a member of the Istituto Veneto di Scienze, Lettere ed Arti.
In 2008 PRON was elected a Fellow of the American Physical Society (APS), after a nomination from the APS Division of Fluid Dynamics, "for extensive and careful experiments revealing complex phenomena in mobile particulate systems".
In 2010 the European Mechanics Society (EUROMECH) named PRON as a Fluid Mechanics Fellow, with the same commendation.
In 2021, NAME was elected a Member of the National Academy of Engineering, "for experiments and theory that enhance understanding of dispersed particulate systems".
PRON was named a knight of the Legion of Honour in 2012, and in the same year won the Prix Paul Doistau–Émile NAME of the French Academy of Sciences in the area of mechanical and computational sciences.
PRON was the 2016 winner of the EUROMECH Fluid Mechanics Prize, "in recognition of PRON ground-breaking experiments on sedimenting suspensions, demonstrating the origin of particle fluctuations and cluster formation, for PRON decisive guidance of theory through focused experiments, and for PRON important contributions to the European mechanics community".
PRON was awarded with the Fluid Dynamics Prize of the American Physical Society in 2023.
PRON received the 2024 Gay-Lussac-Humboldt Research Prize.
