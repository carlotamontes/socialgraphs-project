Günther Dissertori (born 1969) is an Italian physicist, scientist, professor for particle physics and rector at ETH Zurich.
At the beginning of 2027, PRON will become president of ETH Zurich succeeding NAME.
Life  Günther Dissertori grew up in Algund, a village in South Tyrol, Italy.
PRON studied physics at the University of Innsbruck and worked as a doctoral student at CERN in Geneva, where PRON began PRON research career.
There, PRON worked with the ALEPH particle detector at the Large Electron–Positron Collider (LEP) on studies of strong interactions, which was the subject of PRON doctoral thesis in 1997.
PRON continued working at CERN as CERN Fellow and then as member of its Research Staff.
Together with ETH colleagues, Dissertori helped to set up the CMS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) of CERN.
These experiments allowed to prove the existence of the Higgs boson.
With PRON group, Dissertori also developed new Positron emission tomography (PET) devices.
A cost-efficient brain PET scanner resulted from this work.
In 2001, PRON started teaching as assistant professor at ETH Zurich.
PRON promotion to full professor for particle physics occurred in 2007.
Dissertori received the Golden Owl Award for best teaching, bestowed by PRON students, in 2005, 2009, 2015 and 2020.
In addition, PRON was honored by the Credit Suisse Award for best teaching at ETH in 2013.
In February 2022, Günther Dissertori was elected as Rector of ETH Zurich.
