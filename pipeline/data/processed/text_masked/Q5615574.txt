NAME (born in 1967) is an Italian physicist (statistical physics) and full professor in Theoretical Physics at Ca' Foscari University of Venice.
From 2024 PRON is Director of the Institute of Complex Systems of the National Research Council of Italy  Biography Caldarelli received PRON Ph.D. from SISSA, after which PRON was a postdoc in the Department of Physics and School of Biology,  University of Manchester.
PRON then worked at the Theory of Condensed Matter Group, University of Cambridge, where PRON worked with NAME.
PRON returned to Italy as a lecturer at the National Institute for Condensed Matter (INFM)  and later as NAME in the Institute of Complex Systems of the National Research Council of Italy.
During this period, PRON was also the coordinator of the Networks subproject, part of the Complexity Project, for the Fermi Centre.
From 2012 to 2020, PRON was a professor at IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca.
PRON also spent some terms at University of Fribourg (Switzerland) and across 2003–2004, PRON has been visiting professor at École Normale Supérieure in Paris.
In CCS2018 (The Conference of Complex Systems Society), PRON was elected President of the Complex Systems Society for the mandate 2018–2021.
From July 1, 2024, PRON is the Director of the Institute of Complex Systems of National Research Council (Italy).
Research The activity of NAME is mainly focused on scale-free networks, complex networks in theoretical models and in their application to financial networks.
PRON has been coordinator of the European project COSIN, one of the first international activities based on the study of complex networks as well as coordinator of the European project FOC on the study of financial networks and the project MULTIPLEX on Multi-level Complex Networks.
All this activity contributed to the creation of network science community in the early stage of this discipline.
PRON has published numerous papers in physics and interdisciplinary journals, including Physical Review Letters and Nature.
PRON work on beauty and the stable marriage problem has been reported in the press.
PRON is also known for PRON research activity on systemic risk in networks of financial institutions.
