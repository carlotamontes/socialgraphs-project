Biography  Ishino was born in Kyoto Prefecture, Japan.
PRON received PRON BS, MS and PhD in 1981, 1983 and 1986 from Osaka University.
From 1987 to 1989, PRON served as a post-doctoral fellow in Dieter Söll's laboratory at Yale University.
In 2002, PRON became a professor at Kyushu University.
Since October 2013, PRON has been a member of the NASA Astrobiology Institute, University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign.
After completing PRON PhD, Ishino became senior research scientist at the Bioproducts Development Center of NAME.
Later in life, PRON joined BERI (Biomolecular Engineering Research Institute), in which PRON conducted research on nucleic acids-related enzymes.
Research contribution  Ishino has contributed to the development of enzymology and nucleic acids research in PRON life.
The "iap" gene in gut microbe E. coli was sequenced by Ishino and PRON colleagues in 1987.
As the DNA segment used was longer than the gene itself, they accidentally discovered a partial DNA sequence of then-unnamed CRISPR in the process, which would eventually become the basis of CRISPR gene editing.
Ishino was one of the first scientists to have detected CRISPRs in E. coli.
In 1990, Ishino began researching DNA replication of micro-organisms in the NAME domain.
