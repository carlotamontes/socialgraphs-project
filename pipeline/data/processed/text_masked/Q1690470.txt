NAME (29 March 1942 – 15 August 2024) was a German and Swiss physical chemist and specialist in NMR Spectroscopy.
PRON was one of the founding parents of the Biozentrum of the University of Basel.
PRON reached emeritus status in 2012.
Background  Joachim Seelig was born in Cologne on 29 March 1942.
PRON studied chemistry and physics from 1961 till 1966 at the University of Cologne.
In 1968 PRON graduated with a doctorate under the guidance of NAME at the Max Planck Institute for Biophysical Chemistry in Göttingen.
As a postdoc PRON conducted research on electron spin resonance at Stanford University in 1968/69.
In 1970 PRON moved as a postdoc to the Institute of Physical Chemistry at the University of Basel where PRON became a group leader and assistant professor in 1972.
PRON became full professor in 1974 and in 1982 Professor of Structural Biology at the Biozentrum of the University of Basel.
Seelig died in Basel, Switzerland on 15 August 2024, at the age of 82.
Work  Joachim Seelig developed biophysical methods for studying the structure and thermodynamic properties of biological cell membranes.
PRON investigated the interactions of proteins and lipids by EPR-spectroscopy, deuterium  and phosphorus nuclear magnetic resonance, neutron diffraction and calorimetric methods.
The quantitative characterization of the biological membrane became the international standard for further theoretical studies.
PRON second field of research was magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) in the humans and animals.
With C-13 NMR the metabolism in the human and animal brain could be traced in a non-invasive manner.
With faster MRI imaging techniques the tonotopy of the human brain has been described.
