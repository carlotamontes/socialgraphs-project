NAME (born 1979) is a Spanish-German nuclear astrophysicist whose research topics have included the creation and decay of heavy elements through the r-process, and neutrino-driven outflows, in energetic stellar events including supernovae and neutron star mergers.
PRON is a professor of theoretical astrophysics at Technische Universität Darmstadt in Germany, and a researcher in the GSI Helmholtz Centre for Heavy Ion Research in Darmstadt.
Education and career NAME was born in 1979 in Madrid.
PRON completed a Ph.D. in 2007 through the Max Planck Institute for Astrophysics under the supervision of NAME.
After postdoctoral research at Technische Universität Darmstadt and then the University of Basel, PRON returned to NAME with a tenure-track assistant professorship in 2012, and was promoted to associate professor in 2016.
PRON holds a joint appointment in the GSI Helmholtz Centre for Heavy Ion Research in Darmstadt, and from 2012 to 2017 led a Helmholtz Young Investigator Group at the centre.
Recognition NAME was named a Fellow of the American Physical Society (APS) in 2020, after a nomination from the APS Division of Nuclear Physics, "for seminal contributions in astro- and nuclear physics, especially to the understanding of heavy elements creation in supernovae, neutron star mergers, and their associated kilonova".
