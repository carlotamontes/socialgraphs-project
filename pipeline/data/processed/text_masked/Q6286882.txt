Dr. NAME is a distinguished research professor and Leo Mandelkern Professor of Polymer Science of the department of chemistry and biochemistry at Florida State University.
PRON graduated with PRON doctorate in chemistry from UMass-Amherst in 1987 and became a professor at Florida State University in 1988.

This number of issued patents places PRON second among faculty at Florida State University behind only NAME.
PRON is the founder of the start-up, nanoStrata, a company that manufactures novel robots to create biofilms.
Dr. NAME was one of three researchers from around the world awarded a Gutenberg Chair for 2011 at the Université de Strasbourg in France.
