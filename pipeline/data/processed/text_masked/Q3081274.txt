NAME (born 1947) is a French geochemist.
PRON is professor at the Ecole Normale Superieure de Lyon and a member of the Academia Europaea.
In January 2011, PRON became the first director of the Laboratory of Geology Lyon (UMR5276).
Biography In 1960 NAME studied natural sciences at the University of Montpellier.
PRON defended a graduate thesis in geochemistry (advised by NAME) at the Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris ("Paris Institute of Earth Physics"), where PRON remained several years as a teacher and researcher.
In 1976 PRON defended a thesis on isotope state geochemistry, then spent two years in the U.S. (Caltech).
In 1979 PRON was promoted to professor at the National School of Geology in NAME, where PRON remained for 12 years.
Finally, in 1991 PRON joined the Ecole Normale Superieure de Lyon where PRON now serves as the director of the department of earth and life sciences.
The scientific influence of NAME is very strong in the field of geochemistry and earth sciences in general.
PRON leading position in PRON discipline was accompanied by the acquisition in the laboratory of the first commercial sample mass spectrometer multicollection plasma source in the mid-1990s, and the editorial responsibility of prestigious international scientific journals.
In 1995 NAME authored a reference book, Introduction to Geochemical Modeling.
PRON received the 2008 Goldschmidt Award of the Geochemical Society in Vancouver, in July 2008.
PRON is married to geochemist NAME.
