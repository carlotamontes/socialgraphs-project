NAME (born on 5 June 1974 in Lausanne) is a Swiss climate scientist, professor at the Institute for Atmospheric and Climate Science of the ETH Zurich.
PRON is a specialist of extreme climate events.
Biography   Sonia Seneviratne studied biology at the University of Lausanne and environmental sciences at the ETH Zurich.
In 2002, PRON received a PhD in atmospheric and climate science from ETH Zurich.
PRON worked as postdoctoral researcher at the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
Since 2007, PRON is professor at the Institute for Atmospheric and Climate Science of the ETH Zurich.
NAME is a member of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).
PRON was a lead author of the IPCC's Special Report on Global Warming of 1.5 °C (2018) and a coordinating lead author of the Sixth Assessment Report (2021).
In 2025, PRON team published in Nature an analysis showing that the emissions of oil giants made 213 heatwaves (reported over 2000–2023) more intense and 200 times more likely.
Honours   NAME was included in Thomson Reuters's 2015 list of the most cited modern scientists.
PRON was the lead author on a 2014 article in Nature Climate Change that showed no pause in the increase of hot temperature extremes from 1997 to 2012.
PRON is a NAME committee member.
In 2013, PRON received the NAME from the American Geophysical Union.
In 2014, PRON received a consolidator grant from the European Research Council.
