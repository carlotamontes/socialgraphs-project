NAME (born 2 May 1945) is an Italian physicist working on theoretical physics of elementary particles and mathematical physics.
PRON is renowned for the discovery of theories introducing supersymmetry as a symmetry of elementary particles (super-Yang–Mills theories, together with NAME) and of supergravity, the first significant extension of NAME's general relativity, based on the principle of "local supersymmetry" (together with NAME, and NAME).
PRON is an emeritus staff member at CERN and a professor emeritus at the University of California, Los Angeles.
Career   Sergio Ferrara was born on 2 May 1945 in Rome, Italy.
PRON graduated from the University of Rome, obtaining in 1968 the Laurea Degree (the highest Degree that was awarded in Italy at the time).
Since then PRON has worked as a CNEN and INFN researcher at the Frascati National Laboratories; as a CNRS Visiting Scientist at the Laboratoire de Physique Théorique, École Normale Supérieure, Paris, and at the Theory Division at CERN, Geneva.
In 1980 PRON was nominated Full Professor of theoretical physics in Italy.
PRON became a staff member of the Theory Division at CERN in 1981.
In 1985, PRON became a Professor of Physics at the University of California, Los Angeles.
Since 1986 PRON has been a senior staff member of the Physics Department at CERN.
During the years 2010-2014 PRON has been a guest professor at CERN as Principal Investigator of the European Research Council Advanced Grant SUPERFIELDS.
PRON is currently a senior staff member, emeritus, at CERN.
Conformal field theory  In 1971–1975, Sergio Ferrara did pioneering work on conformal field theory and the conformal bootstrap.
In a series of papers written in collaboration with Raoul Gatto, NAME and Giorgio Parisi, PRON studied constraints imposed by conformal symmetry on the operator dimensions, the form of the correlation functions, the operator product expansion, and the conformal partial wave expansion for the four-point correlation functions of the theory.
This work, together with the similar work by NAME, laid the foundations of Conformal Field Theory.
NAME theory In 1974, with NAME, PRON formulated supersymmetric gauge theories, which opened the way to building supersymmetric extensions of the Standard Model of particle physics and exploring their consequences.
This result was obtained independently by NAME and NAME.
Supergravity In 1976, NAME, NAME, and NAME discovered supergravity at Stony Brook University in New York, specifically by describing pure 4D N = 1 supergravity.
It was initially proposed as a four-dimensional theory.
The theory of supergravity generalizes NAME's general theory of relativity by incorporating the principles of supersymmetry.
In 1982 Sergio together with NAME, NAME, and NAME were the first to derived the full action of 4D N = 1 supergravity.
In 2019 the three were awarded a special Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics of $3 million for the discovery.
Black hole attractors In 1995, with NAME and NAME, PRON formulated the theory of Black Hole attractors, a dynamical mechanism which determines the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy for extremal black holes in terms of their charges.
