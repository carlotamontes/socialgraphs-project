NAME (born October 2, 1949, in Benešov, Czech Republic) is an electroanalytical chemist and university teacher of analytical chemistry.
PRON graduated from Charles University, Faculty of Science, Department of Analytical Chemistry in 1972, and obtained PRON PhD in 1976 from the same institution.
From 1977 to 1987 PRON served as a Lecturer, 1986–2006 as a NAME, and from 2006 PRON is a professor at the Department of Analytical Chemistry, Faculty of Science, Charles University, Prague.
From 1993 to 1997, PRON served as the Deputy-Head and 2006–2012 as the Head of the Department of Analytical Chemistry, Charles University, Prague.
