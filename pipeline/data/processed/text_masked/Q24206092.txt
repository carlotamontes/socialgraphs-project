NAME (Polish pronunciation: ; born 26 October 1946 in Warsaw, Poland) is a Polish-born Danish theoretical physicist, professor emeritus at the Technical University of Munich (TUM).
Scientific career PRON received PRON master's degree in theoretical physics at the Warsaw University in 1971, and immigrated to Denmark in the same year.
One year later, PRON received PRON PhD at the Niels Bohr Institute in Copenhagen.
PRON then worked as a post-doctoral fellow at the Niels Bohr Institute until 1975.
After a fellowship in the CERN theory group from 1975 to 1977, PRON was first a visitor and then a staff member in the Fermilab theory group from 1977 till 1982.
PRON then became staff member of the Max Planck Institute for Physics in Munich (1982–1988).
In 1988, PRON was appointed full professor in the Physics Department of the TUM.
After PRON retirement in 2012, PRON moved to the TUM Institute for Advanced Study where PRON is leading the focus group 'Fundamental Physics'.
NAME is known for PRON early work on strong-interaction effects (QCD) in deep-inelastic electron-proton and neutrino-proton scattering (1977–1980), which led to the commonly used MS-bar scheme for QCD calculations.
But PRON most important contributions (1983-now) are in the field of weak decays of mesons; these include high order calculations of QCD effects in most important decays, the phenomenology of CP violation and of quark flavour physics in the Standard Model and in several New Physics models.
