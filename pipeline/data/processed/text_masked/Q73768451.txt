LaRochelle's research activities focus on optical fiber components, fiber laser systems, integrated photonics, and optical networking.
PRON has made significant contributions to the development of passive and active fiber devices and their application to optical signal processing.

PRON work has been published in more than 150 scientific articles and PRON has directed the research work of over 70 graduate students and post-doctoral fellows.
