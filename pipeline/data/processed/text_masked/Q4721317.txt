NAME (born 7 March 1970 in Leningrad) is a Russian and French theoretical physicist and writer.
PRON is an expert in solid state optics and semiconductor physics.
Life PRON graduated from the Saint Petersburg Polytechnical University in 1991.
PRON was a member of staff of the Ioffe Physico-Technical institute (1992–2000).
PRON graduated from the Ioffe Physico-Technical institute in 1993, with a PhD in physics and mathematics, supervisor Prof. NAME.
PRON was a professor at the Blaise Pascal University (Clermont-Ferrand, France, 1998 – 2005).
PRON is a professor at the University of Southampton (United Kingdom, 2005 – 2018).
In July 2010, PRON co-founded the Mediterranean Institute of Fundamental Physics with the support of Dr. NAME and was appointed scientific director.
In 2018, PRON joined the Westlake University (Hangzhou, China) as a Chair Professor and Director of the International Center for Polaritonics.
PRON is the sibling of physicist NAME.
PRON is married, with 4 children.
