Nicolao Fornengo (born 20 November 1966, NAME) is an Italian physicist.
PRON is currently Full Professor at the University of Turin and PRON works on astroparticle physics, neutrino physics and cosmology.
Biography PRON graduated in Physics at the University of Turin in 1990 with a first-class honour’s degree.
In the same university PRON got a Ph.
D, working in the group of NAME, and in 1995 PRON presented PRON dissertation: “Dark matter: neutralino relic abundance and its detection signals”.
PRON worked as a postdoc at the Johns Hopkins University (USA), at the University of Valencia (Spain) and as a visiting scientist at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study (South Corea), Laboratoire de Physique Theoretique (France), Galileo Galilei Institute for Theoretical Physics (Italy) and CERN (Switzerland).
PRON got a position as researcher (1999-2006) and then PRON became associate professor at the University of Turin.
PRON is currently a member of the scientific commission of the Gran Sasso National Laboratories, co-chair of the scientific commission of the International School on Astroparticle Physics (ISAAP) and chair of the governing board of the International conference on Topics in Astroparticle and Underground Physics (TAUP).
