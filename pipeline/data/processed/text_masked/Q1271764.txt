NAME (born 10 July 1943 in Berlin) is a French  theoretical physicist.
Biography Zinn-Justin was educated in physics (undergraduate 1964) at the École Polytechnique, and did graduate work in theoretical physics at Orsay, (Ph.D. 1968) under the supervision of  NAME.
Zinn-Justin has worked since 1965 as a theoretical and mathematical physicist at the Saclay Nuclear Research Centre (CEA), where PRON was head of theoretical physics in 1993−1998 .
PRON has served as a visiting professor at  the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT),   Princeton University,   State University of New York at Stony Brook (1972), and  Harvard University, and further guest scientists at  CERN.
From 1987 to 1995 PRON was Director of the Les Houches School of Physics.
In 2003 PRON became leader of DAPNIA (Department of Astrophysics, Particle Physics, Nuclear Physics and Associated Instrumentation) at Saclay.
PRON has made  seminal contributions to the renormalizability of gauge theories.
PRON is a world authority on quantum field theory in particle and phase transitions in statistical physics, and, in particular, the renormalization group organizing and connecting these two areas.
PRON has written definitive books on the subject.
In 1977, PRON was awarded the Paul Langevin Prize of the Société Française de Physique; in 1981 the Ampère prize of the French Academy of Sciences; in 1996 the Gentner-Kastler Prize of the Société Française de Physique  jointly with the Deutsche Physikalische Gesellschaft (DPG); in 2003 the Gay-Lussac-Humboldt prize.
In 2011, PRON was elected to the French Academy of Sciences.
