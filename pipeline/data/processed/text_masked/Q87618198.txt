Research The current research of NAME and PRON group centers on the development of new chemistry, physics, and technological applications of nano-structured materials – a class of materials which feature sizes less than 100 nm.
PRON research includes the chemistry and physics of nanomaterial synthesis, the application of nanomaterials to biomedical research, and developing nanomaterials for energy- and environment-related applications.
NAME co-invented soft lithography while PRON was a PhD student with NAME at Harvard University.
As an independent researcher, PRON and PRON group have made original and important contributions to the following areas: colloidal crystals and their photonic applications, colloidal assembly, one-dimensional nanostructures, electrospinning and alignment of nanofibers, electrospun nanofibers as scaffolds for tissue engineering, shape-controlled synthesis of colloidal nanocrystals and their applications in plasmonics, spectroscopy, heterogeneous catalysis, and electrocatalysis, invention of silver nanostructures with many different morphologies such as nanowires and nanocubes, and invention of gold nanocages and exploration of their biomedical applications.
