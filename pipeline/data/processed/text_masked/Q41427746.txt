PRON was the Director of AUB's Nature Conservation Center from 2013 till 2020.

PRON completed PRON doctoral studies at the University of Southern California in 1999.

Research and career  NAME returned to Lebanon after the Civil War, and joined the American University of Beirut in 2001.
PRON helped to establish the Ibsar Nature Conservation Center for Sustainable Futures in 2002, which looked to protect Lebanon's biodiversity.
PRON used to be the Director of Ibsar, which has since been renamed the Nature Conservation Center, at the American University of Beirut.
NAME established the Atmospheric and Analytical Laboratory.
In PRON early career PRON struggled to get chemicals, as the majority of Western companies would not ship to Lebanon for fear that they would be used to make weapons.
PRON research considers ambient pollutants in Lebanon and the Middle East.
NAME investigates the toxic and carcinogenic chemical constituents of electronic cigarettes and hookahs.
PRON was the first to identify compounds such as formaldehyde in hookahs, and went on to show that electronic cigarettes can generate carbon monoxide.
PRON is part of the Center for the Study of Tobacco Products at Virginia Commonwealth University.

PRON established the first Lebanese atmospheric pollutants database.
PRON became concerned about the open-air burning of Lebanese waste; and showed that the incineration could increase the amount of carcinogens in the air by 2,300%.
PRON showed a variety of toxins are emitted during the burning of waste, and measured their concentration at the top of a four-story apartment building in Beirut.
PRON identified particulate matter and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons as well as lead, cadmium, titanium and arsenic from metal burning.
