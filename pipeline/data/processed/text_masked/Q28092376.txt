NAME (born 15 July 1938) is an English ionospheric physicist.
Early life  After Merchant Taylors' School, Northwood (1951–57) PRON first degree was in physics from Imperial College London (1960) and PRON PhD was in meteorological physics from Churchill College Cambridge (1964), supervised by Dr NAME who had in turn been supervised by NAME.
Career PRON research is principally in solar-terrestrial physics and atmospheric electricity.
In 1977, PRON was a British finalist in a selection process for European astronauts.
PRON began PRON career at the University of Southampton (1966-1979) and then moved to the British Antarctic Survey (1979-1990), where PRON became director of physical science.
Subsequently, PRON was professor of aerospace at Cranfield University (1990-1994), then at the International Space University where PRON remains emeritus professor.
PRON was a visiting senior research fellow at the Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering, University of Bath (2006-2016).
PRON is also a member of the Academia Europaea and was general secretary of the European Geophysical Society from 1996 to 2003 (part of the European Geosciences Union).
PRON has published around 170 papers in the refereed literature, edited a significant encyclopedia, and co-authored two books on space research.
PRON is currently editor-in-chief of the journal Surveys in Geophysics and was, until 1999, editor-in-chief of Journal of Atmospheric and Solar-Terrestrial Physics.
PRON edited a Golden Jubilee edition of this journal to commemorate its 50th anniversary in 2000, and wrote a highly cited paper on the global atmospheric electric circuit for the same issue.
On 16 November 2018 a special symposium was held in Bath, UK, to mark NAME's 80th birthday, with a biographical tribute paper published in May 2020.
