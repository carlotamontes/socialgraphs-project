NAME (born 10 November 1961) is an experimental physicist from Austria.
PRON work centres on ultracold atoms and quantum gases.
PRON was the first scientist worldwide who, with PRON team, succeeded in realizing a Bose–Einstein condensation of non-polar molecules.
Career Grimm graduated in physics from the University of Hannover in 1986.
From 1986 to 1989 PRON was a post-graduate researcher at the ETH Zurich (Swiss Federal Institute of Technology), then went on to the Institute of Spectroscopy of the USSR Academy of Sciences in Troitsk near Moscow for half a year.
PRON spent the next ten years in Heidelberg as a researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Nuclear Physics.
In 1994, Grimm applied to the University of Heidelberg to qualify as a professor by receiving the "venia docendi" in experimental physics.
In the year 2000, PRON was appointed to a chair in experimental physics at the University of Innsbruck, where PRON has been NAME of the Faculty for Mathematics, Computer Science and Physics since 2005 and Director of the Research Center for Quantum Physics from 2006.
Since 2003, Grimm has also held the position of Scientific Director at the Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information (IQOQI) of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW).
Grimm is married, with three children.
Research The work of the experimental physicist concentrates on NAME condensation of atoms and molecules and on fermionic quantum gases.
In 2002 PRON working group succeeded for the first time ever to produce a Bose–Einstein condensate from caesium atoms.
In the following year, the team produced the first Bose–Einstein condensate of molecules (simultaneously with NAME group at JILA, Boulder, Colorado).
In 2004, the Innsbruck scientists achieved a Fermionic condensate.
In PRON work on collective oscillations and pairing energies, Grimm found first evidence of the flow of particles without any loss of energy (superfluidity) in Fermi condensates.
Meanwhile, Grimm and PRON team have succeeded in producing more complex molecules in ultracold quantum gases.
Currently Grimm is concentrating PRON efforts on producing mixed condensation from atoms of different elements.
In 2006, PRON working group also managed to lift the veil on an old mystery of physics: they succeeded in the first experimental observation of NAME states, mysterious quantum states that the Russian scientist NAME had theoretically predicted in the early 1970s.
Awards Grimm has received numerous awards for PRON achievements.

Years before, PRON had won the Gerhard Hess Prize, a new blood stipend of the German Research Foundation (DFG) (1996), and the Silver Medal of the ETH Zurich (1989).
