NAME (born 17 May 1962) is a Hungarian physicist working in attosecond science.
PRON was a director at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics and a professor of experimental physics at LMU Munich in Germany.
PRON research team generated and measured the first attosecond light pulse and used it for capturing electrons' motion inside atoms, marking the birth of attophysics.
In 2023, jointly with NAME and NAME, PRON was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics.
Since November 2025, PRON has been Chair Professor at Department of Physics at the University of Hong Kong, Pokfulam, Hong Kong.
Academic career  From 1981 until 1985 NAME studied theoretical physics at Eötvös Loránd University and electrical engineering at the Technical University of Budapest in Hungary.
From 1987 to 1991 PRON graduated with a PhD at the Technical University of Vienna, in Austria, and from 1991 to 1993 PRON also did PRON habilitation there.
1996–1998 PRON became associate professor, from 1999 until 2004 professor of electrical engineering at the same institute.
In 2003, PRON was appointed director at the Max Planck Institute for Quantum Optics in Garching, and in 2004 became chair of experimental physics at LMU Munich.
In November 2025, PRON was appointed Chair Professor at Department of Physics at the University of Hong Kong, Pokfulam, Hong Kong.
