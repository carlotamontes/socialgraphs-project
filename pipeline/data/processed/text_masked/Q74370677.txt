NAME (born 11 January 1963) is an Argentine nanoscientist.
A 2007 Guggenheim Fellow, PRON is a researcher at the National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET).
Biography NAME was born on 11 January 1963 in Rosario.
PRON obtained a BS from the National University of Rosario in 1986 and a PhD from the Balseiro Institute and National University of Cuyo in 1992; PRON doctoral dissertation was titled Magnetismo en cupratos superconductores de alta NAME y compuestos relacionados.
In 1993, NAME joined the Laboratoire de Physique des Solides as a National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET) postdoctoral fellow, serving until 1995.
In 1996, PRON moved to the Bariloche Atomic Centre's Magnetic Resonance Laboratory as a CONICET Associate Researcher.
PRON also became an associate professor at the Balseiro Institute in 1998, and directed their master programme in physics from 2002 until 2004.
In 2003, PRON was a visiting professor at the Sabato Institute of Technology.
NAME became a CONICET independent researcher in 2003, and later became a senior researcher with them.
By 2023, PRON had been appointed deputy director of the CONICET/National Atomic Energy Commission Institute of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology.
NAME's academic specialty is in magnetic nanotechnology.
In 2007, PRON was awarded a Guggenheim Fellowship for a "study of spin-polarized transport phenomena in artificial magnetic nanostructures".
PRON and NAME co-authored a book published by the Editorial Académica Española in 2012.
In 2017, PRON coordinated the Argentina chapter of the European Union-funded SPICOLOST project on developing nanostructures to reduce cell phone energy usage.
