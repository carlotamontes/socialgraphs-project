Research interest  Chakraborti research interests have focused on kinetic exchange models of income and wealth distributions.
Kinetic exchange models are a class of multi agent models, inspired from statistical physics and thermodynamics which attempts to explain the universal features present in income and wealth distributions.
NAME, along with NAME, proposed the "Chakraborti-Chakrabarti model" which was able to describe the gamma like distributions of income and wealth by taking saving propensity of agents into consideration.
Another model that NAME proposed, named NAME-sale model attempts to describe mechanisms through which inequality arises in wealth and income.
