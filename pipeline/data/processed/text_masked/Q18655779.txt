NAME (born 23 January, 1938 in Avrillé-les-Ponceaux) is a French chemist specialising in the physico-chemistry of inorganic solids.
Biography  After studying at the NAME high school in Angers and at the faculties of science at University of Rennes and University of Bordeaux, NAME specializes in the physico-chemistry of inorganic solids: oxides of transition metals, electronic properties (magnetism, insulation-to-metal transition) and electrochemistry (materials for energy, membranes, electrodes for SOFC fuel cells in particular), NAME) and in the science of functional materials.
Trainee then research associate at the CNRS from 1960 to 1967 (director of the materials technology dissemination department at the CNRS from 1975 to 1984), PRON was a lecturer at the Faculty of Sciences, University of Bordeaux from 1967 to 1970, then professor at the University of Bordeaux I from 1970 to 1992 (professor emeritus from 2004).
From 1992 to 2002, PRON was a professor at the Institut universitaire de France (of which PRON was a director from 1993 to 1997).
PRON was elected a member of the French Academy of sciences on 16 November 1992.
PRON is also a member of the Academy of Technologies, the French Society of Chemistry, the Academia europaea (1998) and the Leopoldina Academy (Germany) (2000).
Distinctions    Prices  Langevin Prize of the French Academy of sciences (1977)   Decorations   Chevalier of the Légion d'honneur.
Commandeur of the Ordre national du mérite.
PRON was promoted to officier on May 15, 2017, and then obtained the rank of commandeur by decree on November 18, 2017.
