NAME (born 3 October 1943) is a German physicist.
Life and work NAME was born in Erfurt.
After attending school in Erfurt and Leipzig, PRON studied physics at the University of Leipzig, whose member PRON remained in the subsequent years, interrupted by guest stays in Prague, Leningrad, Moscow, Paris and Fredericton/Canada.
PRON doctorate in 1970 with the dissertation "The diffusion of water at 13X zeolites: investigated by the method of nuclear magnetic resonance with the aid of pulsed field gradients" under the supervision of NAME was followed in 1978 by PRON doctorate B (1991 with conversion to habilitation) as well as the appointment to an extraordinary professorship in 1989 and a full professorship for experimental physics / interface physics in 1994.
PRON scientific career was determined by PRON work on the use of NMR spectroscopy to measure molecular diffusion in nanoporous materials (zeolites), which PRON had already begun during PRON doctorate.
After PRON measurements, existing ideas had to be corrected by orders of magnitude, which led to a paradigm shift in the understanding of molecular mass transport in such systems.
The mechanism of two-range diffusion PRON applied became a widely used model for NMR diffusion measurements in complex systems, including their use for magnetic resonance imaging.
PRON monographs Diffusion in Zeolites and Other Microporous Solids (Wiley, 1992, together with NAME) and Diffusion in Nanoporous Materials (together with NAME and NAME) became core literature in sorption research, i.e. in the investigation of the behavior of molecules in interaction with interfaces.
PRON work was recognized with the award of the Gustav Hertz Prize of the GDR Physical Society (1978, together with NAME), NAME of the International Zeolite Association (1986, together with NAME, NAME and NAME) and the Max Planck Research Award (1993, together with NAME).
PRON is the author of over 500 papers in periodicals, author/editor of 10 books.
Together with NAME, PRON initiated the Diffusion Fundamentals conference series, which is devoted to the phenomena of random movement and propagation in their entirety.
The objects considered can thus be of a material or immaterial nature and range from atoms and molecules as the traditional objects of diffusion research to new species in the animal and plant world and new words in our vocabulary.
Since the 6th conference 2015 in Dresden the conference series with the accompanying Diffusion-Fundamentals-Online-Journal has been under the auspices of the Saxon Academy of Sciences and Humanities in Leipzig.
The Dresden conference gave rise to the edition of a book on "Diffusive Spreading in Nature, Technology and Society", for which PRON, together with NAME, NAME and Gero Vogl, was awarded the Literature Prize of the Fonds der NAME in 2019.
In 2020 PRON and NAME, PRON first doctoral student, received the ENI award in the “Advanced Environmental Solutions” category for their work, which led to the development of micro-imaging methods for the observation of diffusive molecular flows in nanoporous materials.
In 2022, NAME was awarded NAME by the Magnetic Resonance Division of the German Chemical Society, in recognition of PRON fundamental contributions to the application of nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy.
In 2005, NAME was awarded the Theodor Litt Prize of the University of Leipzig for PRON merits in teaching.
PRON Sunday lectures at the Faculty of Physics and Earth Sciences with up to 500 listeners had a special impact on the media.
