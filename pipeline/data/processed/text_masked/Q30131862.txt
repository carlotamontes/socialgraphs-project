Three years later, PRON received from University of Paris IX, Dauphine a Ph.

PRON obtained PRON HDR degree from University of Nice Sophia Antipolis in 1991.
Contributions  Deriche has made major contributions to the scientific community, mainly in image processing, computer vision and neuro-imaging.
In 1987, Deriche has developed Deriche Edge Detector which is a low-level, recursively implemented, optimal edge detector based on Canny's edge detector criteria for optimal edge detection.
In 1998, based on PRON works in Computational image processing, Early vision, 3D reconstruction, panoramic photography, image-based modeling, and motion analysis, PRON co-founded with PRON Inria colleagues (among which NAME, T. Papadopoulo and NAME),  Realviz, a start-up specialized in image-based content creation solutions for the film, broadcast, gaming, digital imaging and architecture.
The startup was acquired by NAME in 2008.
Deriche is currently devoting PRON research to explore the Central Nervous System by developing mathematical models and tools to reconstruct the functional and anatomical connections network of the brain.
