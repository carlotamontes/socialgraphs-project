Petr Čársky (also spelled NAME; born 29 May 1942) is a Slovak quantum chemist.
Life Čársky was born on 29 May 1942 in Banská Bystrica.
PRON obtained in 1968 PRON Ph.D. at Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences, Prague.
PRON worked as senior researcher at the Institute of Organic Chemistry and Biochemistry, Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences, Prague (1989–1990).
PRON became in 1990 Theoretical group leader, Jaroslav Heyrovský Institute of Physical Chemistry, Academy of Sciences of the Czech Republic, Prague.
Research  Development of quantum-chemical methods in general.
In particular:  Multireference coupled cluster   Electron-molecule scattering theory and its applications to high resolution electron energy loss spectroscopy   Ab initio calculations on molecules with unusual bonding.
Awards and honors   Fellow of the Czech Learned Society (1994).
International Academy of Quantum Molecular Science (1994)
