NAME (born November 30, 1949) is a Turkish theoretical physicist.
Life and academic career   PRON studied at Ankara Science High School and the Middle East Technical University.
PRON was an associate professor and a Professor of Physics at Middle East Technical University (1984–1987, 1993–2001); professor at Faculty of Science at Ankara University (1987–1993), Leverhulme Visiting Professor at Lancaster University (2000–2001)  and since 2001, PRON is a professor at the department of physics at Koç University.
TÜBİTAK honored PRON with TÜBİTAK Junior Science Price in 1982 and TÜBİTAK Science Prize in 1996.
PRON also was awarded prestigious Turkish prizes for science by Sedat Simavi Trust  in 1989 and METU Mustafa Parlar Foundation Science Award in (1993).
PRON is a member of Turkish Academy of Sciences (TAS) since 1993.
Research interests  PRON research interests are Yang-Mills gauge theories, supersymmetry, supergravity, quaternion and octonion algebras, spin structures, generalised theories of gravity, cosmological solutions, integrable systems and phase space quantisation.
Personal life  PRON is married with two children.
