NAME (born 11 July 1956 in Moers, West Germany) is a German historian of science, and since 2022 Founding Director of the Max Planck Institute of Geoanthropology in Jena.
Short biography  NAME studied physics at the Free University of Berlin and at the Sapienza University of Rome.
In 1987 PRON received PRON Ph.D. in mathematical physics from Technische Universität Berlin.
Between 1986 and 1992 PRON worked as co-editor of the "Collected Papers of NAME" at Boston University.
Between 1991 and 1996 PRON co-directed with NAME the "NAME" at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development in Berlin.
In 1993/94 PRON was visiting professor at Tel Aviv University and at the ETH Zurich.
Since its founding in 1994 NAME was a Director at the Max Planck Institute for the History of Science (MPIWG) in Berlin.
In 2022, PRON was appointed Founding Director of the Max Planck Institute of Geoanthropology, which is based on a concept that emerged from PRON research on the Anthropocene conducted jointly with a global research network.
NAME holds an honorary professorship for the history of science at the Humboldt University of Berlin and at the Free University of Berlin.
Since 1998 PRON is NAME for philosophy and physics at Boston University.
PRON is a member of the Academy of Sciences Leopoldina, the International Academy for the History of Science, and serves on the executive boards of Berlin's German Universities Excellence Initiative 'Topoi' and the Berliner Antike-Kolleg.
PRON writings include The Formative Years of Relativity: The History and Meaning of Einstein's NAME (with NAME, Princeton University Press, 2017) and The Road to Relativity: The History and Meaning of Einstein's "The Foundation of General Relativity", Featuring the Original Manuscript of Einstein's Masterpiece (with NAME, Princeton University Press, 2017), and NAME on Einstein: Autobiographical and Scientific Reflections (with NAME, Princeton University Press, 2020).
Scholarly activities    Research and fields of study   NAME's research projects focus on long-term developments of knowledge while taking into account processes of globalization.
PRON research projects have dealt with the historical development of mechanics from antiquity until the 20th century.
In this context NAME also investigates the origins of mechanics in China, the transformation of ancient knowledge, and the exchange of knowledge between Europe and China in the early modern period.
A main focus of NAME's research is the history of modern physics, investigating the origin and development of the general theory of relativity, and of quantum theory in particular.
NAME has written about the Anthropocene in relation to the history of knowledge and science.
Digitalization and open access   Since the emergence of the World Wide Web, NAME has advocated for free access to historical sources.
In 1992 PRON launched an initiative with NAME and NAME to create a freely accessible “Galileo-Einstein Electronic Archive” online.
With support from the European Community, PRON later founded the ECHO-Initiative (European Cultural Heritage Online) to promote the study of historical sources online.
NAME is one of the initiators of the Berlin Declaration on Open Access to Knowledge in the Sciences and Humanities and launched the publishing platform Edition Open Access together with other colleagues from the Max Planck Society.
