NAME is a German virologist.
Until 2019 PRON was probably best known in connection with PRON work on the Cytomegalovirus.
During 2020 PRON emerged as a much consulted expert-pundit for media commentators keen to discuss the COVID-19 pandemic.
NAME took a robust public position in the campaign against pandemic misinformation:  PRON described the so-called "virus of false information" as "more deadly than the [COVID-19] virus itself".
Early life and education  NAME was born at Neustadt am Rübenberge, a town near Hanover, and attended school in nearby Garbsen.
In 1993 PRON enrolled at the University of Hanover where for a year PRON studied Anglistics and Sociology.
In 1994 PRON transferred to NAME, emerging two years later with a first degree in Biology.
Following a formal 'very good' () commendation in recognition of PRON exam grades NAME moved on again, this time to Berlin, still studying biology.
However, it was from the University of Hanover that in 2004 PRON received PRON doctorate, for which PRON was supervised by NAME.
PRON dissertation concerned the "Functional properties of proteins encoded by the K15 gene of the Kaposi's sarcoma-associated herpesvirus".
As well as the doctorate, this work earned PRON a "summa cum laude" commendation and the doctorates prize awarded by the Hannover Medical School.
In 2007 PRON received a postdoctoral research prize for Virology from the Berlin-based NAME.
Career Supported by a research grant from the Bonn-based DFG, between 2006 and 2010 Brinkmann worked as a postdoctoral researcher with NAME at the Whitehead Institute for Biomedical Research in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
PRON research focus was on Toll-like receptors (TLRs).
In July 2010 NAME took on the leadership of the "Immunotherapy Successor Group" ("Nachwuchsgruppe: Virale Immunmodulation") at the Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research (HZI) in Braunschweig.
PRON has since been involved in several virology and immunology research projects backed by the DFG.
The work PRON undertook between 2005 and 2008 in Massachusetts on TLRs was one of these: the project had as its objective the investigation of Herpesvirales immune escape.
Since 2010 PRON has been co-leader of the  DFG project on Modulation of the Immune Response through the NAME-linked Kaposi's sarcoma, associated with the NAME and the Murines Herpesvirus 68 (BO3).
Since 2018 NAME has held a Level W2 professorship in Virus Genetics at the Genetics Institute of the "Technical University of Braunschweig" (TUB), in which PRON oversees the "Infections and Active Elements" ("Infektionen und Wirkstoffe") research speciality.
Media profile  During the spread of the NAME pandemic NAME became a frequent media presence, principally in Germany.
In May 2020 PRON was co-organiser and a co-signatory, with NAME of the Charité, of an open letter from 100 doctors, nurses and health experts calling for stronger action to be taken by providers of media platforms/services against pandemic misinformation.
Billed, by this time, as "Germany's best known virologist", PRON shared these concerns in a television interview on 7 May 2020, insisting on the importance to virologists that infected people should not use false information as the basis for decisions that could imperil their lives or the lives of others:  "We must ensure that information that has not yet been adequately evaluated by experts does not become widely disseminated".
