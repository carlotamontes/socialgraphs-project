NAME (born 1 December 1947) is a German theoretical physicist who works on the mathematical foundations of quantum field theory.
Biography  Klaus Fredenhagen was born on 1 December 1947 in Celle, a German city in Lower Saxony.
PRON graduated in 1976 from the University of Hamburg under the supervision of NAME and NAME.
In 1985 PRON became a privatdozent and in 1990 a full professor at the second theory institute of the Hamburg University.
Since 2013 PRON has been a professor emeritus and has continued to be active in research.
Scientific career  PRON research interests are algebraic quantum field theory and quantum field theory in curved spacetime.
In 1981 PRON proved the existence of antiparticles in massive quantum field theories without using the CPT-invariance.
In 1990 PRON and NAME made important contributions to the understanding of the Hawking radiation of black holes on a rigorous mathematical footing.
In 1994, together with NAME and NAME, PRON investigated the mathematical foundations of quantum gravity in terms of the quantum structure of spacetime at the Planck scale.
In 1996, together with NAME, PRON started working on the generalization of the Epstein-Glaser renormalization procedure of interacting quantum field theories in curved spacetime using techniques from the microlocal analysis.
PRON is currently working, together with NAME, on a new C*-algebraic approach to interacting quantum field theories.
Honors and awards  In 1987 NAME was awarded the physics prize of the Göttingen Academy of Sciences and in 1997 PRON was Leibniz Professor at the University of Leipzig.
In December 2017 the workshop Quantum Physics meets NAME was held in honor of PRON 70th birthday at the University of Hamburg.
