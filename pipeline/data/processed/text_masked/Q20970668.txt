NAME (born 29 May 1948) is a British materials scientist specialising in polymers and composites.
PRON is Emeritus Professor of Polymer Science and Technology at the Department of Materials and National Graphene Institute at the University of Manchester.
Education  Young was educated at St John's College, Cambridge, where PRON received PRON Master of Arts and Doctor of Philosophy degrees.
Research and career Young has published over 330 research papers which have been cited over 37,000 times, leading to a h-index of 91.
PRON is known for research on the relationships between the structure and mechanical properties of polymers and composites.
A particular focus of PRON work has been the study of how materials such as carbon fibres and spider silk deform at the molecular level.
PRON has also studied carbon-fibre composites, carbon nanotubes and the deformation of graphene — a one-atom thick sheet of carbon.
Among PRON work on polymer-graphene composites, one important result elucidated for the first time the relationship between composite reinforcement and matrix modulus.
In PRON research, Young made a novel use of Raman spectroscopy.
In this technique, laser light is shone onto a material and the wavelength and intensity of the resulting scattered light is measured and analysed.
The changes in the light relate to changes in bond length between the atoms of the molecules in the material when the material is deformed.
PRON has also co-authored the widely used textbook: Introduction to Polymers.
Awards and honours  Young received the 2011 Leslie Holliday Prize and the 2012 NAME from the Institute of Materials, Minerals and Mining, and delivered the Swinburne Lecture in 2013.
PRON was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 2013.
