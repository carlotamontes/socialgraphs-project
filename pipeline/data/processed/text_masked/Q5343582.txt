Research NAME began PRON independent career in late 1975 at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology as an assistant professor, where PRON continued to study blue copper proteins.
In 1981, PRON was promoted to the rank of full professor, and in 1982 PRON moved to Stanford University.
At this point, bioinorganic chemistry became the dominant focus of PRON laboratory.
NAME's research focuses on the spectroscopic study of metal-containing enzymes involved in electron transfers and oxygen activation, and small molecules mimicking the active sites of these enzymes.

PRON is an expert in magnetic circular dichroism spectroscopy.
