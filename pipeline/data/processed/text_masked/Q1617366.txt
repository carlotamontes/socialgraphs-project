Hideo Ohno (; Hideo Ōno; born 18 December 1954, Tokyo) is a Japanese physicist.
PRON is the 22nd president of Tohoku University, succeeding NAME in April 2018.
Biography  NAME received B.S., M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from the University of Tokyo in 1977, 1979 and 1982.
PRON spent one year as a visiting graduate student at Cornell University in 1979.
PRON was a lecturer at the School of Engineering at Hokkaido University from 1982 to 1983, and an associate professor from 1983 to 1994.
PRON was a visiting scientist at IBM T. J. Watson Research Center from 1988 to 1990.
In 1994 NAME was appointed a professor at Tohoku University and a professor at the Research Institute of Electrical Communication (RIEC) from 1995.
In 2004 PRON became the head of the laboratory of Nanoelectronics and Spintronics at Tohoku University.
From 2010 until March 2018, Ohno served as the director of the Center for Spintronics Integrated Systems.
PRON was tipped as a possible candidate to receive a Nobel Prize in 2011, for PRON work in spintronics.
Ohno later in 2021 became an international fellow at the Royal Swedish Academy of Engineering Sciences (IVA).
