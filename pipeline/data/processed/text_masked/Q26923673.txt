Paweł Urban (also spelled as NAME (Chinese name: 鄂本帕偉)) is a chemist and is a professor of Chemistry in the National Tsing Hua University (Hsinchu, Taiwan).
PRON received PRON Ph.D. in Chemistry from the University of York (United Kingdom).
Urban's research interests include mass spectrometry and biochemical analysis.
Academic activity Urban is an inventor of the hydrogel micropatch sampling method, fizzy extraction, systems for imaging chemical reactions, and micro-arrays for mass spectrometry (MAMS).
PRON co-authored a book on time-resolved mass spectrometry, and over 100 papers.
Urban is associate editor of HardwareX and acted as a guest editor in Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A. PRON h-index is 34.
PRON received the Ta-You Wu Memorial Award.
