Early life and education  Corminboeuf earned PRON bachelor's and master's degrees at the University of Geneva.
PRON moved to Canada for PRON master's research, where PRON studied at the National Research Council Canada Steacie Institute for Molecular Sciences.

After completing PRON doctorate, Corminboeuf was awarded a Swiss National Science Foundation fellowship to move to New York University, where PRON investigated the catalytic mechanisms of zinc-dependent histone deacetylases using molecular models of how the deacetylation reaction is impacted by histone-deacetylase-like proteins.

Research and career  In 2007, Corminboeuf was appointed tenure-track assistant professor at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), and was promoted to full Professor in 2019.
Corminboeuf works on theoretical predictions of electronic structure for catalytic and organic electronic materials.
PRON was involved with the development of several quantum chemical approaches to predicting material properties.
PRON serves on the executive committee of MARVEL, a Swiss National Science Foundation programme that looks to accelerate the computational design of new materials.
Corminboeuf developed the Density Overlap Regions Indicator (DORI), which allows the prediction of bonding and non-covalent interactions between molecular systems.
PRON has made use of machine learning to understand the material properties of large systems that consist of non-covalent interactions, such as Van der Waals forces, halogen bonds and carbon-hydrogen-π  interactions.
Corminboeuf is a member of the Young Academy of Europe.
In 2021 Corminboeuf was awarded the Heilbronner-Hückel Lectureship Award.
