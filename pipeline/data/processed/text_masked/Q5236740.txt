NAME (born 27 August 1956) is a Northern Irish chemist, and has been Coulson Professor of Theoretical Chemistry at the University of Oxford since 2005.
Early life PRON went to Gilnahirk Primary School in east Belfast.
PRON attended the grammar school Sullivan Upper School in Holywood, County Down.
Originally reading Philosophy at Trinity College, Cambridge PRON quickly changed to the Natural Science Tripos, gaining a BA in 1978, and a PhD in 1982 in Theoretical Chemistry.
From 1982-86 PRON was a Junior Research Fellow at Christ's College, Cambridge.
Career  University of Oxford From 1986 PRON worked at the University of Oxford.
From 1986-2005 PRON was Waters Fellow at Balliol College, Oxford.
From 1996-2005 PRON had the Title of Distinction of professor of chemistry.
In 1996 PRON spent time at the Theory Division of the Institut Laue–Langevin in Grenoble.
From the Royal Society of Chemistry PRON was awarded the Marlow Medal and Prize in 1990.
PRON is based in the Physical and Theoretical Chemistry Laboratory on South Parks Road in Oxford and is responsible for the Theoretical Chemistry Group.
PRON is a professorial fellow of University College, Oxford.
