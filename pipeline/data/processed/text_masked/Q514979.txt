NAME (born February 15, 1951, in Andrychów, Poland) is a Polish process engineer and the Head of Laboratory of Fluid Separations at the Department of Biochemical and Chemical Engineering at the TU Dortmund University.
Career  Professor NAME studied chemistry at the Technical University of Lodz in Poland.
PRON obtained PRON Ph.D. from the Faculty of Process Engineering in 1979.
PRON thesis treated continuous distillation of multi-component mixtures.
PRON worked at the same faculty as senior researcher until 1988.
The following four years PRON spent as a researcher at Henkel KGaA in Düsseldorf.
Having completed PRON “habilitation” at RWTH Aachen in 1989 and at Technical University Warsaw in 1990, NAME became professor at the chair of fluid separation processes at  TU Dortmund University in 1992.
In 1996 PRON took over the chair of fluid separation processes at the University of Essen.
Four years later, in 2000, PRON returned to TU Dortmund University to become the head of Laboratory of Fluid Separations, former chair of fluid separation processes.
Apart from this, PRON has been full professor at the Technical University of Lodz since 2003.
Since 2009 PRON has been decan of the Faculty of Biochemical and Chemical Engineering until the University Council of TU Dortmund University elected PRON as prorector for research in April 2011.
PRON kept this position until January 2014.
Between 2010 and 2012 PRON was member of Council of National Centre for Research and Development, appointed by Polish minister of science.
The scientific activities of professor NAME are focused on the computer aided simulation and experimental validation of integrated reaction and separation processes, like reactive distillation and reactive absorption as well as on the analysis of hybrid separation processes.
Professor NAME is an editor of the journal "Chemical Engineering and Processing: Process Intensification“.
In 2010 PRON was awarded the Federal Cross of Merit on ribbon (Verdienstkreuz am Bande) of the Federal Republic of Germany on 29 June 2010 for PRON contributions to international understanding between Poland and Germany.
In 2013 President of Republic Poland awarded PRON with NAME of Merit of the Republic of Poland.
In 2014 PRON was also awarded by DECHEMA and NAME Ingenieure with Kirschbaum-Medaille for PRON outstanding achievements in fluid separations.
PRON is also editor of the series of three books on distillation (2014), which are the most comprehensive review on this technology ever published.
In 2018 professor NAME received the North Rhine-Westphalia order of merit in recognition of PRON social commitment.
