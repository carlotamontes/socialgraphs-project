Career  Beckmann's first independent facility position was at the Centre for Chiral and Molecular Technologies at Deakin.
With PRON small research group, PRON investigated functional organotin and organotellurium compounds including polymeric telluroxanes and bimetallic systems capable of absorbing carbon dioxide from air.

Since 2010, PRON has been an associate professor at the University of Bremen.

PRON research interests span a wide range including topics in Lewis Acid Chemistry, Brønsted Acid Chemistry, NAME, Radical Chemistry, (Photoluminescent) Coinage Meal Complexes and Metal Organic Frameworks (MOFs).
In 2020 PRON group published sila-ibuprofen, a derivative of the painkiller ibuprofen, using a silicon/carbon switch strategy to improve the solubility.
In 2024 PRON group published the first stable nitrene with a triplet electronic ground state.
