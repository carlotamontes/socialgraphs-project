PRON is known for PRON contributions to the theory of turbulent transport in plasmas, and in particular the derivation of the Hasegawa–Mima equation in 1977, which won PRON the 2011 Hannes Alfvén Prize.
Life and career  Mima studied physics at Kyoto University and graduated with a bachelor's degree in 1968 and a PhD in 1973.
PRON was a post-doctoral student at Hiroshima University until 1975 and then started work at Osaka University, where PRON became an assistant professor in 1978 and a professor in 1984.
From 1995 to 1999, PRON was director of the Institute of Laser Engineering.
There, PRON work involved laser fusion (experiments with the Gekko XII laser and FIREX program), free electron lasers, relativistic plasmas and laser-plasma interaction.
Mima died on 5 January 2025, at the age of 79.
Honors and awards  Mima was a Fellow of the American Physical Society and a Member of the Physical Society of Japan and the Japan Society of Plasma Science at Nuclear Fusion Research.
PRON was a co-recipient of the 1993 NAME in Plasma Physics Research and won the 2007 Edward Teller Award.
