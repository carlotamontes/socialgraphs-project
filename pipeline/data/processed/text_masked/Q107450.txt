Asymptotic freedom In 1973, while a graduate student working with NAME at Princeton University, Wilczek (together with NAME) discovered asymptotic freedom, which holds that the closer quarks are to each other, the less the strong interaction (or color charge) between them; when quarks are in extreme proximity, the nuclear force between them is so weak that they behave almost as free particles.
