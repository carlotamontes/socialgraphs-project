Research As a student attending the University of Arizona, NAME worked in the Spacewatch project; PRON discoveries through the project included multiple minor planets as well as C/2003 NAME (Gleason), a comet.
As of 2022, it still had the most distant perihelion (the closest point in its orbit to the sun) of any known long-period comet.
PRON research on materials in extreme environments has included the unexpected discovery of weaknesses and non-uniform structure in the solid iron and nickel of Earth's inner core.
PRON has also found ways to create ice VII and superionic ice, exotic forms of water, by using intense laser light to vaporize a target sandwiching water between thin diamond crystals.
PRON served as principal investigator of an experiment at the SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory that investigated the behavior of silicon under extreme shock compression.
Using ultrafast X-ray pulses from the Linac Coherent Light Source, the research team observed how a single-crystal silicon sample deformed at nanosecond timescales.
The findings helped clarify the mechanisms through which silicon responds to extreme pressure.
