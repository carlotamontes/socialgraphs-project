NAME (; born August 1, 1962) is a South Korean organic chemist.
PRON is a distinguished professor in the Department of Chemistry at Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST).
PRON is also the director of the Institute for Basic Science (IBS) Center for Catalytic Hydrocarbon Functionalizations (CCHF).
PRON is the fourth president of IBS and the first to serve in the role after first being a principal investigator at that institute.
PRON was an associate editor on ACS Catalysis and has served on the editorial advisory boards of The Journal of Organic Chemistry, Journal of the American Chemical Society, and Accounts of Chemical Research.
PRON major research interest is transition metal catalyzed C-H bond functionalization for the carbon-carbon bond and carbon-heteroatom bond formation.
Career  NAME received PRON B.S degree from Korea University in 1985, and M.S degree from KAIST in 1987.
Then, PRON joined NAME group and received PRON PhD in 1996 at Harvard University.
PRON subsequently worked with NAME at Caltech as a postdoctoral fellow from 1996 to 1998.
In early 1998, PRON joined the faculty of Ewha Womans University as an assistant professor, and moved to KAIST as a full professor in 2002.
In 2012, PRON was selected as a director of the Center for Catalytic Hydrocarbon Functionalizations at the Institute for Basic Science, which is the biggest Korean government funded research institute.
PRON also has been working as an associate editor of the journal ACS Catalysis since 2015.
In 2023, PRON was selected to co-run the KAIST Cross Generation Creation Lab, a laboratory designed to continue the know-how of professors about to retire through collaboration with younger professors.
In mid 2026, PRON was appointed president of the Institute for Basic Science where PRON will serve a five-year term.
Major contributions  NAME's group studies new organic reactions and mechanisms with transition metal catalysis.
In particular, PRON group contributed to the development of "copper catalyzed multicomponent coupling" in the 2000s.
Since 2008, PRON group has focused on C-H functionalization and made a number of contributions.
Copper-catalyzed multicomponent coupling   Cu-catalyzed multicomponent coupling is a notable process developed by NAME's group.
In 2005, they published a highly efficient and mild catalytic three component coupling between an alkyne, sulfonyl azide, and amine.
Unlike click chemistry which generates 1,4-triazoles as products, in this case a Cu(I) catalyst, sulfonyl azide and alkyne generate ketenimine intermediate after releasing N2 gas.
This electrophilic ketenimine intermediate reacts with amines and to generate asymmetric imines as products.
NAME's group also showed water, alcohols, the C3 position of pyrrole and other nucleophiles can be used in this reaction.
Rhodium, Iridium-catalyzed C-N bond formation  460x460px  Rhodium or iridium catalyzed C-H amidation and amination are other achievements of PRON group.
In 2012, PRON group published rhodium catalyzed intermolecular amidation of arenes using sulfonyl azide as a nitrene precursor.
This reaction generates N2 as the single byproduct, doesn't need external oxidant, has broad substrate scope and high functional group tolerance.
NAME's group advanced their work by using different directing groups, different azides and various substrates.
They also published that iridium also works well for C-H amidation/amination.
In 2016, NAME's group discovered new nitrogen sources.
Their new nitrene precursor, 1,4,2-dioxazol-5-one, is more convenient to prepare, store and use compared to azides.
Moreover, it has a strong affinity to the rhodium or Iridium metal center, and thus gives excellent amidation efficiency.
They later published selective formation of gamma-lactams via C-H amidation with this type of nucleophile.
