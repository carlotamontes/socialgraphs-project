NAME (born 12 February 1968 in Bonn) is a German physicist.
PRON is a professor of physical chemistry at the Ruhr University Bochum.
Education   PRON studied physics and computer science at the universities of Bonn and Knoxville.
PRON was awarded a diploma in physics in 1993 (Forschungszentrum Jülich and University of Bonn), and a diploma in computer science in 1994 (GMD Forschungszentrum Informationstechnik and University of Bonn).
PRON then   obtained a doctoral degree in surface physics in 1996 (Forschungszentrum Jülich and University of Bonn) and completed PRON habilitation in experimental physics in 2002 (Free University of Berlin).
Career  Morgenstern  was a researcher at the University of Aarhus in 1996, then at the University of Lausanne from 1996 to 1999 and at the Free University of Berlin from 1999 to 2003.
PRON then became a professor of solid state physics at the University of Hannover in 2005 and from 2012 PRON was a professor of physical chemistry at the Ruhr University Bochum.
Since 2018, PRON has been the director of the DFG graduate school "Confinement-controlled Chemistry" and the dean of the department of chemistry and biochemistry.
