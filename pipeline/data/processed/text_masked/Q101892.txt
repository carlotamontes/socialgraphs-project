Uwe Meierhenrich (born 23 October 1967 in Detmold) is a German Physico-Chemist.
PRON is a professor of Analytical and Physical Chemistry at the University of Nice Sophia Antipolis in France.
Academic life  NAME was raised in a family of teachers and professors.
PRON studied chemistry at the Philipps-University Marburg and obtained a Ph.D. degree in physical chemistry at the University of Bremen by Thiemann.
PRON did postdoctoral work at the Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research in Katlenburg-Lindau and at the French Synchrotron Center LURE.
In 2003 PRON earned PRON habilitation with the publication of The Origin of Biomolecular Asymmetry at the University of Bremen.
In 2005 PRON was hired as a professor at the University of Nice Sophia Antipolis in France.
For PRON work on chirality, PRON was awarded the Horst-Pracejus-Prize of the Gesellschaft Deutscher Chemiker (German Chemical Society) in 2011.
Career  NAME was hired by the European Space Agency to develop instrumentation for its Rosetta mission to 67P/Churyumov–NAME.
The purpose of the instrumentation was to detect organic compounds on the comet's surface.
To test it on Earth, PRON worked with scientists at Leiden University in the Netherlands who were making artificial cometary ice.
They replicated the conditions in space, creating a cold vacuum and bombarding the ice with ultraviolet light.
They succeeded in synthesizing 16 amino acids.
When the Rosetta spacecraft reached 67P in 2014, it detected similar organic compounds in the comet.
In further experiments at PRON lab in Nice, PRON analyzed cometary ice analogs with multidimensional gas chromatography and detected ribose, one of the sugars that make up DNA.
Further experiments at the French SOLEIL synchrotron showed that life's homochirality can also originate under interstellar conditions.
