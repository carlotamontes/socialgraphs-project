NAME (born December 23, 1944) is an American pharmacologist and medicinal chemist.
Previously the Robert C. and NAME in Pharmacology at Purdue University, NAME has worked in the field of psychoactive drugs since 1969.
While still a graduate student, PRON patented the method that is used to make the optical isomers of psychedelic amphetamines.
PRON contributions include the synthesis and reporting of escaline, LSZ, 6-APB, 2C-I-NBOMe and other NBOMe variants (NBOMe-2C-B, NBOMe-2C-C, NBOMe-2C-D), and several others, as well as the coining of the term "entactogen".
PRON is the founding president of the Heffter Research Institute, named after German chemist and pharmacologist NAME, who first discovered that mescaline was the active component in the peyote cactus.
In 2004 PRON was named the Irwin H. Page Lecturer by the former International Serotonin Club (now the International Society for Serotonin Research), and delivered an address in Portugal titled, "35 years studying psychedelics: what a long strange trip it's been".
Among pharmacologists, PRON is considered to be one of the world's top experts on psychedelics.
PRON has also been described as the "greatest living psychedelic chemist" or "greatest living psychedelic scientist" by NAME and others in the 2020s.
NAME's other professional activities include teaching medicinal chemistry and molecular pharmacology at Purdue University in West Lafayette, IN, and teaching medical students at the Indiana University School of Medicine.
PRON officially retired in 2012 but has continued to work in the field.
As of 2019, NAME was an adjunct professor at the UNC Eshelman School of Pharmacy, Chapel Hill, NC.
Education   BS – University of Cincinnati (1969)  PhD – University of Iowa (1973)  Postdoctoral researcher – University of Iowa (1973–74)   Research areas  NAME is still carrying out academic research on the chemistry of psychedelics.
PRON has published approximately 250 scientific reports and book chapters, all describing the relationship between the structure of a molecule and its biological effects (often referred to as a Structure-activity relationship, or SAR).
PRON has done extensive work using the rat model assay of drug discrimination in characterizing hallucinogenic drugs.
Although PRON research mostly uses rats, a number of compounds included in Shulgin's PIHKAL were actually first synthesized in NAME's lab.
PRON lab also first developed [125I]-(R)-DOI as a radioligand.
NAME is one of the few people who has published legitimate research on the chemistry and pharmacology of LSD in the last 20 years, and first reported that several LSD analogues, including ETH-LAD, PRO-LAD, and AL-LAD, were more potent than LSD itself.
Their human effects are described in TiHKAL.
PRON also improved the synthesis of psilocybin so that it could be accessible for several recent clinical studies.
Other notable research that PRON helped carry out includes extensive studies of the structure-activity relationships and mechanisms of action of MDA and MDMA, during which PRON helped to discover many novel analogues including such compounds as 5-methyl-MDA, 4-MTA and MDAI.
NAME has said that PRON believes gray-market chemists used information from papers PRON published on 4-methylthioamphetamine (MTA) in the 1990s to synthesize the drug, which they sold in tablets nicknamed "flatliners" as a substitute for MDMA (NAME).
More recently, NAME has become one of the world leaders in research on dopamine, and PRON team has developed several notable dopamine receptor ligands, including the selective D1 full agonist compounds dihydrexidine and dinapsoline which have been researched for the treatment of Parkinson's disease, as well as a number of other subtype-selective dopamine agonists derived from dinoxyline.
PRON co-founded DarPharma, Inc. to commercialize PRON dopamine compounds; several of PRON team's compounds are now being studied in clinical trials for the treatment of Parkinson's disease and the cognitive and memory deficits of schizophrenia.
Impact on the designer drug market  Designer drug producers who scan scientific literature for information on compounds with potential grey market value have described NAME' publications as an "especially valuable" road map to making new designer drugs.
Several deaths have been attributed to compounds that have been discovered in NAME' lab, which PRON finds quite upsetting, "I was stunned by this revelation, and it left me with a hollow and depressed feeling for some time."
Personal life  NAME has disclosed that PRON is writing three books as of the 2020s.
These books include a memoir, a collection of PRON letters with NAME, and a science fiction novel.
