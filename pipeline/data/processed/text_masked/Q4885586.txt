NAME, , (born 6 November 1970) is Professor in the School of Physics at the University of Sydney, where PRON leads a research group in integrated photonics, nonlinear optics and smart sensors and serves as co-director of the NSW Smart Sensing Network (NSSN).
Education and career Eggleton obtained the bachelor's degree (with honours) in Science in 1992 and PhD degree in physics from the University of Sydney in 1996.
In 1996, PRON joined Bell Laboratories, Lucent Technologies as a Postdoctoral Member of Staff in the Optical Physics Department under the supervision of Dr NAME.
In 1998 PRON transferred to the Optical Fiber Research Department as a Member of Technical Staff and was promoted to Technical Manager of the Fiber Gratings Group in 2000.
PRON was then promoted to Research Director within the Specialty Fiber Business Division of Bell Laboratories, where PRON was engaged in forward-looking research supporting Lucent Technologies business in optical fiber devices.
NAME returned to the University of Sydney in 2003 as a full Professor and ARC Federation Fellowship in the School of Physics.
PRON was founding Director of the ARC Centre of Excellence for Ultrahigh bandwidth Devices for Optical Systems (CUDOS) and served as Director from 2003 to 2017.
In 2009 Eggleton established the Institute of Photonics and Optical Science (IPOS) at the University of Sydney and was the Director from 2009 to 2018.
PRON was an ARC Laureate Fellow and an ARC Federation Fellowship twice.

Research Eggleton's research links fundamental to applied science and spans physics and engineering.
PRON has made pioneering contributions to nonlinear optics and all-optical signal processing with recent breakthrough achievements in the nonlinear optics of periodic media, slow-light in photonic crystals and ultrafast planar waveguide nonlinear optics.
PRON research into new classes of nonlinear waveguides has created a new paradigm for photonic chip based ultrafast optical signal processing and PRON group holds various world records.
Eggleton's breakthroughs in the nonlinear optics of chalcogenide glasses have led to PRON demonstrations of new ultrafast optical devices for telecommunications applications, record low-threshold supercontinuum generation sources and on-chip parametric sources.
PRON group reported the first demonstration of on-chip stimulated Brillouin scattering, and holds the record for on-chip SBS gain.
PRON fundamental breakthroughs include the first demonstrations of NAME soliton and Gap soliton formation in periodic media and of slow-light-enhanced nonlinear optics in photonic crystals.
