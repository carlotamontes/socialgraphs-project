NAME (; born January 15, 1955) is a South Korean organic chemist.
Biography  Song was born and raised in Seoul.
PRON received PRON B.S. degree from Chung-Ang University in 1980, and received a diploma (1985) and a Ph.D. (1988) at RWTH Aachen University in Germany.
After completing PRON Ph.D., PRON worked as Principal Research Scientist at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST).
In 2001, PRON was appointed as a head of the National Research Laboratory for Green Chirotechnology in Korea.
In 2004, PRON moved to Sungkyunkwan University (Department of Chemistry) as a full professor.
In 2005, PRON was elected as the vice-president of Korean Chemical Society.
From 2006 to 2014 PRON worked as a director at the Research Institute of Advanced Nanomaterials (University-centered Lab, Korea Research Foundation).
In 2016 PRON was appointed as a director of National Research Laboratory for Chiral Organic Molecular Materials in Korea.
PRON research has been focused on asymmetric organocatalysis, biomimetic catalysis, artificial enzyme, on-water catalysis and on-droplet catalysis.
PRON current research also focuses on prebiotic chemistry, specifically, the origin of homochirality (chirality amplification process).
Honors and awards   2000: Scientist of the Month Award, Korea Institute of Science and Technology 2002: Scientist of the Month, Ministry of Science and Technology  2013: Academic Excellence Grand Prize, Korean Chemical Society 2018: Member, Korean Academy of Science and Technology
