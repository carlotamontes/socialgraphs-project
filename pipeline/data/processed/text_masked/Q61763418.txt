Dominique Franck Escande (born 1948) is a French physicist.
PRON is known for PRON research in foundations and methods of plasma physics, wave-particle interactions in plasmas, Hamiltonian dynamics, deterministic chaos, thermonuclear fusion by magnetic confinement, self-organization of the reversed field pinch, plasma-wall self-organization in magnetic fusion  Education and career After graduating in physics in 1967 from the École polytechnique, Dominique Franck Escande became a graduate student in physics at Paris-Sud University.
There PRON graduated in 1971 with a  (DEA) and a Ph.D. in 1978.
PRON Ph.D. thesis is entitled Ondes haute fréquence dans un plasma en présence de fluctuation de basse fréquence (High-frequency waves in a plasma in the presence of low-frequency fluctuation).
From 1981 to 1992 PRON was a CNRS researcher at the École polytechnique, where PRON held an appointment as maître de conférences (assistant professor) in physics.
During the years of PRON appointment at the École polytechnique, PRON also worked at several other scientific institutions.
For the academic year 1983–1984 PRON was on sabbatical at the University of Texas at Austin's Institute for Fusion Studies (IFS) (which, among other things, was the principal site for USA-Japan collaboration on theory in fusion research).
From 1987 to 1992 PRON was a part-time consultant for X-Recherche Service.
In 1988 PRON created, in collaboration with NAME, the research team Equipe Turbulence Plasma inside laboratory Physique des Intéractions Ioniques et Moléculaires (PIIM) at Université de Provence in Marseille.
From 1988 to 1992 Escande held an appointment as CNRS researcher in this laboratory.
Escande was from 1992 to 1996 the head of the Département de Recherches sur la Fusion Contrôlée at CEA-Cadarache, whose activity was centered on the Tore Supra tokamak group.
From 1995 to 1996 PRON chaired the Euratom Fusion Technology Steering Committee-Implementation.
At  (Consortium RFX) in Padua, Italy, PRON was from 1996 to 1998 a full-time advisor and is since 1998 a part-time advisor.
In 1998 PRON became a CNRS researcher at the laboratory PIIM, where PRON now has emeritus status as Directeur de Recherche Emérite.
PRON contributed three book chapters.
PRON is the co-author with NAME (a physics professor at the University of Provence) of the book Microscopic dynamics of plasmas and chaos (2003, IOP Publishing); with a 2019 paperback edition published by CRC Press.
Escande is author or co-author of many scientific articles related to the device called the reverse field pinch (RFP)  and is an internationally recognized expert on RFPs.
More recently, PRON worked on plasma-wall self-organization.
PRON former doctoral student NAME has acquired an international reputation in fusion research and plasma physics.
In 1994 Escande was awarded NAME.
