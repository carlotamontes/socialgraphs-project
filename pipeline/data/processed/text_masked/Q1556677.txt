NAME (born 2 January 1947) is a German chemist working in the fields of polymer chemistry, supramolecular chemistry and nanotechnology.
PRON is known for the synthesis and exploration of the properties of graphene-like nanostructures and their potential applications in organic electronics.
Early life and education  NAME was born in Cologne.
PRON studied chemistry there and was awarded a PhD in 1971 under the supervision of NAME at the University of Basel.
PRON specialized in electron spin resonance (ESR) spectroscopy and PRON dissertation was on nuclear resonance and electron spin resonance spectroscopic studies on bridged annulenes.
PRON completed post-doctoral studies at the ETH Zurich under NAME (1926–2003) and received PRON habilitation in 1977 with a thesis on dynamic NMR spectroscopy and electrochemistry.
Research career  In 1979, PRON became professor of organic chemistry at the University of Cologne, and in 1983, PRON went to the University of Mainz as a professor.
Since 1989, PRON has been director and scientific member of the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research.
In 2016, PRON became emeritus.
Since 1995, PRON has been honorary professor at the University of Mainz and at the Gutenberg Research College of the University of Mainz.
PRON research interests are in the field of preparative NAME and supramolecular chemistry.
Among other things, PRON group has succeeded in synthesizing and characterizing hitherto unattainable large polycyclic aromatics such as superphenalene, which has a molecular mass of 1182 g·mol−1 and consists of 34 condensed benzene rings.
PRON has developed small disc-like organic building blocks using alkyl-substituted hexabenzocoronene, and in particular HBC-C12 – which self-assembles into crystalline liquid-phase structures (columnar liquid crystals) as potential organic field-effect transistors.
The considered two-dimensional benzene ring structures are examples of subunits of graphene lattices (graphene nanostructures).
The graphene-like structures synthesized and investigated by NAME include two-dimensional bands of less than 50 nanometers width with jagged edges.
Of interest here are the electronic conduction properties and spintronics properties with a view to future replacement of silicon-semiconductor technology.
In synthesis, PRON introduced a new method in graphene polymer chemistry: soft-landing mass spectrometry.
Applications include synthetic light-emitting organic materials (such as OLEDs) and incorporation of molecular defects (defect engineering) organic analogues of semiconductor technology.
