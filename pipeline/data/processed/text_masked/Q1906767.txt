NAME (born 17 September 1952) is a Japanese researcher in optical communication engineering.
PRON is a distinguished professor at Tohoku University in Japan.
PRON pioneering work on erbium-doped fiber amplifier (EDFA) has made a significant contribution to the development of global long-distance, high-capacity optical fiber network.
Biography  Masataka Nakazawa received B.S. in Electronics from Kanazawa University in 1975, M.S. in Physical Electronics from Tokyo Institute of Technology in 1977, and Ph.D. in Applied Electronics from Tokyo Institute of Technology in 1980.
After receiving a Ph.
D. degree, PRON joined the Electrical Communication Laboratory of Nippon Telegraph & Telephone Public Corporation in 1980.
PRON was a visiting scientist at Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 1984.
In 1999, PRON became an NTT R&D Fellow.
Then, in 2001, PRON moved to the Research Institute of Electrical Communication (RIEC) at Tohoku University.
PRON became a distinguished professor (DP) in 2008 and the director of RIEC in 2010.
PRON also served as the director of Japan Council for Research Institutes and Centers of National Universities and of Research Organization of Electrical Communication (ROEC) in 2011.
Currently, PRON is a director of Kanazawa University (part time) and a specially appointed professor/ distinguished professor at the International Research Institute of Disaster Science at Tohoku University   Research  PRON introduced erbium ions into optical communication in 1984, when PRON constructed the first erbium (Er3+): glass laser operating at 1.55 μm, and then used it as an optical time domain reflectometer (OTDR).
This enabled a fault to be located in a 130 km-long single-mode fiber, which remains the world record distance.
PRON then began research on an erbium-doped fiber laser in 1987 and amplifier in 1989.
After Dr. NAME of Prof. NAME group reported the first EDFA in 1987, Dr. NAME used a 1.48 μm InGaAsP laser diode (LD) to pump the erbium fiber and reported the highest gain of 46.5 dB in 1989 after employing the LD for Raman amplification at 1.55 μm in 1988.
PRON invented the LD pumped erbium-doped fiber amplifier (EDFA), which made it possible to construct a compact, reliable, and low-power consumption optical repeater for high-speed, high-capacity, and long-distance optical communication systems.
PRON also reported backward Raman amplification in 1984, which remains in commercial use.
PRON subsequently undertook intensive work on high-speed optical transmission technology using ultrashort NAME pulses, optical solitons, optical NAME transformation, and Nyquist pulses.
Nakazawa’s work spans diverse areas of photonics including optical communication, various fiber lasers, and quadrature amplitude modulation (QAM) coherent transmission with the highest multiplicity of 4096.
Recently, PRON has been concentrating on NAME locking technology for the generation of various optical pulses and a QAM quantum noise stream cipher with continuous variable quantum key distribution (QKD).
PRON has published more than 500 academic journal papers and given 400 international conference presentations.
