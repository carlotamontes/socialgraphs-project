NAME (born 1945) is an American chemist.
PRON is currently NAME and Lillie Cranz Cullen University Professor at the University of Houston.
Career  Dr. NAME received PRON B.S. from the University of Michigan in 1967 and PRON PhD from Pennsylvania State University in 1970.
After completing a postdoctoral fellowship at Louisiana State University, New Orleans (LSUNO), PRON spent one year as Chargé de Research at the University of Paris VI in France.
PRON was an assistant professor at California State University, Fullerton from 1972 to 1976 and has been at the University of Houston since 1976, where PRON holds the position of NAME and Lillie Cranz Cullen University Professor in the Department of Chemistry.
Dr. NAME has also held visiting professorships at Osaka University (Japan), the University of Sydney (Australia), the California Institute of Technology, the University of Rome, the University of Dijon, (France), Ecole Superieure de Chimie Industrielle de Lyon (ESCIL)(France) and Université Louis Pasteur (Strasbourg, France)  PRON has published over 600 research papers, the majority of which are on topics related to porphyrins, phthalocyanines, corroles and related macrocycles, while also editing more than 95 books on these topics and directing a research group, which, in total, has numbered over 125 different graduate students and postdoctoral associates.
PRON is the founder and first president of the Fullerenes Division of the Electrochemical Society and since 1994 has organized or co-organized more than 25 symposia on the topic of fullerenes and carbon nanomaterials.
PRON was a co-organizer of the first (2000), third (2004), sixth (2010), ninth (2016) and eleventh (2020) International Conference on Porphyrins and Phthalocyanines.
Dr. NAME is editor-in-chief of the Journal of Porphyrins and Phthalocyanines and also serves as president of the Society of Porphyrins and Phthalocyanines (SPP), a position PRON has held continuously since June 2000.
