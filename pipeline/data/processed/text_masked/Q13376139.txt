NAME (born 11 March 1953) is a German physicist and mathematician.
PRON is senior advisor in the research department Complexity Sciences of the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research, a professor of nonlinear dynamics at the Institute of Physics at the Humboldt University, Berlin, and a 6th-century chair for complex systems biology at the Institute for Complex Systems and Mathematical Biology at Kings College, Aberdeen University (UK).
PRON research is mainly concerned with nonlinear physics and complex systems sciences and their applications to challenging problems in Earth system, physiology, systems biology and engineering.
Biography NAME studied mathematics at the University of Rostock and was awarded PRON PhD in 1983 at the GDR Academy of Sciences, followed by PRON installation in 1991 in theoretical physics at the University of Rostock.
In 1991, in a special program of the Max-Planck-Society, PRON was selected as one of a few scientists from East Germany to become a director of a new working group and PRON has formed an internationally well-known group on nonlinear dynamics.
In 1994 PRON got a full-chair on theoretical physics/nonlinear dynamics at the University of Potsdam.
There PRON was also NAME of the Science Faculty (1996–1999), and established as the founding director the Interdisciplinary Centre for Dynamics of Complex Systems (1994–2008).
PRON was the founding director of the Leibniz-Kolleg Potsdam.
In 2008, PRON was called to re-create the research domain NAME at PIK and to bring complex systems perspectives into Earth system research and has become a Professor of Nonlinear Dynamics at the Institute of Physics at the Humboldt University, Berlin, and in 2009 a 6th-century chair for Complex Systems Biology at the Institute for Complex Systems and Mathematical Biology at Kings College, Aberdeen University.
Since 2021, PRON is senior advisor at the PIK.
Research impact From time series analysis and its application to solar and stellar activity phenomena, PRON interest was drawn in the 1980s to complex systems and nonlinearity or chaos theory.
NAME has been noted especially for PRON seminal contributions to new synchronization phenomena, recurrence, coherence resonance, measures of complexity and causality as well as dynamics and stability of complex networks.
This is what later led PRON to do research on the basics of complex systems theory as well as applications to the Earth system, the human brain, the cardio-respiratory system and other systems which are characterised by a high degree of complexity and nonlinearity.
NAME maintains a large network of scientific collaborators and PRON has had more than 60 PhD students from about 20 countries, 30 of whom now have tenured positions in various countries.
PRON published more 1200 articles (as of October 2022) and eight books.
PRON is currently on the editorial board of more than 10 scientific journals, among them Chaos, Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A, PLOS One, European Journal of Physics, J. Nonlinear Science and Nonlinear Processes in Geophysics and of the Springer Series Complexity.
International scientific activities NAME has been a leader in many international scientific activities, including the presidency of the EGU Nonlinear Processes in Geosciences Division (2000–2005).
PRON efforts to promote international collaboration led PRON to organise several large projects in the EU and DFG and to become the speaker of an International Research Training Group on complex networks (DFG and Brazil) since 2011.
Prizes and awards NAME is an elected fellow of the American Physical Society (1999).
PRON received an NAME research award from Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) (India) in 2005.
PRON has become a member of the Academia Europaea in 2010 and of the Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts in 2012 and was bestowed with a Dr. honoris causa from the Lobachevsky University in Nizhny Novgorod in 2008 and one from the Chernishevsky University, Saratov.
PRON is honorary professor at the University of Potsdam and guest professor at the Southeast University in Nanjing.
PRON was awarded the Lewis Fry Richardson Medal of the European Geosciences Union in 2013.
