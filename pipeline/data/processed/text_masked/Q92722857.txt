NAME (6 June 1953 – 10 February 2024) was a German professor in Geosciences at the Institute for Environmental Physics (IUP) of Heidelberg University.
PRON work with atmospheric measurements significantly contributed to the knowledge of greenhouse gas dynamics.
PRON set up a global network that measures radiocarbon in carbon dioxide (), information that can be used to verify bottom-up estimates of  emissions.
PRON uses the concentration data of several chemical species to constrain carbon emissions and help validate global atmospheric models.
Biography  NAME was born in Erlangen, Germany in 1953 and studied physics at Heidelberg University.
PRON completed PRON diploma thesis in 1978, on regional modeling of the atmospheric CO2 based on C-13 and C-14 measurements, followed by PRON PhD thesis in 1984 on atmospheric CO2 sources and sinks across the European Continent.
NAME showed an early interest in finding ways to measure greenhouse gases, especially carbon dioxide, on the regional scale and had several significant accomplishments, according to European Geosciences Union: The measurements obtained through NAME’s efforts are widely used across the geoscience community to characterise associated carbon fluxes and have proven to be extremely important for our understanding of the global carbon cycle.
They have provided key insights in atmospheric and ocean sciences as well as in biogeochemistry and climate science.
In 2020, PRON was the first GENDERWORD to receive the NAME medal from the European Geosciences Union "for fundamental contributions to our present knowledge and understanding of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, including the global carbon cycle."
PRON has been the group leader of the Carbon Cycle group in the Institute for Environmental Physics for more than 20 years, and a lecturer at the Faculty of Physics and Astronomy of Heidelberg University.
Since the 1990s, NAME's group has also measured the concentrations of methane and nitrogen oxides, with the aim of also using them to measure human impact.
NAME died on 10 February 2024.
