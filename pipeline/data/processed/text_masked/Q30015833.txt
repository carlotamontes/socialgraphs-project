PRON then became a staff scientist at the Superconducting Super Collider Laboratory in Dallas, Texas.
In 1994, PRON returned to Oxford as a Research Officer on the NAME proposed experiment, subsequently bringing that experiment to University College London in 1996, and leading the NAME collaboration since 2010.
PRON was instrumental in broadening the range of the experiment to search for the hypothetical sterile neutrinos.

PRON is the co-spokesperson for the MINOS/MINOS+ experiment and is a member of the NEMO-III and SuperNEMO experiments, where PRON researched neutrinoless double beta decay and potential neutrino CP violation.
