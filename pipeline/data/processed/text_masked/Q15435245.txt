NAME (26 June 1967 in Milan) is an Italian mathematician and mathematical physicist, specializing in geometry related to quantum field theory and string theory.
Biography After attending NAME in Milan, Cattaneo studied physics at University of Milan, graduating in 1991.
In 1995, PRON obtained a PhD in theoretical physics at the same university; PRON thesis, entitled Teorie topologiche di tipo BF ed invarianti dei nodi (Topological BF theories and knot invariants), was supervised by NAME.
Cattaneo worked as a postdoc in 1995-1997 at Harvard University (with NAME) and in 1997-1998 at the University of Milan (with NAME).
In 1998, PRON moved to University of Zurich's mathematics department as an assistant professor, and PRON became a full professor in 2003.
In 2006 PRON was an invited speaker, with the talk From topological field theory to deformation quantization and reduction, at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Madrid.
Cattaneo was elected a Fellow of the American Mathematical Society in 2013.
Research  Cattaneo's research interests include deformation quantization, symplectic and Poisson geometry, topological quantum field theories, and the mathematical aspects of perturbative quantization of gauge theories.
With NAME PRON developed a path integral interpretation of the deformation quantization of Poisson manifolds (introduced in 2003 by NAME), as well as a description of the symplectic groupoid integrating a Poisson manifold as an infinite-dimensional symplectic quotient.
PRON supervised 14 PhD students as of 2022.
