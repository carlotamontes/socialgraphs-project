NAME (born 1980) is a Canadian mathematician and mathematical physicist whose research connects combinatorics to quantum field theory.
PRON holds the Canada Research Chair in Combinatorics in Quantum Field Theory at the University of Waterloo.
Biography Yeats is from Halifax, Nova Scotia.
As an undergraduate at the University of Waterloo, PRON won an honourable mention for the 2003 Morgan Prize for PRON research in number theory, the theory of Lie groups, and non-standard models of arithmetic.
PRON graduated in 2003, and went to Boston University for graduate school, where PRON completed PRON Ph.D. in 2008.
PRON dissertation, Growth Estimates for Dyson-Schwinger Equations, was supervised by NAME.
In 2016, NAME was awarded a Humboldt Fellowship to visit NAME at the Humboldt University of Berlin.
NAME is the author of the books NAME–Schwinger Equations (Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society, 2011) and A Combinatorial Perspective on Quantum Field Theory (Springer, 2017).
