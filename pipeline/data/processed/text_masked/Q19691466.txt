Maciej NAME (born 1975) is a Polish physicist and professor of physical sciences specialising in optics and biomedical imaging.
PRON received the Prize of the Foundation for Polish Science for developing NAME-domain optical coherence tomography and introducing it into ophthalmic practice.
PRON is a co-founder of the International Centre for Translational Eye Research (ICTER) at the Institute of Physical Chemistry of the Polish Academy of Sciences and the centre's director.
Education and career NAME graduated in physics from Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń in 1999.
PRON received PRON doctorate in 2003 with the dissertation Spectral Optical Coherence Tomography Using Partially Coherent Light in Medical Applications, and PRON habilitation in 2010 on the basis of Imaging Using Fourier-Domain Optical Coherence Tomography (FD-OCT).
Both degrees were awarded at the university's Institute of Physics within the Faculty of Physics, Astronomy and Informatics.
PRON received the Polish academic title of professor of physical sciences in 2014.
PRON appointment document was presented at a ceremony at the Presidential Palace in Warsaw on 28 January 2015.
PRON began working at Nicolaus Copernicus University in 1999 and led the Optical Biomedical Imaging Group within the Institute of Physics' Department of Biophysics and Medical Physics.
Between 1998 and 2001, PRON conducted research through an exchange programme at the University of Vienna.
PRON worked at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology from 2003 to 2005 and also undertook research visits at the University of Kent in Canterbury and the University of Western Australia in Perth.
PRON is affiliated with the Department of Biophotonics and Optical Engineering at Nicolaus Copernicus University.
In 2016, following an international competition, PRON was selected to head the Department of Physical Chemistry of Biological Systems at the Institute of Physical Chemistry of the Polish Academy of Sciences.
The department was established through the CREATE project ("The CREAtion of the Department of Physical Chemistry of Biological SysTEms"), funded under Horizon 2020.
Together with NAME, PRON founded ICTER within the institute.
PRON has directed the centre since 2019.
PRON also heads its Physical Optics and Biophotonics research group.
NAME leads the TRIO-VI CoE project, which supports ICTER's development into a centre of excellence for translational research and innovation in ophthalmology.
The project began in January 2025 and is scheduled to end in December 2030.
It received €15 million in European Union funding through the Teaming for Excellence action under Horizon Europe.
The Institute of Physical Chemistry coordinates the project, with University College London and the Institut de la Vision associated with Sorbonne University as strategic partners.
Its activities include research collaboration, training and the translation of research findings into ophthalmic practice.
Research  NAME studies the interaction of light with tissue and develops optical methods for imaging living organisms.
PRON research includes optical coherence tomography, fluorescence imaging of the retina and two-photon vision.
Optical coherence tomography  In 2002, NAME and PRON collaborators published results of in vivo imaging of the human retina using NAME-domain optical coherence tomography.
In 2003, with NAME, Piotr NAME and NAME, PRON presented a high-speed spectral optical coherence tomography method that enabled real-time imaging of the eye.
The exposure time for an individual axial scan was 64 microseconds.
Between 2003 and 2006, prototypes of PRON tomograph were tested at ophthalmic clinics in Boston, Pittsburgh and Bydgoszcz.
In 2005, PRON began collaborating with Optopol on industrial production of the device.
Two-photon vision and retinal imaging  In 2014, NAME led research into two-photon vision that demonstrated the role of two-photon absorption in the isomerisation of retinal pigments producing visual sensations.
In 2021, PRON and PRON collaborators presented in vivo images of the human retina obtained using two-photon-excited fluorescence.
PRON research group at ICTER develops imaging methods that use both the amplitude and phase of light, including techniques based on spatiotemporal optical coherence manipulation (STOC).
Research projects  PRON received support through the Foundation for Polish Science's START (2003), POWROTY/HOMING (2006) and TEAM (2011) programmes.
In 2007, PRON received a European Young Investigator NAME grant.
From 2017 to 2022, PRON led the "Two photon vision and two photon eye imaging" project (2x2-PhotonVis), funded through the Foundation for Polish Science's TEAM-TECH programme.
It sought to develop methods and instrumentation for functional studies of vision using two-photon processes.
From 2018 to 2021, PRON led the institute's participation in IMCUSTOMEYE ("IMaging-based CUSTOMised EYE diagnostics"), a Horizon 2020 project concerning the use of photonics and modelling in personalised ophthalmic diagnostics.
From 2019 to 2023, PRON led the "International Centre for Translational Eye Research" project (MAB/2019/12), funded through the Foundation for Polish Science's International Research Agendas programme, through which ICTER was established.
