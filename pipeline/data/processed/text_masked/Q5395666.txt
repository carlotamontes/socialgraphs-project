Ulrich Mosel (born Febr.
8, 1943 in Hannover, Germany) is a German theoretical physicist, professor emeritus at Justus-Liebig-Universität Giessen, Germany  Ulrich Mosel studied mathematics and physics at the J.W. Goethe University at Frankfurt a.M., Germany, from 1962 - 1968.
PRON finished PRON studies with a degree of 'Diplom Physiker' in 1967.
After that PRON worked with NAME and received PRON PhD in physics in 1968 with a thesis entitled "Investigation of collective potential energy surfaces of nuclei: superheavy nuclei".
At that time PRON worked on a theoretical description of the structure of heavy and superheavy nuclei.
In early 1970 PRON went to the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, and to the Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
From there PRON moved in 1971 to the University of Washington in Seattle.
During this time PRON worked on a theoretical description of nuclear fission.
In 1972 PRON was appointed as full professor for theoretical physics at the Justus-Liebig-Universität Giessen, Germany.
In 1980/81 and in 1993/1994 PRON served as GENDERWORD of the physics department.
PRON became a professor emeritus in April 2011.
During PRON time at Giessen 9 professors and 47 PhDs in physics emerged from PRON group.
During PRON time at NAME PRON was visiting scientist at Argonne National Laboratory and Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory.
PRON was also guest professor at the State University of New York at Stony Brook, and the Michigan State University and the University of Washington.
From 1982 - 88 PRON was a member of the Scientific Council of GSI Darmstadt and from 1997 - 2001 in its program advisory committee.
At that time PRON also headed the division of nuclear and hadron physics of the German Physical Society.
From 1995 to 2001 PRON was GENDERWORD of the COSY PAC and from 1997 - 2000 PRON was GENDERWORD of the scientific advisory board for hadron and nuclear physics of the German Federal Ministry for Science and Technology.
PRON early work centered on the structure of heavy and superheavy nuclei.
Following that were studies of nuclear physics with an emphasis on the fission mass distributions within the two-center shell model.
In 1976 PRON published the first microscopical calculation of the gas-liquid phase transition of nuclear matter.
Later on self-consistent studies of nuclear rotational states followed, with an emphasis on magnetic moments as indicators for rotational alignment.
Around 1990 Ulrich Mosel started to work with quantum-kinetic transport theory.
This work mainly concentrated on particle production in relativistic heavy-ion collisions.
One results of these studies was (together with NAME) the prediction of the formation of resonance matter in such collisions and the investigation of in-medium properties of hadrons  These calculations were later on extended to photo- and electro-nuclear processes on nuclei and, finally, on neutrino-interactions with nuclei.
From the latter studies the neutrino event generator GiBUU emerged.
