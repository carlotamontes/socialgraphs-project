Dieter Enders (17 March 1946 – 29 June 2019) was a German organic chemist who did work developing asymmetric synthesis, in particular using modified prolines as chiral auxiliaries.
The most widely applied of PRON chiral auxiliaries are the complementary SAMP and RAMP auxiliaries, which allow for asymmetric alpha-alkylation of aldehydes and ketones.
In 1974 PRON obtained PRON doctorate from the University of Gießen studying under NAME and followed this with a postdoc at Harvard University studying with NAME.
PRON then moved back to Gießen to obtain PRON Habilitation in 1979, whereupon PRON became a lecturer, soon obtaining Professorship in 1980 as Professor of Organic Chemistry at Bonn.
In 1985 PRON moved to NAME, where PRON was Full Professor of Organic Chemistry and Director.
PRON was editor-in-chief of Synthesis and was on the advisory boards of many other journals including Letters in Organic Chemistry and SynLett.
During PRON career PRON won many awards, including:   1993 Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize of the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft  1995 Yamada Award, Japan  2000 Max-Planck-Forschungspreis for Chemistry  2002 Emil-Fischer-Medaille of the GDCh  2014 Ryoji Noyori Prize, Japan
