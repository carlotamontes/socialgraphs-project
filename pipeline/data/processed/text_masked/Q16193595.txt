Research career   NAME has dedicated most of PRON scientific life to the Physics of High-Energy Heavy-Ion collisions, first in HELIOS, then in NA50, in the ALICE experiment and finally at GSI and FAIR.
PRON joined the Turin branch of the Istituto Nazionale di Fisica Nucleare (INFN) in 1985.
In 2006 PRON was promoted to "research director", the highest in the three-level INFN career.
Giubellino has been responsible for several scientific programs within INF] and for NATO, INTAS and European Union grants.
From 1990 to 1996 PRON was coordinator of the Group II (one of the five sections in which INFN research is organized) of the Turin branch of the INFN.
From 1995 to 2000 and since 2007 PRON was responsible for the involvement of the Turin group in the ALICE Inner Tracking System project.
Giubellino has participated in CERN heavy-ion programme from the early days of PRON career.
PRON was in charge of the design, construction and operation of the SCI-PAD detector for the NA34/1 experiment as well as for the NA34/2 silicon pad detectors for the Ring Counters.
In 1988 PRON joined the NA50 collaboration and worked in one of the fixed-target experiments.
PRON was responsible in NA50 for the design, construction and commissioning of the silicon multiplicity detectors (MD).
During PRON entire career, Giubellino has participated in several R&D projects directed to the development of silicon detectors and radiation tolerant electronics.
PRON is also one of the founding parents of the microelectronics group at INFN Turin.
Giubellino has been involved in ALICE from the very first feasibility studies, and has later carried a number of responsibilities in the experiment, including Project Leader for the Inner Tracking System, Chair of Conference Committee, Upgrade Coordinator and, for six years, Deputy NAME (July 2000 – September 2002 and August 2006 – December 2010).
PRON was elected NAME of the ALICE Collaboration for the first time in March 2010 and re-elected in July 2013.
During this period PRON led the ALICE Collaboration to the preparation of an Upgrade proposal for experiment, spanning the years 2018 to 2025.
The upgrade project, which will involve 163 Institutions from 40 countries, has been approved by the Large Hadron Collider Committee in September 2012.
On 1 January 2017, NAME became the first joint scientific managing director of Facility for Antiproton and Ion Research in Europe GmbH (FAIR GmbH) and GSI Helmholtzzentrum für Schwerionenforschung GmbH in Darmstadt.
In addition, PRON has taken over the position of spokesperson of the management of FAIR and GSI.
In September 2016, the FAIR Council and the GSI Supervisory Board announced their decision to appoint Giubellino.
Since 1 January 2017 Giubellino is professor at the Institute of Nuclear Physics of the Technische Universität Darmstadt.
