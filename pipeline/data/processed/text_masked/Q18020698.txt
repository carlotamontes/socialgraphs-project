Hans-Wolfgang Spiess (born 14 October 1942 in Frankfurt am Main), also NAME, is a German polymer chemist who specializes in the Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy of macromolecules.
PRON was director of the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research in Mainz until PRON retirement in 2012.
Education and career  Spiess obtained PRON PhD in physical chemistry from the Goethe University of Frankfurt in 1968, under the supervision of NAME.
PRON then did postdoctoral work with NAME at the Florida State University.
In 1970, PRON moved to the Department of Molecular Physics headed by NAME at the Max Planck Institute for Medical Research in Heidelberg, where PRON studied high-resolution NMR in solids and liquids.
In 1975, PRON moved to the Institute of Physical Chemistry at the University of Mainz, where PRON worked with NAME and obtained PRON habilitation in 1978.
From 1981 to 1982, PRON was professor of physical chemistry at the University of Münster, and from 1983, PRON was professor of macromolecular chemistry at the University of Bayreuth.
In 1984, Spiess became director of the Max Planck Institute for Polymer Research in Mainz.
PRON research focuses on NMR methods and studies for the determination of the structure, dynamics, and phase behavior of synthetic macromolecules and supramolecular structures.
Honors and awards  In 2002, Spiess received the Liebig Medal and the Ampère Prize, in 2010 the Paul J. Flory Research Prize and the NAME award, in 2005 the Walter Nernst Medal, and in 1988 the Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize.
PRON is an honorary doctor at the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca in Romania and at the Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań, and an honorary professor at the East China Normal University in Shanghai.
In December 2011, Spiess received the Order of Merit of the State of Rhineland-Palatinate.
From 1991 to 1992, Spiess was a member of the board of the European Polymer Federation.
