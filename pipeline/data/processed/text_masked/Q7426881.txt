PRON is known for PRON studies on Langmuir probes and other contributions in space and plasma sciences.
A protégé of NAME, NAME is an elected fellow of all the three major Indian science academies such as Indian Academy of Sciences, Indian National Science Academy and National Academy of Sciences, India as well as the Gujarat Science Academy and is a recipient of the Hari Om Ashram Prerit Senior Scientist Award.

Biography  NAME was born on 1 July 1929, in Najibabad, Uttar Pradesh, India.
PRON is a Physicist and has done pioneering work in the study of Solar time variation of NAME, the study of the equatorial ionospheric D, E and F region using space borne as well as ground based techniques.
In recognition to these researches, PRON was awarded the First Hari Om Prerit Vikram Sarabhai award during 1975 and was decorated with Padma Shree award during 1982 by the President of India.
In recognition PRON was elected as fellows of all the three important Indian Academies.
PRON was elected as a Fellow of INSA during 1983.
Later on PRON was also elected a fellow of Indian Academy of Sciences Bangalore, National Academy of Sciences, Allahabad, and Gujarat Science Academy.
NAME earned PRON bachelor's degree and master's degree in Science from Allahabad University in 1951, joined Physical Research Laboratory (PRL) Ahmedabad and worked on Solar influence on the cosmic ray neutrons under the supervision of Prof. NAME.
For PRON studies, which was during the International Geophysical year PRON developed and established neutron piles at Kodaikanal and Gulmarg to measure cosmic ray produced neutrons.
For the neutron counters used in the pile, PRON developed an all cooper metal system for purification of highly corrosive BF3 gas used in the counters.
After PRON Ph D degree from Gujarat University, PRON continued PRON cosmic ray studies at National Research Council Canada  as a Post- Doctoral fellow with Prof. NAME.

At PRL during 1962, PRON developed a group for in-situ studies of the upper atmospheric plasmas, plasma density irregularities and associated instability mechanisms, fields, and currents in the Equatorial Electrojet (EEJ), E and F region of the ionosphere.
PRON indigenously developed a modified Langmuir probe and other supporting payloads that enabled PRON to carry out pioneering in-situ measurement of the plasma density irregularities in the EEJ leading to the identification of various plasma processes.
Rocket borne payloads of Resonance probe, Electric field probe and NAME detector with many special features were developed and used to carry out various studies.
Ultraviolet monochromator and light sources were developed for the calibration of UV detectors.
Payloads for the study of the energetic electrons were developed and flown on the first Indian Satellite Aryabhata.
VHF radar operating at 50 MHz was developed and established at Thumba for structure and dynamics of the EEJ.
Theoretical studies of the gravity wave wind generated electron density irregularities in the D, E and F region, electric field in the E region and their transmission to the F region via the geomagnetic field lines.
PRON guided several research scholars and had many scientific publications in reputed journals.

The vacuum ultraviolet monochromator, light sources, and other associated techniques development by PRON lead to the development of Laboratory Astrophysics group at Physical Research Laboratory (PRL).
On the initiation Prof. Sarabhai and Prof. NAME, PRON initiated jointly with Prof. NAME a plasma simulation experimental program of the EEJ associated plasma density irregularities at Physical Research Laboratory.
