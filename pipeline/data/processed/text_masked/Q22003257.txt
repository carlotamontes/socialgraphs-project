Evert Jan Baerends (born 17 September 1945) is a Dutch theoretical chemist.
PRON is an emeritus professor of the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
Baerends is known for PRON development and application of electronic structure calculations, which over time led to the development of the Amsterdam Density Functional.
PRON worked extensively on density functional theory.
Career Baerends was born on 17 September 1945 in Voorst.
PRON obtained PRON PhD at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam under professor NAME.
Baerends became a professor of Theoretical chemistry at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
PRON did extensive research on density functional theory and was involved in the development and application of electronic structure calculations, which later led to the development of the Amsterdam Density Functional.
Baerends became a member of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences in 2004.
In 2010 PRON was awarded the Schrödinger Medal by the World Association of Theoretical and Computational Chemists, being noted for: "For PRON pioneering contributions to the development of computational density functional methods and PRON fundamental contributions to density functional theory and density matrix theory."
Baerends is a member of the International Academy of Quantum Molecular Science.
After PRON retirement in the Netherlands Baerends lectured at Pohang University of Science and Technology in South Korea.
In 2019 PRON received an honorary doctorate from the University of Girona.
