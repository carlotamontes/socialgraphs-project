András Perczel (born 10 May 1959) is a Hungarian chemist, biochemist, university professor, an elected member of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences.
PRON major field of research is structural chemistry and biology including synthesis of biomolecules and NMR spectroscopy of peptides and proteins.
PRON was awarded the Bolyai prize (in Hungarian Bolyai János Alkotói díj) in 2011.
Life   Graduating from Piarista Gimnázium, Budapest, PRON studied chemistry from 1980 at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE), where PRON graduated as chemist in 1985.
As a PhD student, besides PRON research PRON held practical classes in organic chemistry at the teachers’ training college of ELTE.
While carrying on the practical classes, in 1987 Perczel became a scientific member of the Faculty of Organic Chemistry at ELTE.
After habilitation in 1999 PRON was nominated as a full professor in 2001.
In 2007 became the head of the Department of Organic Chemistry.
In addition PRON university work PRON is the head of the joint Protein Modelling Group of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences and ELTE.
PRON has performed PRON research work between 1998 and 2001 as a Széchenyi Professor, and participating the Fulbright program (2006).
PRON received PRON PhD in 1992, and PRON academic doctorate (D.Sc) in 1998.
In 2010 PRON was elected a “corresponding” member of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences, Member of The Academy of Europe.
In addition to PRON academic title, PRON is the founding president of the Biomolecular Chemistry Group of the Hungarian Chemical Society, member of the Hungarian Biochemical Society, and curator oft several foundations (Márton Kajtár Foundation, Foundation for the Hungarian Peptide and protein research, Lajos Kisfaludy Foundation, The Rosztoczy Foundation).
PRON is a member of the European Peptide Symposium (EPS) and member of the editorial board of Journal of Molecular Structure.
Work   András Perczel's main field of research is organic and bioorganic chemistry, structure elucidation of biomolecules, peptides and proteinsby CD-, NMR spectroscopy  and molecular modelling.
PRON major researches include synthesis of peptides, foldamers and  proteins by solid state synthesis and biotechnological methods.
PRON has achieved PRON most important results by use of spectroscopy: PRON has studied bioactive peptides with CD, NMR spectroscopy and molecular modelling.
PRON is the author or co-author of about  200 scientific publications with more than 3000 independent references.
PRON is the author of a book (in Hungarian) and several course book chapters.
