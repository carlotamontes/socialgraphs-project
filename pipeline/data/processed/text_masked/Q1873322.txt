PRON is also Director of the Institute of Complex Systems of the National Research Council (Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche).
Biography Pietronero was born in Rome and obtained a degree in Physics there in 1971.
PRON is married, has two children and lives in Rome.
Career PRON worked first at the Xerox Webster research Center and then the Brown Boveri Research Center where PRON stayed until 1983.
After that PRON moved to NAME in the Department of Physics as Professor of Condensed Matter Theory.
PRON is now a full professor of Condensed Matter Physics, University of Roma "La Sapienza".
In 2004 Pietronero founded the CNR Institute of Complex Systems (ISC).
The Institute includes more than 200 scientists (in various locations in Rome and Florence) from different groups of CNR, INFM, INOA and Universities.
PRON was elected a Fellow of the American Physical Society in 1990 "for contributions to the theory of fractals, including the dielectric-breakdown model and PRON extensions of the theory of diffusion-limited aggregation and applications of fractal methods to astronomy"  In 2007 PRON was the GENDERWORD of the 23rd edition of the STATPHYS conference held in Genova.
In 2008 PRON was awarded the Enrico Fermi Prize, the top prize of the Italian Physical Society.
