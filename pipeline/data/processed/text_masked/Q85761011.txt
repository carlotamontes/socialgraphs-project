PRON research explores the quantum aspects of the gravitational field, in the framework of Loop Quantum Gravity.
PRON work covers topics from the cosmological and astrophysical applications of quantum gravity to the reflections on the nature of space-time and the foundations of quantum mechanics.
PRON is best known for two research directions: Spin foam Cosmology, and Planck stars, with special emphasis on white holes and black hole remnants.
PRON main research interests have been cosmology, the quantum effect of black holes, and the foundations of quantum mechanics.
