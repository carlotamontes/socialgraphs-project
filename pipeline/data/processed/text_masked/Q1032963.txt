Biography NAME received PRON PhD from the University of Chicago in 1986.

PRON then moved back to the US in 1991 to work at FNAL, where PRON led the construction and design of silicon detectors and co-led the search for the top quark using lifetime tagging of b quark jets.

In 2011 PRON was elected to be the spokesperson for the CMS experiment.
On July 4, 2012, NAME announced the discovery of the Higgs Boson.
