NAME (born 1971) is a theoretical chemist, Professor of Computational Materials Science at the Otto Schott Institute of Materials Research at the Friedrich Schiller University Jena.
Biography  PRON studied chemistry and physics at the Jagiellonian University (1991–1994), the University of Bergen (1994–1995) and the Humboldt University of Berlin (1995–1996).
In June 1996 PRON graduated with a Master of Science degree in chemistry from the Jagiellonian University.
In November 2000 PRON obtained a doctorate in chemistry (summa cum laude) from the Humboldt University of Berlin, under the supervision of NAME.
In June 2009 PRON obtained habilitation in theoretical chemistry from the Humboldt University of Berlin.
In October 2009 PRON was appointed as lecturer at the Humboldt University of Berlin.
In September 2011 PRON was a visiting professor at the Italian Institute of Technology at the National Nanotechnology Laboratories in Lecce.
In 2012 PRON was appointed Professor of Computational Materials Science at the Otto Schott Institute of Materials Research at the Friedrich Schiller University Jena.
Between 2016 and 2017 PRON was a lecturer at the Jagiellonian University.
PRON fields of interest include modeling and atomistic simulations, structure, properties and reactivity of low-dimensional systems (clusters, nanoparticles, thin layers, surfaces) as well as methods for global structural optimization and design of functional materials.
Since 2017 PRON has been a member of the editorial board of Materials.
PRON was elected a member of the Gesellschaft Deutscher Chemiker e.V. (GDCh) and the Arbeitsgemeinschaft für Theoretische NAME (AGTC).
