NAME (born 9 November 1943 in Nonantola) is an Italian theoretical physicist active at the International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA),  and at the Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP), both in Trieste, Italy.
PRON is a broad-scope theorist who carried out research on a  wide range of condensed matter physics phenomena.
PRON early work dealt with optical properties, electron energy loss, theory of excitons and nonlocal dielectric response  in solids, including layer crystals such as graphite and semiconductors; charge- and spin-density-waves;  surface physics in all its aspects, particularly reconstruction, roughening and melting, also in clusters; the prediction the Berry phase in fullerene; the first calculated STM map of graphite, now a standard in the field; matter at extreme pressures: carbon, oxygen, hydrogen, , iron at earth core conditions, water and ammonia at deep planetary conditions, pressure-induced insulator-metal transitions in layer compounds like MoS2.
In nanophysics, PRON and PRON group predicted helical structures of metal nanowires; the spontaneous magnetism of metal nanocontacts, including the electronic circumstances for normal or ferromagnetic Kondo effect therein.
PRON and PRON collaborator's theory of strongly correlated superconductivity was recently confirmed in compounds such as Cs3C60.
Pioneering papers on quantum annealing are now basic to current developments in quantum computing.
More recently PRON moved on to the theory of nanofriction, a field where PRON  obtained the ERC Advanced Grant MODPHYSFRICT 2013–2019, and subsequently, as co-PI with an experimental group, another  ERC Advanced Grant ULTRADISS 2019-2024 .

Life  Born of a peasant family,  PRON made it to the renowned Istituto tecnico industriale NAME  high school of Modena, where PRON early subjects were  technical and practical—electronics, nuclear technology, etc.
Skills that allowed PRON to earn a living as a technician in a human physiology research laboratory while studying physics at the local Universita' di NAME.
Associated from boyhood to NAME's  renowned Societa' NAME directed by Livio Borri , a great music teacher, PRON sang for some years opera and church music within the choir which included among others the young NAME.
PRON physics degree (Universita' di Modena 1967) was on pion-nucleon phase shift dispersion relations, a subject suggested by NAME.
Admitted to doctoral studies in NAME's famous Scuola Normale Superiore  PRON switched to the burgeoning field of solid state physics in the freshly established research group of NAME working on the optical properties of semiconductors.
PRON  doctoral work (Scuola NAME, Pisa 1970) employed optical and electron energy loss data along with dispersion relations typical of high energy physics  to work out the  anisotropic dielectric tensor of graphite, later useful in the context of graphene.
After serving as a weather forecast under-lieutenant  (GARF) for PRON (then compulsory) military service in the Italian Air Force,  PRON moved to University of Rome—where PRON also collaborated with NAME—with a researcher position of Italy's National Research Council (CNR) .
That position allowed PRON freedom to leave for a joint 18-month long  Royal Society -  NATO fellowship to the Cavendish Laboratory, at the University of Cambridge, UK, where in 1972-73 PRON collaborated with NAME.
PRON also spent most of 1974 with a DFG fellowship at the University of Stuttgart in the group of NAME and most of 1977 at Stanford University, in the group of NAME .
From Rome PRON was called to Trieste in 1977 by NAME and NAME to start within the  International Centre for Theoretical Physics  the  ICTP Condensed Matter Theory Group, now flourishing.
In 1980 PRON also became a professor at the newly established  International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA), where PRON founded and directed for 27 years the Condensed Matter Theory group, and where PRON still works.
From 1977 onwards PRON double involvement with SISSA and ICTP kept PRON in Trieste, with the exception of a sabbatical year at the IBM Zurich Research Laboratory where in 1984-85 PRON also collaborated with NAME, as well as with NAME and NAME.
PRON strong connection to the  ICTP  also brought PRON to serve as  Director in 2002–2003.
