NAME (; born 7 February 1951) is a French physicist.
PRON was a permanent professor in theoretical physics at the Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques (IHÉS) from 1989 to 2022.
Since then, PRON is professor emeritus.
An expert in general relativity, PRON has long taught this theory at the École Normale Supérieure (Ulm).
PRON contributed greatly to the modelling of gravitational waves from compact binary systems, and with NAME, PRON invented the "effective one-body" approach to representing the orbital trajectories of binary black holes.
In 2021 PRON was awarded, with NAME, the Balzan Prize for Gravitation: physical and astrophysical aspects as well as the Galileo Galilei Medal and the Dirac Medal of the ICTP, both also shared with Frans Pretorius.
