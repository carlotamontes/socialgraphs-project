NAME (; born 1936 in Sofia, Bulgaria) is an Israeli chemist and materials scientist.
PRON is an emeritus professor at the Weizmann Institute of Science.
Biography  NAME emigrated to Israel in 1948.
PRON studied at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, where PRON obtained a master's degree in polymer chemistry in 1962.
With NAME at the Weizmann Institute of Science, PRON obtained a Ph.D. in solid state chemistry.
As a postdoctoral fellow, NAME worked with Paul Doughty Bartlett at Harvard University, among others, before returning to Israel in 1971.
Since 1985 PRON has held a full professorship at the Weizmann Institute.
In a long-term collaboration with NAME, NAME dealt with how crystal formation and growth can be influenced by targeted impurities (useful impurities) and with the direct determination of the sense of chirality of molecules.
PRON investigated crystallization and interface phenomena such as the packing of amphiphilic molecules at the phase boundary and the basis for heterogeneous catalysis.
PRON sought to understand biomembranes and designed self-assembling Langmuir-Blodgett layers (see self-assembling monolayer), investigated reaction pathways through asymmetric transformations into chiral and centrosymmetric crystals, guest-host interactions in organic solids, the correlation of macroscopic phenomena with molecular chirality, as well as stereochemical and regiospecific photoreactions within organic solids.
NAME received the Centenary Prize of the Royal Society of Chemistry in 1984/85, the Prelog Medal in 1987, the Gregori Aminoff Prize in 2002, the Chirality Medal in 2006, and the Israel Prize in 2016.
For 2018 PRON was awarded the EMET Prize.
In 2021 PRON and NAME jointly received the Wolf Prize in Chemistry.
PRON has been a member of the NAME since 1997.
