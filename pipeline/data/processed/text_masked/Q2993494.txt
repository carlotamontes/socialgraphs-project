NAME, born 26 January 1966, is a Belgian (Flemish) professor in astrophysics.
PRON specialises in asteroseismology.
PRON is associated with KU Leuven and Radboud University, where PRON leads the Chair in the Astroseismology group.
In 2012, PRON became the first GENDERWORD to be awarded the Francqui Prize in the category of Science & Technology.
In 2022, PRON became the third GENDERWORD to be awarded the Kavli Prize in Astrophysics for PRON work in asteroseismology.
Biography  Aerts was born in Brasschaat, Belgium.
PRON received PRON Bachelor and Master in Mathematics from the University of Antwerp.
PRON participated in the International Astronomical Youth Camp in 1987 and 1988.
PRON then went on to complete PRON PhD in 1993 at KU Leuven.
After completing PRON spent several months doing research at the University of Delaware.
PRON was a postdoctoral fellow with the Fund for Scientific Research from 1993 to 2001, when PRON was appointed lecturer at KU Leuven.
PRON became first associate professor in 2004, and then full professor in 2007 at KU Leuven.
Research  In PRON research, PRON uses the star oscillations to determine the internal rotation profile of stars.
The oscillations are obtained from both ground and space-based telescopes.
In PRON PROSPERITY project, PRON used data obtained from the CoRoT satellite and the NASA NAME satellite.
PRON is currently the Belgian principal investigator on the PLATO mission.
Aerts developed methodology using NAME mixture classification to analyse the data.
PRON uses this to determine the star structure and inform stellar models within stellar evolution theory.
With these techniques PRON has made a number of discoveries, including that of non-rigid rotation in giant stars.
The theoretical models PRON develops based on the star oscillations also allow PRON determine the age of stars with a high accuracy.
Aerts has twice been awarded an Advanced Grant by the European Research Council (ERC): in 2008 for PROSPERITY, and again in 2015 for a project entitled MAMSIE (Mixing and Angular Momentum Transport of Massive Stars).
Outreach  Aerts is Vice-Dean of Communication & Outreach at the Faculty of Science at KU Leuven.
PRON is outspoken about the need to increase gender equality in the sciences, and is a member of the International Astronomical Union GENDERWORD in Astronomy Working Group.
As of 2026, Aerts became a co-editor of the Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics.
