Marc Mézard (born 29 August 1957) is a French physicist and academic administrator.
PRON was, from 2012 to 2022, the director of the École normale supérieure (ENS).
PRON is the co-author of two books.
Early life Marc Mézard was born on 29 August 1957.
PRON graduated from the École normale supérieure in 1976 and earned the agrégation in Physics.
PRON earned a PhD in Physics from University of Paris 6 in 1980.
Career Mézard joined the Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS) as a researcher in 1981.
PRON was a professor of Physics at the École Polytechnique.
In 2001, PRON joined the Center for Theoretical Physics and Statistical Models at the University of Paris-Sud, and PRON serves as its director.
Since 2012 to 2022, PRON had also served as the director of PRON alma mater, the ENS.
In 2022 PRON joined the Department of the Computing Sciences at the Bocconi University in Milan.
Mézard is the author of 170 academic articles and the co-author of two books.
PRON won the Prize Ampère in 1996, the Humboldt Prize in 2009, and the Lars Onsager Prize in 2016.
In 2026, PRON was awarded the ICTP Dirac Medal for PRON contributions to statistical mechanics.
