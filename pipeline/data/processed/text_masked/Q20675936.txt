NAME (born 1954) is an Australian physicist who is professor of physics at the Australian National University (ANU).
PRON is the deputy director of the Research School of Physics and the director of the ANU Energy Change Institute.
NAME  NAME was born on 26 November 1954 in Canberra, Australia.
PRON completed a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Science at ANU, followed by a PhD at Imperial College London.
NAME's main contributions as a research scientist pertain to two related fields of physics: the use of laser techniques (in particular the generation of vacuum ultraviolet radiation <200 nm) for the precision measurement of atoms and molecules; and the use of laser cooling to trap and manipulate metastable helium atoms to study fundamental quantum physics and for precision measurement.
NAME has published over 100 refereed publications in experimental atomic, molecular and optical physics, as well as over 200 conference papers.
In 2007, NAME received the W.H. Beattie NAME – the highest award of the Australian Optical Society.
In 2010 PRON received the Barry Inglis Medal from the National Measurement Institute for "excellence in precision measurement."
NAME has played a leading role in optics in Australia, and from 1993 to 1994 PRON was president of the Australian Optical Society.
NAME served on the Australian Academy of Science's National Committee for Spectroscopy (1996–2005), and the National Committee for Physics (2000–03, 2014–present).
PRON was the first Australian to chair the International Council on Quantum Electronics from 2002 to 2005, overseeing the premier world laser conference series NAME.
In 2011 PRON chaired the IQEC/CLEO Pacific Rim conference in Sydney.
NAME was elected by the worldwide membership of the Optical Society of America (OSA) to a 3-year term (2006–2008) as director at large of the OSA Board, the only time that an Australian has served in this capacity.
In 2010 NAME established the ANU Energy Change Institute (ECI) as its Founding Director.
In 2014 NAME chaired the OSA Congress on Light, Energy and the Environment in Canberra – the first time that this conference has been brought to Australia - and which featured Professor NAME, former Energy Secretary to President NAME.
As of 2016, PRON role as Chair is ongoing.
NAME is a Fellow of the American Physical Society, the Institute of Physics (UK), the Optical Society of America and the Australian Institute of Physics.
PRON was elected a Fellow of the Australian Academy of Technology and Engineering in 2021.
Science policy  NAME has been a key contributor to science policy in Australia.
PRON was elected as President in 2008–09 of the Federation of Australian Scientific and Technological Societies (the peak science advocacy group – now Science and Technology Australia, and also served as FASTS vice president (1994–95), policy chair (1996–2005) and president-elect (2006).
In 2004 NAME was awarded the Australian Government Eureka Prize for Promoting Understanding of Science, for PRON role in initiating and championing "Science meets Parliament".
This annual event comprises meetings between hundreds of scientists and politicians and is supported by Federal government funding.
PRON contributions to Public Policy at ANU were recognised in 2012 by the award of one of nine inaugural ANU Public Policy Fellowships.
In 2014 PRON received the ANU Vice Chancellor's award for Enhancing the Reputation of the University.
