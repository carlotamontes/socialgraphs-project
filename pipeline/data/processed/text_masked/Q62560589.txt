NAME has written over 280 papers and has been cited over 12000 times.
PRON research interests include Statistical Physics, non-equilibrium pattern formation and soft condensed matter physics.

Research Van Saarloos has focused PRON research on theoretical physics, in particular in statistical and condensed matter physics.
PRON work in pattern formation in non-equilibrium systems centers on the development of the amplitude equation description of systems exhibiting a transition to standing waves.
With Hohenberg PRON developed a classification of fronts, pulses, sources and sinks in such systems.
Condensed matter and statistical physics Together with PRON thesis advisor Mazur, NAME calculated the mobility tensors submerged in viscous fluid of an arbitrary number of spheres in an expansion of power series.
Their expressions, which involve three- and four particle interactions, provide the basis for many particle hydrodynamic simulations.
NAME work on propagating fronts on a number of years culminated in a paper together with NAME in which they derived the universal asymptotic behavior of a large class of fronts – so-called pulled fronts—propagating into an unstable state.
The review van Saarloos wrote on this type of fronts propagation, which occurs in many disciplines in the natural sciences, is PRON most highly cited paper.
Together with Hohenberg PRON worked in particular on the classification of coherent structures (fronts, pulses sources and sinks) in the one-dimensional complex NAME equation and its generalizations, which is the proper amplitude equation for traveling wave pattern forming systems.
With NAME and Nussinov, NAME reviewed the variety of ways in which Landau Fermi liquids can break down, giving rise to singular behavior.
Together with Morozov, NAME predicted that viscoelastic Poiseuile flow in a straight channel would exhibit a nonlinear instability at low Reynolds numbers and high NAME numbers.
The predicted instability was later experimentally observed.
Together with NAME and several coworkers NAME contributed to the understanding of force distributions in static granular packings and of the jamming transition in granular media.
NAME was co-editor of the book Dynamical Heterogeneities in Glasses, Colloids, and Granular Media in which PRON surveyed the theoretical and experimental research that deals with glassy physics and wrote the textbook Soft Matter: concepts, phenomena and applications together with NAME and NAME, which is supported by the website softmatterbook.online.
The Dutch Polder Model in science and research In a significant essay together with PRON predecessor as academy president, NAME highlighted how research and education together have led to the success of this small country becoming a key player in the world of science and research.
They also discussed how little is being invested in its research base and how this, together with increased international competition and increasing student numbers, erodes these trusted foundations.
The authors then made suggestions about the ways in which the country can recover and relish in their big successes while making sure that research plays a big part in their nation's future.
