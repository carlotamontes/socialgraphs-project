NAME (born 30 January 1954) is a Franco-Norwegian physical chemist and professor at the University of Strasbourg in France, known for PRON pioneering work in nanoscience.
PRON received the Kavli Prize in Nanoscience “for transformative contributions to the field of nano-optics that have broken long-held beliefs about the limitations of the resolution limits of optical microscopy and imaging”, together with NAME, and GENDERWORD NAME in 2014.
Academic career NAME obtained PRON bachelor's degree from Oberlin College, and a PhD from Pierre and Marie Curie University in Paris in the field of photo-physical chemistry.
PRON then worked at the Notre GENDERWORD Radiation Laboratory before joining the NEC Fundamental Research Laboratories in Japan in 1988 where PRON research shifted first to novel carbon materials such as fullerenes (C60), graphene and carbon nanotubes.
After discovering how to mass-produce carbon nanotubes, PRON and PRON colleagues measured many of their unique features such as their mechanical and wetting properties.
For PRON pioneering and extensive contribution to the field of carbon nanotubes, PRON shared the 2001 Agilent Europhysics Prize with NAME, NAME and NAME.
While working at NEC, NAME discovered a major new optical phenomenon.
PRON found that, contrary to the then accepted theory, it was possible to transmit light extremely efficiently through subwavelength holes milled in opaque metal films under certain conditions.
The phenomenon, known as extraordinary optical transmission, involves surface plasmons.
It has raised fundamental questions and is finding applications in broad variety of areas from chemistry to opto-electronics.
NAME has received several awards for the discovery of the extraordinary optical transmission such as the 2005 France Telecom Prize of the French Academy of Sciences and the 2009 Quantum Electronics and Optics Prize of the European Physical Society.
In 1999, NAME joined ISIS founded by Jean-Marie Lehn at the University of Strasbourg, which PRON headed from 2004 to 2012.
In 2017–2018, PRON held the L. Bettencourt chair for Technological Innovation at the Collège de France.
PRON is currently the director of the International Center for Frontier Research in Chemistry.
and the University of Strasbourg Institute for Advanced Study.
Since 2005 PRON has developed new field of research at the interface of quantum electrodynamics and physical chemistry.
PRON team demonstrated for the first time that material properties such as chemical reactivity could be modified by strongly coupling the molecules to the electromagnetic fluctuations of an optical cavity.
For this work PRON was awarded the 2018 Grand Prix of the Maison de la Chimie foundation.
PRON is a member of the Institut Universitaire de France, the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters, the French Academy of Sciences and the Royal Flemish Academy of Belgium for Sciences and the Arts.
