PRON also is an adjunct professor in the department of chemistry at Kansas State University.

Biography   Sorensen was born in Omaha, Nebraska.
PRON earned a Bachelor of Science in physics from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in 1969.
PRON was drafted and served in Vietnam in military intelligence.
PRON earned PRON Ph.D. in physics in 1977 from the University of Colorado.
PRON joined the physics department at Kansas State University in 1977 as an assistant professor of physics; PRON was promoted to associate professor in 1982 and professor in 1986.
PRON was named a university distinguished professor in 2000 and the Cortelyou-Rust University Distinguished Professor in 2009.
PRON is also a University Distinguished Teaching Scholar at Kansas State University.
Research   Sorensen's research focuses on graphene materials synthesis via an explosion/detonation method PRON invented.
PRON also performs experimental and theoretical studies of light scattering by particles of arbitrary size and shape and  aggregation and gelation kinetics in aerosols and colloids.
PRON is the author of more than 300 papers and holds seven patents including a method for graphene synthesis.
PRON has directed the research of  20 master's recipients, 21 doctorate recipients, and 11 postdoctoral students.
In 2007-2008 PRON served as president of the American Association for Aerosol Research.
PRON is a fellow of the American Association for Aerosol Research, the American Association for the Advancement of Science,  and  the American Physical Society.
