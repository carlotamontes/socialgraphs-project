NAME  (born 19 September 1961) is a British-Polish physicist.
PRON is a professor of quantum physics at the Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford, professorial fellow in quantum physics and cryptography at Merton College, Oxford, NAME Centennial Professor at the National University of Singapore and the founding director of the Centre for Quantum Technologies (CQT).
PRON research interests extend over most aspects of information processing in quantum-mechanical systems, with a focus on quantum communication and quantum computation.
PRON is best known as one of the pioneers of quantum cryptography.
Early life NAME was born in Wrocław, and studied physics at the Jagiellonian University in NAME and at the University of Oxford.
Between 1987 and 1991 PRON was a graduate student at Wolfson College, Oxford.
In PRON doctoral thesis PRON showed how quantum entanglement and non-locality can be used to distribute cryptographic keys with perfect security.
Career In 1991, PRON was elected a junior research fellow and subsequently (1994) a research fellow at Merton College, Oxford.
At the time, PRON established the first research group in quantum cryptography and computation, based in the Clarendon Laboratory, Oxford.
Subsequently, it evolved into the Centre for Quantum Computation, now based at Faculty of Mathematics, University of Cambridge in Cambridge.
Between 1993 and 2000, PRON held a position of the Royal Society Howe Fellow.
In 1998 PRON was appointed a professor of physics at the University of Oxford and a fellow and tutor in physics at Keble College, Oxford.
From 2002 until 2006 PRON was the Leigh-Trapnell Professor of Quantum Physics at the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics, Cambridge University and a professorial fellow of King's College, Cambridge.
Since 2006 PRON has been a professor of quantum physics at the Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford.
Also in 2006 PRON was appointed a NAME Centennial Professor at the National University of Singapore and became the founding director of the Centre for Quantum Technologies (CQT).
After retiring from the director position in 2020, PRON remains a Distinguished Fellow at CQT.
In 2020, PRON joined the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology as an adjunct professor.
PRON has worked with and advised several companies and government agencies, served on various professional advisory boards, and is the Vice GENDERWORD of The Noel Croucher Foundation.
Research NAME's research extends over most aspects of information processing in quantum-mechanical systems, with a focus on quantum cryptography and quantum computation.
Building on the idea of quantum non-locality and Bell's inequalities   PRON introduced entanglement-based quantum key distribution.
PRON 1991 paper generated a spate of new research that established a vigorously active new area of physics and cryptography.
It is one of the most cited papers in the field and was chosen by the editors of the Physical Review Letters as one of their "milestone letters", i.e., papers that made important contributions to physics, announced significant discoveries, or started new areas of research.
PRON subsequent work with NAME and NAME, from the Defence Research Agency (DRA) in Malvern, resulted in the proof-of-principle experimental quantum key distribution, introducing parametric down-conversion, phase encoding, and quantum interferometry into the repertoire of cryptography.
PRON and collaborators were the first to develop the concept of a security proof based on entanglement purification.
NAME and colleagues have made a number of contributions to both theoretical aspects of quantum computation and proposals for its experimental realisations.
These include proving that almost any quantum logic gate operating on two quantum bits is universal, proposing one of the first realistic implementations of quantum computation, e.g. using the induced dipole-dipole coupling in an optically driven array of quantum dots, introducing more stable geometric quantum logic gates, and proposing "noiseless encoding", which became later known as decoherence-free subspaces.
PRON other notable contributions include work on quantum state swapping, optimal quantum state estimation and quantum state transfer.
With some of the same collaborators, PRON has written on connections between the notion of mathematical proofs and the laws of physics.
PRON has also contributed semi-popular writing on the history of science.

PRON is also a co-recipient of the 2004 European Union Descartes Prize.
In 2016 PRON was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society.
PRON is a fellow of the Singapore National Academy of Science and a recipient of the 2017 Singapore Public Administration Medal (Silver) Pingat Pentadbiran Awam.
