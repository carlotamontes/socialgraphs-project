Jiun-Huei Proty Wu is a cosmologist in Taiwan.
PRON is currently on secondment serving as the Director of UK Office for Ministry of Science and Technology, Taiwan.
Previously PRON was the Deputy Vice President for International Affairs, National Taiwan University.
PRON is a tenured professor at Physics Department and Institute of Astrophysics, National Taiwan University, a Joint Researcher, Institute of Astronomy and Astrophysics, Academia Sinica, and an adjunct professor, Institute of Physics, National Chengchi University.
PRON also promotes telescope-DIY and 3D film making.
Life and career Jiun-Huei Proty Wu was born in 1970.
PRON parent, NAME, is a retired professor in art and an oil painter.
PRON grew up in Taichung, Taiwan, and received PRON BSc in physics from National Taiwan University in 1993.
PRON then spent two years in Navy for the mandatory military service.
After that, PRON moved to United Kingdom for advanced studies and received an MSc in astronomy with distinction from Sussex University in 1996, under the supervision of NAME.
PRON then obtained a PhD in cosmology under the supervision of Paul Shellard at Prof. NAME Relativity and Gravitation Group at DAMTP, University of Cambridge in 1999.
During the PhD years, PRON won the J.T.Knight Prize, and the thesis title is 'Cosmological Perturbations from Cosmic Strings'.
PRON then started working at U.C.Berkeley as a KDI Fellow, jointly appointed by NASA as a Long-Term Space Astronomer.
PRON participated and led a few papers in the projects MAXIMA and MAXIPOL.
PRON joined the faculty of Physics Department at National Taiwan University in 2001, and is currently a professor in both the Physics Department and Institute of Astrophysics.
PRON is also the Project Scientist of AMiBA, a cosmological telescope based in Hawaii and funded by Ministry of Education and Ministry of Science and Technology.
In addition, PRON is a Joint Researcher at Institute of Astronomy and Astrophysics, Academia Sinica (ASIAA), and an adjunct professor at National Chengchi University.
PRON was a visiting scholar at U.C. Berkeley, LBNL, UMN, and Fermilab.
Between 2010 and 2011, PRON was the Chief Editor of Physics Bimonthly, a major journal of the Physics Society Taiwan.
Between 2012 and 2014, PRON was a NAME (as Chair in Astrophysics) at Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST).
Since 2015, PRON has been the Advisory Panelist, Ministry of Science and Technology.
Since 2014, PRON has been the Deputy Vice President for International Affairs, National Taiwan University.
PRON is also the President of Cambridge Society Taiwan.
Since 2003, PRON has been serving in the national committee for the student selection and training for the International Physics Olympiad (IPhO), and has been participating in the IPhO leading the Taiwan team almost every year since.
PRON has been serving the same role for the Intel International Science and Engineering Fairs (Intel ISEF).
PRON is also a two-time Taiwan representative giving invited talks in the Intel ISEF Educator Academy (held every other year) to share the Taiwanese experience in education.
PRON has been a high-school textbook writer in natural sciences (for age 12–18) since 2003 and has been serving on the exam board for national entry exams for universities.
Research PRON general research interest is in cosmology, in particular the cosmic strings, loop quantum gravity, cosmic microwave background, cosmic topology, dark energy, and applications using machine learning.
PRON has expertise in telescope making, and designed the largest and the second largest home-designed observatories in Taiwan.
PRON also owns a few patents related to optics, including applications in light-field camera and 3D visualization.
These are widely applied in digital cameras and cell phones.
PRON also designed the largest 3D planetarium in Taiwan, providing self-made 3D films related to astrophysics and cosmology.

Popular science Jiun-Huei Proty Wu made PRON first two telescopes at the age of 12.
PRON used them to image Halley's Comet in 1986.
PRON started promoting the self-making of telescopes in 2003, and in the past years more than three thousands of telescopes have been made through the national camps that PRON conducted.
PRON also led the projects of building the largest and the second largest observatories in Taiwan.
With these contributions, PRON won the Excellence Award for Social Service, National Taiwan University, in 2012, and the Silver Medal of the first World Chinese Award for Popular Science in 2013.
PRON is also the first TED speaker from National Taiwan University, delivering an 18-minute talk in the 2011 annual meeting organized by TEDxTaipei then the co-curator of the 2012 annual meeting, and the co-host for the TED Global-Taiwan 2013.
PRON is a council member of TEDxTaipei.
Since 2011, PRON has been a columnist of BBC Knowledge (International Chinese Edition), a monthly journal for popular science.
In 2017 PRON co-hosted the NASA Hackathon (Space Apps Challenge) in Taiwan.
The champion team of Taiwan, SpaceBar, then won the Global Winner for Best Use of Data.
PRON led the team for an invited trip to watch the rocket launch at NASA in August 2017.
In 2018 and 2019 PRON continued to serve as a Taipei lead judge and the coach of the Taipei representative teams.
