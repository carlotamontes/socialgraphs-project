NAME (born 4 December 1976) is a particle physicist with dual Algerian and French nationality.
After PRON master of theoretical physics in Lyon, PRON joined LAPP (Laboratoire d'Annecy le Vieux de Physique des Particules) to work on the BaBar experiment, located at the Stanford Linear Accelerator Center.
PRON thesis, defended in 2003, was about the production of high energy Eta prime mesons in the decays of B mesons.
After that PRON was a Postdoctorate in England at the Rutherford Appleton Laboratory, where PRON worked on the ATLAS experiment at LHC.
PRON then joined the high energy physics department of École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) and works currently on the LHCb experiment.
NAME was arrested in France on 8 October 2009, after police allegedly intercepted emails between PRON and Al-Qaida in Islamic Maghreb, an Algerian-based terrorist organisation.
Two weeks after PRON arrest, the Algerian media compared Hicheur to NAME, who was first suspected of terrorism before being released without charges.
An International Support Committee was organized by NAME's friends and colleagues to support NAME and to request a fair investigation.
General director of the National Police Frédéric Péchenard stated in November 2009 that Hicheur planned to attack a base of the National Defence in Annecy, which harbours the 27ème bataillon de chasseurs alpins, involved in Afghanistan.
Hicheur's trial started on 29 March 2012 in Paris.
In May 2012 Hicheur was sentenced to "five years in prison for plotting terrorist attacks."
On 15 May Hicheur left prison and decided not to appeal against PRON sentence.
PRON was a visiting professor at Instituto de Física of Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
In July 2016, the government of Brazil had Hicheur deported back to France, where PRON was placed under house arrest.
Hicheur has decided to renounce PRON French citizenship and move to Algeria.
