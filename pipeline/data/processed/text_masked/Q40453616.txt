PRON is a full professor at the University of Mons (UMONS), where PRON was rector from 2018 to 2026.
PRON has been a member of the Class of Sciences of the Royal Academy of Science, Letters and Fine Arts of Belgium since 2010.
PRON received the Quinquennial Prize of the National Fund for Scientific Research (FNRS) in 2015.
Early life and education  Dubois was born in Charleroi.
PRON parent was a miner at the Roton colliery in Farciennes and PRON parent a homemaker.
PRON has said that without a university close to home PRON parents could not have paid for PRON studies, and that this shapes PRON view of higher education.
PRON studied chemistry at the Facultés universitaires Notre-GENDERWORD de la Paix in Namur, now the Université de Namur, and graduated in 1987 with a master's degree in organic chemistry.
PRON then moved to the University of Liège, where PRON obtained a doctorate in science in 1991.
PRON thesis, Molecular Engineering of Biocompatible and Biodegradable Materials through Ring-Opening of Lactones and Lactides, was passed with honours and led to PRON first patent, filed in 1989.
Career   Research posts and University of Mons  After PRON doctorate Dubois did an industrial postdoctoral period with Dow in the Netherlands.
PRON then held research posts of the FNRS at the University of Liège, first as research associate and later as senior research associate.
In 1994 PRON spent a second postdoctoral period in chemical engineering at Michigan State University, working on bio-based polymer materials.
In 1997 PRON moved to the University of Mons-Hainaut, which merged into the University of Mons in 2009.
There PRON founded the Service des Matériaux Polymères et Composites (SMPC), a polymer and composite materials group associated with the  centre of excellence.
PRON became professor in 1999 and full professor in 2003.
PRON was vice-dean of the Faculty of Science from 2002 to 2005, chaired the Department of Chemistry from 2003 to 2007, and advised the rector on research from 2006 to 2009.
In 2007 PRON founded the Centre for Innovation and Research in Polymer Materials (CIRMAP), which PRON directed until 2016.
PRON then served as the university's vice-rector for research for more than seven years.
Dubois co-founded NAME, a non-profit applied research centre in Mons, and is its president and one of its scientific directors.
Through NAME PRON helped set up the spin-off company GATE2, which markets technologies developed and patented at the centre.
Luxembourg  In January 2016 Dubois was appointed to lead the newly created National Composite Centre Luxembourg (NCC-L).
PRON was its scientific director and head of the sustainable materials unit at the Luxembourg Institute of Science and Technology (LIST) until 31 August 2017, and returned to the University of Mons on 1 September 2017.
PRON continued to advise the NCC-L on polymer and composite materials.

Research  Dubois works on the synthesis, processing and properties of polymers and (nano)composites, and much of PRON work concerns materials from renewable sources.
PRON doctoral work and PRON first papers, written in Liège with NAME and NAME, concerned the controlled ring-opening polymerization of lactones and lactides, the monomers of biodegradable polyesters such as polylactic acid (PLA).
One of these papers described the polymerisation of lactide with aluminium isopropoxide.
PRON team patented a catalyst system for producing PLA by reactive extrusion, which according to the University of Montpellier made the first continuous production process for PLA possible.
The process was described in a 1999 paper with NAME, NAME and others.
PRON returned to the subject in a 2022 article on the use of reactive extrusion to deal with plastic waste in the oceans.
PRON 2000 review with NAME on nanocomposites of polymers and layered silicate clays such as montmorillonite surveyed how these materials are made and what properties they have.
By 2011 one of PRON articles had been cited about 2,350 times, according to the Thomson Reuters data used for the ranking of materials scientists.
Further reviews followed on flame retardant polymer materials (2009), PLA-based nanocomposites (2013) and PLA composites (2016).
PRON group also grafted polycaprolactone chains onto cellulose nanocrystals, a form of nanocellulose, to make bionanocomposites.
With NAME and Jean-Marie Raquez PRON edited the Handbook of Ring-Opening Polymerization (2009).
Several of PRON group's results have been taken up by industry.
When PRON received the FNRS prize in 2015, Dubois said that about fifteen of its patents were in industrial use.
PRON gave as examples plastics made from agricultural surplus such as beet, wheat and maize, used for mobile phone cases and car interiors, and a hull paint that reduces the build-up of marine organisms on ships.
The University of Montpellier described the paint as free of bactericides.
By 2024 PRON had filed 76 patents, nearly a quarter of which had led to products or processes on the market.
PRON more recent work includes the enzymatic recycling of bio-based polymers.
PRON is one of the authors of a 2024 paper in Nature, written with researchers from the French company Carbios, on PLA with an engineered enzyme embedded in it so that the plastic breaks down by itself.
As holder of the Francqui Chair at the University of Liège in 2021, PRON gave a series of lectures at Gembloux Agro-Bio Tech on plastic pollution and bioplastics.
