NAME (; born 31 October 1961) is a Serbian chemist, university professor and politician.
A professor of organic chemistry and faculty of the department of Chemistry of the University of Belgrade, PRON has been a full member of the Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts (SANU) since 2018.
A supporter of the student-led anti-corruption protests, PRON was one of the founders of the citizens' group through which the Student List ran as a political party in the 2026 Serbian parliamentary election, and a candidate for Student List.
On election night, PRON announced Student List's victory from its headquarters.
Early life and education  NAME was born on 31 October 1961 in Belgrade.
PRON parent, Nikola Saičić, was an architect and university professor, and PRON parents are NAME.
PRON attended the Mathematical Grammar School in Belgrade.
PRON studied chemistry at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics of the University of Belgrade (now the Faculty of Chemistry), graduating in 1983, and obtained PRON master's degree there in 1987.
PRON defended PRON doctoral dissertation on free-radical chemistry, under the supervision of NAME, in 1991.
From 1995 to 1996, PRON completed postdoctoral studies at the Institute for the Chemistry of Natural Products of the French National Centre for Scientific Research in Gif-sur-Yvette.
PRON served in the Yugoslav Wars.
Academic career  After graduating, NAME became a trainee teaching assistant at the Faculty of Natural Sciences and Mathematics, and was appointed teaching assistant in 1989.
PRON was an associate professor at the Faculty of Chemistry from 1997 to 2002, when PRON became a full professor.
PRON headed the Department of Organic Chemistry from 1999 to 2003 and again from 2023, and chairs the faculty's ethics commission.
PRON research focuses on organic synthesis and medicinal chemistry, particularly the total synthesis of natural products such as paclitaxel (taxol) and its derivatives, which are used in chemotherapy.
In 2020, PRON became principal investigator of a project funded by the Science Fund of the Republic of Serbia under its special COVID-19 programme, on the repurposing of iminosugars and chloroquine analogues as therapies against RNA viruses.
The project involved the Faculty of Chemistry, the Institute of Chemistry, Technology and NAME and the Innovation Centre of the Faculty of Chemistry, in collaboration with the Pasteur Institute in Paris.
NAME was elected a corresponding member of SANU, in its Department of Chemical and Biological Sciences, on 1 November 2012, and a full member on 8 November 2018.
PRON has been a member of the Serbian Chemical Society since 1984.
Political activity  NAME supported the student-led anti-corruption protests that followed the Novi Sad railway station canopy collapse in November 2024, and was targeted by pro-government media for doing so.
In June 2025, PRON was one of 25 SANU members who signed a petition calling for the resignation of Aleksandar Vučić over the "collapse of the democratic, institutional and social foundations of our country".
In May 2026, PRON walked out of the opening of an exhibition of the Serbian Orthodox Church in the SANU Gallery when Minister of Culture Nikola Selaković was announced as a speaker, saying that Selaković had caused "immeasurable damage to Serbian culture".
Speaking at a protest at Slavija Square later that month, PRON accused the government of beating "children who seek justice" while protecting criminals.
2026 parliamentary election  NAME was one of 14 founders of the citizens' group "Student List – Students Are Winning", through which the Student List ran in the 2026 snap parliamentary election, and a candidate of the party.
Confirming PRON candidacy on 10 September 2026, PRON described the government as "criminalised and corrupt" and said PRON was prepared to put "even PRON life" at the disposal of the students and the country.
PRON spoke at the rally in Novi Sad that opened Student List's campaign the same day, where the first candidates were presented, saying that Serbia was "occupied today too, not by a foreign invader, but from within".
On the parliamentary election night, after Vučić conceded defeat, Saičić, described by Bloomberg as one of the Student List's leaders, addressed supporters from Student List headquarters and announced its victory.
PRON said: "Dear Serbia, tonight the voice of the people is being heard.
Tonight we celebrate victory, tomorrow we build the future."
Calling the result "not anyone's trophy, but a victory of the citizens", PRON urged supporters to celebrate "without hatred", adding that "tomorrow the work begins".
In October 2026, NAME and The Economist mentioned PRON among possible candidates in the upcoming presidential election.
Political positions  In an interview with RTS during the campaign, NAME said that PRON considered Kosovo a "temporarily occupied territory", that the Student List's starting point was the Constitution of Serbia, and that Serbia should secure a good life for the Serbs there through negotiations with relevant international actors.
PRON supported "productive relations" with Russia, citing Serbia's energy needs and historical ties, and described the future of NAME as "part of the future of the Serbian people".
PRON said that Student List offered the rule of law and equality before the law, regardless of citizens' religious or political views.
Asked about PRON differences with sociologist and fellow candidate NAME, PRON said that their shared political goals mattered more than personal differences, and that they agreed on environmental protection.
Personal life  NAME is religious and an Orthodox Christian.
