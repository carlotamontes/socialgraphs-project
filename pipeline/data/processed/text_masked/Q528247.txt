NAME (born 1970) is an American-Israeli-Brazilian physicist known for PRON work on silicon photonics.
A member of the National Academy of Sciences since 2019 and the National Academy of Engineering since 2025, NAME was named a 2010 MacArthur Fellow for contributions to silicon photonics especially towards enabling GHz silicon active devices.
Until 2014, PRON was the Given Foundation Professor of Engineering at Cornell University in the school of electrical and computer engineering and a member of the Kavli Institute for Nanoscience at Cornell.
PRON is now the NAME Professor of Electrical Engineering at Columbia University.
In 2009 PRON co-founded the company PicoLuz, which develops and commercializes silicon nanophotonics technologies.
In 2019, PRON co-founded Voyant Photonics, which develops next generation lidar technology based on silicon photonics.
In 2022, NAME was a co-founder of Xscape photonics to accelerate AI, ML, and simulation hardware.
In 2020 NAME was elected the 2021 vice president of Optica (formerly the Optical Society), and PRON served as the Optica president in 2023.
Education NAME was born in Haifa, Israel.
After spending two years as an undergraduate student at the Instituto de Física at the University of São Paulo, PRON continued at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, where PRON earned a BSc in physics in 1992, followed by an MSc in 1994 and a PhD in 1998, both also in physics.
PRON doctoral research focused on "Coupled Exciton-Photon Modes in Semiconductor Optical Microcavities".
NAME then spent 2 years as a postdoctoral associate with Lionel Kimerling at MIT, and then accepted a position at Cornell University in 2001.
Career and research NAME is best known for PRON work on silicon photonics.
PRON developed (along with other researchers around the world at IBM, Intel, Ghent University) silicon photonic components such as waveguide couplers, ring resonators, modulators, detectors, WDM wavelength sources and sensors on silicon platform.
PRON published the first paper on a class of versatile waveguides known as Slot-waveguides in 2004, which has since been cited over one thousand times.
In all PRON work has been cited 32,373 times (as of January 18, 2018).
PRON was also the first to demonstrate optical parametric gain in silicon, which was considered an important step towards building optical amplifiers in silicon.
NAME's NAME fellowship  citation mentions PRON work in ring modulators (circular waveguides) as the key contribution of NAME via the continued refinement of both opto-electronic and purely optical circuits for smaller size, increased efficiency, and accelerated switching speed  The resulting silicon-based photonic integrated circuits have the potential to improve signal transmission and processing dramatically.
NAME has received numerous honors, including being the recipient of a Fulbright Fellowship and an National Science Foundation CAREER Award.
PRON is also an elected fellow of Optica.
PRON current research interests include optical metamaterials, low-power and compact optical modulators, and slot waveguides.
PRON work has appeared in Nature, Nature Photonics, and other journals.
