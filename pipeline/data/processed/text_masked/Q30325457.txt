Noemí NAME (born 7 February 1951) is an Argentine chemistry professor and researcher.
PRON holds a degree in chemical engineering by the Engineering Faculty of the Universidad Nacional de La Plata (1971).
In addition, PRON has a PhD in chemistry from the University of Buenos Aires and is senior lecturer at the Engineering Faculty of the Universidad Nacional de La Plata and the director of the CIDCA.
Biography  Since NAME was a child, PRON showed PRON interest for science.
PRON graduated as a teacher at the age of 16, and subsequently passed baccalaureate subjects in order to go to a public university.
When PRON was 20 years old, PRON had already graduated as a chemical engineer, and married one of PRON faculty partners.
They have three children and four grandchildren.
PRON teaching and research work has resulted in several inventions, which are patented.
Professional career  PRON started as a researcher in the Food Cryotechnology Research and Development Centre (CIDCA), and was the director of this centre from 2003 to mid-2016.
PRON published 250 articles in international journals and over 40 book chapters.
Furthermore, PRON works as a professor at the NULP, where PRON was in charge of human resource management.
NAME is also a senior researcher of the board in the Food Cryotechnology Research and Development Centre (CIDCA), an organisation created by an agreement on 10 February 1973 between the NULP and the National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET).
In order to reduce the pollution caused by the food industry, NAME developed techniques that apply natural coagulants to treat effluents, and created technologies that take advantage of the collaborative work of bacteria.
Microorganisms build up "consortia" that consume pollutants and purify water.
Thus, the use of these technologies in the food industry will reduce the pollution that it causes in the waters of rivers, streams and lagoons.
In addition, NAME found a solution to the problem of waste generated by the processing of prawns, crabs, shrimp and crabs in Patagonia.
NAME is obtained from these crustaceans, which is used to synthesize micro and nanoparticles, which are capable of decontaminating waters with chromium, a carcinogen.
In recognition of PRON research, especially in the cryopreservation of food and biological material, the application of these findings to the productive sector and the training of other researchers, NAME was awarded in 2015 the Bunge y Born Prize of Process Engineering by a jury chaired by NAME and NAME.
Dr. NAME also received the Houssay Trajectory Award in the areas of engineering, architecture and information technology on 20 December 2016.
In 2023 PRON was granted the Platinum Konex Award for PRON work in Agricultural and Food Sciences in the last decade.
