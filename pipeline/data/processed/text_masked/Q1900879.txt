NAME (born 1964 in Hofheim am Taunus) is a German physicist, biophysicist, and author.
PRON is a full professor at Heidelberg University.
Biography and Work Markolf Niemz studied physics at Frankfurt University and Heidelberg University, and bioengineering at the University of California, San Diego.
In 1992, PRON received PRON PhD with a thesis on the construction of a pulse compressed Nd:YLF laser to study the plasma-induced ablation of tissue.
In 1995, PRON was a Research Fellow at Harvard Medical School with a grant from the German Research Foundation.
Niemz worked as head of the Optical Spectroscopy department at the Fraunhofer Institute for Physical Measurement Techniques (IPM) in Freiburg until 1999.
In 2000, PRON has been appointed the Chair of Medical Engineering/Biomedical Engineering at Heidelberg University, settled as a full professor at the Medical Faculty of Mannheim.
Since then, Niemz has been director of the Mannheim Biomedical Engineering Laboratories (MABEL), a joint venture of Heidelberg University and the Mannheim University of Applied Sciences.
PRON research focuses on light and matter, biosignals, and the foundations of physics.
PRON was the first scientist worldwide to apply ultrashort laser pulses to dentistry for a pain-free treatment of caries.
