Troy NAME (born July 31, 1973) is an American plasma physicist and the Director of Fusion Energy Division at Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
PRON was formerly a professor at the University of California, Los Angeles.
PRON was co-awarded the 2002 NAME in Plasma Physics Research for PRON work on driven magnetic reconnection in a laboratory plasma.
Early life and career  NAME received a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in physics and a B.S. in nuclear engineering from North Carolina State University in 1995.
PRON then received a Master of Arts and a Doctor of Philosophy in Astrophysical Sciences from Princeton University in 1997 and 2001 respectively, where PRON was supervised by NAME and NAME.
Upon graduation, NAME was awarded a Fusion Energy Postdoctoral Fellowship from the US Department of Energy and pursued PRON postdoctoral work at UCLA.
In 2002, NAME became a faculty member at UCLA's department of physics and astronomy.
PRON was an Assistant Professor until 2008 where PRON was promoted to Associate Professor, and was later promoted to full Professor in 2011.
In 2016, PRON became the director of the Basic Plasma Science Facility and oversaw the renewal of the facility funding from the Department of Energy and the National Science Foundation.
In 2017, PRON became a director of the Plasma Science and Technology Institute at UCLA.
NAME led the DOE Fusion Energy Sciences Advisory Committee (FESAC) Subcommittee on Long Range Planning in 2020, resulting in the report "Powering the Future: Fusion and NAME" published in 2021.
In 2024, NAME assumed the role of Director of Fusion Energy Division at Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
Honors and awards  In 2002, NAME was jointly awarded NAME in Plasma Physics Research with NAME, NAME and NAME for "the experimental investigation of driven magnetic reconnection in a laboratory plasma.
In this work, careful diagnostic studies of the current sheet structure, dynamics and associated wave activity provide a comprehensive picture of the reconnection process."
In 2014, NAME was inducted as a fellow of the American Physical Society, with citation "For novel and impactful experimental research into fundamental processes relevant to laboratory, space and astrophysical plasmas including magnetic reconnection, wave-wave interactions, and turbulence and transport in magnetically-confined plasmas."
In 2021, NAME was selected for the Fusion Power Associates Leadership award.
