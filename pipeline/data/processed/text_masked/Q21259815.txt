Francesca Ferlaino (born 1977) is an Italian-Austrian experimental physicist known for PRON research on quantum matter.
PRON is a professor of physics at the University of Innsbruck.
Career Francesca Ferlaino was born in Naples, Italy.
PRON studied physics at the University of Naples Federico II (1995–2000) and was an undergraduate research fellow at the International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA) in Trieste (1999–2000).
PRON did a PhD in physics at the University of Florence and the European Laboratory for Non-Linear Spectroscopy (LENS) (2001–2004).
In 2007, PRON moved to the University of Innsbruck, Austria, where PRON was a research and teaching associate and started PRON own research group.
In 2014 PRON became a professor of physics at the University of Innsbruck and research director at the Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information (IQOQI) of the Austrian Academy of Sciences.
Research PRON research activity explores quantum phenomena in atomic gases at ultralow temperatures with contributions spanning topics including quantum matter of atoms and molecules and few-body and scattering physics.
Over the last years, PRON has focused specifically on the strongly magnetic, and rather unexplored, Erbium and Dysprosium atomic species, realizing in 2012 the world's first NAME condensation of Erbium, and in 2018, the first dipolar quantum mixture of Erbium and Dysprosium.
In 2019, PRON was able to prepare the first long-lived supersolid state, an elusive and paradoxical state where superfluid flow and crystal rigidity coexist.
With these systems, PRON has explored a variety of many-body quantum phenomena dictated by the long-range and anisotropic dipolar interaction among the atoms.
In 2021, PRON created supersolid states along two dimensions.
In 2024 PRON team reported the observation of quantum vortices in the supersolid phase  Awards PRON was awarded the Grand Prix de Physique "Cécile-DeWitt Morette/École de Physique des Houches" from the French Academy of Sciences (2019), the Junior BEC Award (2019),  the Feltrinelli Prize (2017) and the Erwin Schrödinger Prize (2017).
PRON was elected as a Fellow of the American Physical Society (APS) in 2019 "for ground-breaking experiments on dipolar quantum gases of erbium atoms, including the attainment of quantum degeneracy of bosons and fermions, studies on quantum-chaotical scattering, the formation of quantum droplets, and investigations on the roton spectrum".
PRON was named NAME's Scientist of the Year 2025 and Austrian of the Year 2024 in research.
Since 2026, PRON is a full member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences.
