NAME (born 10 December 1945) is Professor of Organic Chemistry in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Cambridge, and is a Fellow of Trinity College, Cambridge.
PRON was President of the Royal Society of Chemistry (2000–2002) and was made a CBE in January 2002, in the process.
In 2011, PRON was included by The Times in the list of the "100 most important people in British science".
Education Ley was educated at Stamford and Loughborough University of Technology where PRON was awarded a Bachelor of Science and PhD.  Research Ley's main research field are the total synthesis of biomolecules.
PRON group has published extensively on this topic, and has completed the synthesis of more than 140 natural target compounds, with notable examples including indanamycin, routiennocin, avermectin B1a, NAME, spongistatin, thapsigargin, epothilone A, NAME, bengazole A and rapamycin.
PRON total synthesis of azadirachtin, completed in 2007, is widely regarded as one of the major landmarks in total synthesis.
In the course of this work, PRON has also made substantial advances in many areas of organic chemistry, including the development of new catalysts, protecting groups and reagents.
PRON is one of the inventors of TPAP, a widely employed oxidising reagent.
PRON has also pioneered the use of immobilised reagents and flow techniques in multi-step organic synthesis.
This work now incorporates flow chemistry for multistep organic synthesis applications.
By 2020, Ley had published 888 academic research papers spanning PRON career of 50 years.
