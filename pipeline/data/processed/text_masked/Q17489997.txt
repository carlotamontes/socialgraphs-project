Galves–Löcherbach model   Galves and Eva Löcherbach proposed the Galves–Löcherbach model in 2013.
This is a model with intrinsic stochasticity for biological neural nets, in which the probability of a future spike depends on the evolution of the complete system since the last spike.
This model of spiking neurons was developed by mathematicians NAME and Eva Löcherbach.
In 2013 they called it a model of a "system with interacting stochastic chains with memory of variable length".
