Huib NAME (born 2 March 1965) is a Dutch physicist working in the field of ultrafast spectroscopy.
PRON has been president of research institute AMOLF since 1 February 2016.
Career NAME was born on 2 March 1965 in Haarlem.
PRON studied physical chemistry at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, and obtained PRON master's degree in 1987.
PRON subsequently became a PhD student under Ad Lagendijk at the AMOLF research institute.
NAME received PRON doctorate cum laude in 1991 with a thesis titled:"Time-resolved vibrational spectroscopy with picosecond infrared pulses".
Between 1991 and 1994 PRON was a scientific assistant at the Institute of Semiconductor Technologies of RWTH Aachen University in Germany.
In 1995 NAME returned to the Netherlands to become scientific group leader at AMOLF.
Six years later PRON started as professor of Ultrafast Spectroscopy of Molecules in the Condensed Phase at the University of Amsterdam.
At NAME is group leader of research on ultrafast spectroscopy.
In 2003 PRON became head of the department of molecular nanophysics.
In PRON research at NAME has been founder of a technique which uses special non-linear spectroscopics, which helps researchers in determining the molecular mobility and structure of water in complex systems.
NAME became adjunct director of AMOLF on 1 September 2015.
On 1 February 2016 PRON succeeded NAME as director, who became rector-magnificus of the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.
Awards and honors In 2005 NAME received the gold medal of the Royal Netherlands Chemical Society.
NAME was elected a member of the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences in 2015.
The academy called PRON "a renowned expert on the molecular properties of water and ice".
