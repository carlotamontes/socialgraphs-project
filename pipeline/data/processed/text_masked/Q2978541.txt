Life and career  Maccone was born on 6 February 1948.
PRON obtained two degrees from the University of Turin: in physics in 1972 and in mathematics in 1974.
PRON obtained PRON PhD at the Department of mathematics of King's College London, England in September 1980.

PRON then joined the Space Systems Group of Aeritalia (later called Alenia Spazio S.p.

In 2002 PRON was awarded the "Giordano Bruno Award" by the SETI League, "for PRON efforts to establish a radio observatory on the far side of the NAME."
After PRON early retirement from Alenia Spazio S.p.
A. on 30 December 2004, PRON taught at Polytechnic University of Turin for five years (2005-2009) at Post-Doctoral level.
PRON was Advisor to Ph.D. candidates in the Aerospace and Electronics Departments of Polytechnic University of Turin.
Since 2010 PRON was the Director for Scientific Space Exploration of the International Academy of Astronautics (IAA, based in Paris, France).
From 2012 to 2021, PRON served as Chair of the IAA SETI Permanent Committee.
PRON had published 149 scientific and technical papers.
Maccone died of a heart attack on 20 August 2025, at the age of 77.
