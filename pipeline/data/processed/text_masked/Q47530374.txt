NAME (born 21 October 1969) is a British chemist who is a professor of physical chemistry at the University of York.
PRON was one of the founding scientists and director of the Cape Verde Atmospheric Observatory (CVAO), established on NAME in 2006 and now a key global monitoring site.
PRON work has helped to transform understanding of how oceans shape the air above them.
Education NAME graduated with a BSc in chemistry from the University of Bristol in 1991 followed by a PhD in atmospheric chemistry at the University of East Anglia supervised by NAME and awarded in 1996.
Research and career PRON group studies the complex interaction between the oceans and the atmosphere, in particular the chemistry of reactive halogens, organic carbon, and reactive nitrogen.
PRON work on oceanic and NAME has established this chemistry as an important component of tropospheric ozone cycling and makes use of gas chromatography–mass spectrometry (GCMS).
PRON helped establish the Cape Verde Atmospheric Observatory, one of a few dozen World Meteorological Organization (WMO) Global Atmosphere Watch (GAW) stations worldwide which monitor climate and air quality gases over long time scales, and was a lead chapter author of the WMO/United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) 2014 scientific assessment of ozone depletion.
NAME is one of the co-chairs of the Scientific Assessment Panel for the Montreal Protocol and presented at the 37th Meeting of the Parties (MOP37) in Nairobi.
In March 2026, NAME was featured on The Life Scientific to speak about the impact of oceans on ozone.
Awards and honours NAME has received several awards for PRON research.
PRON received a Philip Leverhulme Prize in 'Earth Ocean and Atmospheric Sciences' in 2006, and was given the Rosalind Franklin Award from the Royal Society in 2015 for "PRON scientific achievement, PRON suitability as a role model and PRON project proposal to promote GENDERWORD in STEM".
PRON received the Tilden Prize in 2017.
In 2019, NAME was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS).
PRON was awarded the 2024 Vilhelm Bjerknes Medal "for establishing a critical link between the production of trace gases in and over the oceans, atmospheric chemistry, and climate change".
NAME was appointed Member of the Order of the British Empire (MBE) in the 2022 New Year Honours for services to atmospheric chemistry.
