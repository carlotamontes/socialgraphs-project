PRON founded a branch of the Society for the Preservation of Monuments in Białystok and chaired it for many years.

Career In the years 1975-1979 NAME was employed at Warsaw University.
Since 1979 NAME was working at University of Białystok (till 1997 it was a branch of Warsaw University).
In the years 1997-2005 PRON was the dean of the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, and from 2008 to 2016 - the dean of the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Białystok.
PRON was also the director of the Institute of Mathematics (2005-2008 and 2016-2019).
PRON founded and was leading a group of researchers working in the area of mathematical physics.
PRON was also a founder and main organizer of the conference series Workshop on Geometric Methods in Physics.
