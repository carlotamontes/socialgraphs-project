PRON research group at Université de Montréal is well known for developing synthetic methodologies, as well as natural product total synthesis.
In addition, PRON group developed Chiron, a computer program used by organic chemists for synthetic planning.
PRON earned PRON Ph.D. at Ohio State University in 1960, under the direction of NAME.
PRON worked as a researcher for Parke-Davis & Co. in Ann Arbor, Michigan until 1968.
In 1969 PRON joined Université de Montréal.
Since 2000, PRON has also been an adjunct professor at University of California, Irvine.
