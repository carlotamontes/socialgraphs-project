NAME (祝介平; born 1965 in Xiaoshan, Hangzhou, China) is an organic chemist specializing in natural product total synthesis and organometallics.
PRON is a professor of chemistry at EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) and the head of the Laboratory of Synthesis and Natural Products.
Career  Zhu studied chemistry at Hangzhou Normal University and at Lanzhou University, both in China.
PRON then joined Henri-Philippe Husson and Jean-Charles Quirion to pursue a PhD in organic chemistry at the Institute de Chimie des Substances Naturelles at the French National Centre for Scientific Research.
In 1991, PRON graduated with a thesis on Synthèse asymétrique de dérivés NAME spiropipéridines apparentées (NAME synthesis of histrionicotoxin derivatives and related spiropiperidines).
As a postdoctoral researcher PRON went to work with NAME at the Texas A&M University and pursue research on phosphonic acid synthesis.
In 1992, PRON joined Institut de Chimie des Substances Naturelles (ICSN) as a chargé de recherche.
In 2000, PRON was promoted to directeur de recherche  2nd class, then 1st class in 2006 in the same institute.
Since 2010, PRON has been full professor of chemistry at EPFL's School of Basic Sciences of EPFL.
Research  Zhu's research focuses on the development of new synthetic methods including multicomponent reactions, catalytic enantioselective transformations, and transition metal catalyzed domino processes.
PRON is also immersed in the total synthesis of complex natural products and bioactive molecules.
Distinctions  Zhu is the recipient of among others the 2016 Natural Product Chemistry Award from the Royal Society of Chemistry (RSC) Natural Product Chemistry Award, the 2010 Division of Organic Chemistry (Prix SCF-DCO) award from the Société chimique de France (French Chemical Society), the 2009 NAME (Qinghua University) awarded by the Chinese Ministry of Education, the 2009 Silver Medal of CNRS, the 2008 Novartis Chemistry Lectureship, the 2004 Liebig Lectureship of the German Chemical Society, and the 2003 Prix “Emile Jungfleisch” of French Academy of Science.
PRON is a  fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry.
PRON is editor at the journal Tetrahedron Letters.
