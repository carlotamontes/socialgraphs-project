Research highlights    Metal complexes of octafluorocyclooctatetraene (OFCOT)  One of Hughes’ research interests focused on the study of transition metal complexes with octafluorocyclooctatetraene (OFCOT), and the comparison of the structural features of these complexes to the hydrocarbon counterpart of OFCOT cyclooctatetraene (COT).
One striking difference in the coordination complexes of OFCOT is that their thermal stability and resistance to dynamic rearrangements in binding geometry (diminished fluxional character) compared to COT analogs.
These differences can be rationalized based on the relative thermodynamic stability of the isomers computed using density functional theory (DFT).
