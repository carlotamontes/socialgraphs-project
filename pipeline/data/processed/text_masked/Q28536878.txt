NAME (, born 3 September 1962) is a Bulgarian politician who served as Prime Minister of Bulgaria from 2023 to 2024.
A member of the PP party, PRON previously served as Member of the National Assembly from 2022 to 2023 and as Minister of Education and Science in 2017 and from 2021 to 2022.
NAME is a physicist, physical chemist and chemist.
PRON is a member of the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences and was a lecturer at the University of Sofia.
Early life  NAME was born on 3 September 1962, in Stara Zagora, Bulgaria.
After elementary school, PRON moved to the Bulgarian capital Sofia, where PRON graduated from the National Gymnasium for Science and Mathematics in 1980.
In the same year PRON was a participant at the 12th International Chemistry Olympiad in Linz (Austria) PRON won a silver medal.
This was followed by a master's degree in chemistry and pharmacy at the St. Kliment-Ohridski University in Sofia, which PRON completed in 1987.
In 1993, PRON defended PRON dissertation and obtained PRON doctorate.
For the early part of PRON career, NAME worked as visiting researcher in JRDC (Japan), senior researcher in Rhone-Poulenc R&D (France), lead scientist in Unilever R&D (USA), and guest professor in France (ESPCI-Paris and NAME.
Academic career NAME has been an adjunct lecturer since 1997 and professor of physical chemistry at the University of Sofia since 2008.
Between 2008 and 2015 PRON was head of the faculty for technical chemistry and director of the master's course Disperse Systems in Chemical Technologies.
PRON has been a doctor of chemistry since 2007.
PRON specialized in Japan and at Uppsala University in Sweden and worked as a senior scientist in the research institutes of private companies such as Unilever (USA) and Rhône-Poulenc (France).
In 2010 PRON was awarded the highest national award "Pythagoras" for scientific achievements by the Bulgarian Ministry of Education and Science.
In 2013 PRON received the Medal of Honor with Blue Ribbon from the University of Sofia.
Between 2012 and 2013, NAME was a member of various working groups in the Ministry of Education and Science and in the Council of Ministers.
PRON actively participated in the development of the concept of the Operational Program Science and Education for Smart Growth and in the discussions for the Partnership Agreement for 2013–2020 between the Republic of Bulgaria and the European Commission.
Civil service career From August 2014 to April 2016, NAME was Deputy Minister of Education and Science in the Borisov II government, responsible for higher education and the European Structural Funds, including the implementation of the Operational Program Science and Education for Smart Growth.
From 27 January 2017, to 4 May 2017, PRON was Interim Minister for Education and Science in Gerdzhikov's interim government.
In 2019, NAME was awarded the Solvay Prize of the European Colloid and Interface Society (ECIS) for PRON research achievements and PRON was elected a full member of the Academia Europaea.
Political career Education minister (2021–2022) Between 12 May 2021, and 13 December 2021, PRON was again interim minister for education and science in the acting governments of Yanev I and Yanev II during the COVID-19 pandemic.
When after the general election in November 2021 that We Continue the Change (PP) had the largest congressional faction was and could form a coalition capable of governing, NAME became the education minister in the Cabinet of Kiril Petkov.
Prime Minister of Bulgaria (2023–2024)   Following the outcome of the Bulgarian parliamentary elections held in April 2023, on 22 May NAME was projected to become Prime Minister of Bulgaria as part of a power-sharing agreement between the two most-voted coalitions, GERB—SDS and PP–DB.
The deal implied that NAME would lead the new government for the following nine months, before switching positions with NAME.
Following extensive talks between the two coalitions involved, as well as the Movement for Rights and Freedoms, an official agreement on the composition of the Denkov-Gabriel cabinet was reached on 2 June.
On 6 June, the 49th National Assembly of Bulgaria voted in NAME's new government, with 132 votes in favor and 69 against.
The new government will focus on fighting Russian influence in Bulgaria's security sector and obtaining membership in the Schengen Area and the eurozone.
A political crisis prompted Bulgaria to postpone adopting the euro until 2025.
In December 2022, Austrian and Dutch opposition blocked Bulgaria and Romania from becoming members of the Schengen Area.
In September 2023, NAME called NAME a "valuable partner" of Bulgaria.
NAME condemned Hamas' actions during the Gaza war and expressed PRON support to Israel.
On 30 December 2023, it became clear that NAME's cabinet had secured membership for Bulgaria in the Schengen Area by air and sea.
Although further negotiations in 2024 would be required to lift border controls by land, this was a major diplomatic success for the Denkov-Gabriel cabinet, which aimed for а positive decision regarding Schengen Area membership by the end of 2023 as part of the five government priorities stated in July of the same year.
NAME resigned on 6 March 2024 as part of the power-sharing agreement with NAME.
However, on 25 March, NAME withdrew PRON nomination for prime minister after announcing that negotiations on forming a new government with PP-DB had failed, and said that new elections would be held.
