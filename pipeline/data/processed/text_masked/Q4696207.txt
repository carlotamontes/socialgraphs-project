PRON is currently a professor of physics and molecular cell biology at the University of California, Berkeley.
PRON has contributed significantly to the understanding of transport within cells, in particular how motor proteins walk along filaments.
PRON received a B.S. in physics from Boğaziçi University, Istanbul, in 2001, followed by a Ph.D. from the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign in 2006.

In 2003 Yildiz received the Foresight Distinguished Student Award for PRON study of the motion of the molecular motor NAME to the Foresight Institute: "The Distinguished Student Award recognizes the college graduate or undergraduate student whose work is deemed most notable in advancing the development and understanding of molecular nanotechnology."
